Profile · Ordinary-student examples档案 · 普通学生示例

Examples for ordinary students: you already have more than you think普通学生的示例：你拥有的比你以为的多

Six invented students with average marks and everyday lives, each with a one-page resume, a matched activity menu, a calm plan and the BC programs that fit. Find the one closest to you and borrow its shape.六位虚构的学生，成绩普通、生活平常；每人一页简历、一份匹配的活动菜单、一个不赶的计划，以及合适的 BC 项目。找到最像你的那个，借用它的结构。

The core核心

BC universities value a coherent story of interest → action → reflection, not the number of activities.BC 的大学看重「兴趣 → 行动 → 反思」这条连贯的故事，而不是活动的数量。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Things you already do — band, a part-time job, helping at home, a children's program — are real resume material once you add a number and a sentence of reflection.你已经在做的事——乐队、打工、帮家里、儿童活动——加上一个数字和一句反思，就是真正的简历素材。
  2. Matching beats ranking: look for programs, volunteering, jobs and routes that fit your time and energy, not the most impressive ones.匹配胜过排名：找适合你时间和精力的项目、志愿、工作和路线，而不是最亮眼的。
  3. A college start and a later transfer is a normal BC route, not a back door.先读学院再转学，是 BC 的常规路线，不是走后门。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“I need a big achievement first.” Most applications are read for steadiness and reflection; small, true and repeated is strong.「我得先有个大成就。」大多数申请看的是稳定和反思；小而真实、反复去做，就很有力。

🎯 What “interest → action → reflection” means, in plain words「兴趣 → 行动 → 反思」用大白话说

Someone reading your application cannot meet you. They look for a thread: something you care about (interest), a few real things you did about it, more than once (action), and a short honest note on what changed in you (reflection). Three activities that connect say more than ten that don't.读你申请的人见不到你。他们找的是一条线：你在意的一件事（兴趣），你为它真正做过的几件事，并且不止一次（行动），以及一段简短诚实的话，说你因此有了什么变化（反思）。三项相互关联的活动，比十项互不相干的更有说服力。

Interest兴趣

Not a passion speech. “I like it when the little kids at the program finally get the game” is an interest.不需要激情演讲。「儿童活动里小朋友终于玩懂游戏时我很开心」就是兴趣。

Action行动

Something you actually did, with a number: 2 hours a week since September. Repeated beats big.你真做过的事，带个数字：9 月起每周 2 小时。反复做胜过做得大。

Reflection反思

Two or three sentences: what you learned, what surprised you, what you want to try next. This is the part most people skip.两三句话：学到什么、什么让你意外、接下来想试什么。这是大多数人跳过的部分。

👥 Who these examples are for这些示例写给谁

  • Average marks, but neat, responsible and self-disciplined — teachers like working with you.成绩中等，但整洁、负责、自律——老师喜欢和你合作。
  • You feel there is “nothing to write”, especially after seeing other people's achievements.觉得「没什么可写」，尤其是看到别人的成绩之后。
  • You tire easily and need a light load, small steps and real rest; you often study lying down or by listening.容易累，需要轻负担、小步子和真正的休息；常常躺着学或用听的。
  • You are interested in health, psychology or helping people — or in something else entirely — without being set on one job.对健康、心理或助人感兴趣——也可能是完全别的方向——但还没定死某个职业。
  • Some of you are new to BC and speak two or three languages.有些人刚来 BC，会两三种语言。

The six students below are invented and mixed on purpose: no one of them is a real person, and the details are common to many students.下面六位学生是虚构的，并有意打散混合：没有一位是真实的人，细节都是很多学生共有的。

⏱️ Turn what you already have into a resume in 20 minutes20 分钟，把你已有的变成简历

① Minutes 0–5 · List第 0–5 分钟 · 列

Everything from the last 12 months, no judging: school, home, family, faith or community, work, online, contests, music, art, sport. Small counts.过去 12 个月的所有事，先不评判：学校、家里、家人、宗教或社区、打工、网上、比赛、音乐、美术、运动。小事也算。

② Minutes 5–10 · Count第 5–10 分钟 · 数

Pick 4–6. For each, add a number: hours a week, months, how many people, how many times.挑 4–6 件。每件加一个数字：每周几小时、几个月、多少人、多少次。

③ Minutes 10–15 · Rewrite第 10–15 分钟 · 改写

Verb first, then the number, then one result or one thing you learned. Use the before/after pairs as models.动词开头，再写数字，再写一个结果或一件学到的事。照着「之前/之后」的例子改。

④ Minutes 15–20 · One sentence第 15–20 分钟 · 一句话

Write your profile sentence: “I'm interested in … so I … and I learned …”. Save, export, stop. That's enough for today.写一句个人简介：「我对……感兴趣，所以我……，我学到了……」。保存、导出、停下。今天就够了。

Fill it in below; it is saved in this browser only. Stop at 20 minutes even if it isn't perfect — you can come back.就在下面填；只存在本机浏览器。到 20 分钟就停，不完美也没关系——随时可以回来。

🗺️ Find the example closest to you找到最像你的示例

🌱 A · The quiet helper安静的帮忙者

Quiet, curious about health and people, already volunteers a little — and thinks none of it counts.安静、对健康和人感兴趣，其实已经在做一点志愿——却觉得这些都不算数。

Matches toward匹配方向 Health-adjacent volunteering → psychology or child and youth care与健康相关的志愿 → 心理学或儿童与青少年关怀

⏰ B · The weekend worker周末打工的人

Works part-time and helps at home; there is very little free time, and every new activity feels like one too many.在打工，也帮家里做事；空闲时间很少，再加任何活动都觉得太多。

Matches toward匹配方向 Count the job as experience → practical health diplomas (pharmacy technician, health information)把打工算作经历 → 实用型健康文凭（药房技术员、健康信息管理）

🧭 C · The newcomer with three languages会三种语言的新来者

Arrived in BC not long ago; English is improving every month, and the languages already spoken are a strength nobody has named yet.来 BC 不久；英语每个月都在进步，而已经会的语言是一项还没人点明的长处。

Matches toward匹配方向 Languages and helping people → social service, social work, psychology via a college start语言与助人 → 经由学院起步的社会服务、社工、心理学

🎨 D · The music-and-art student爱音乐美术的学生

Spends free time drawing or making music and wants work with people — but not sure art and health can meet.空闲时画画或玩音乐，想做与人打交道的工作——但不确定艺术和健康能不能连起来。

Matches toward匹配方向 Arts with people → therapeutic recreation, child and youth care, arts-based volunteering带着艺术与人相处 → 治疗性康乐、儿童与青少年关怀、艺术类志愿

🔀 E · The student who changed direction中途换了方向的学生

Started out aiming at health science, then found they enjoy spreadsheets and making things work on a computer — and worries the change looks like quitting.一开始想读健康科学，后来发现自己喜欢做表格、让电脑上的东西跑起来——担心换方向看起来像放弃。

Matches toward匹配方向 Health + data → health information science or management; or a different field entirely健康＋数据 → 健康信息科学或管理；也可以完全换一个领域

🐢 F · Average marks, keeps showing up成绩普通，但一直坚持

Never the star, always there: three years on a team, helps coach younger kids, marks in the B–C range.从来不是明星，但一直都在：在校队三年，帮忙带小队员，成绩在 B–C 之间。

Matches toward匹配方向 Movement and helping → recreation, kinesiology via a college start, therapeutic recreation运动与帮助人 → 康乐、经由学院起步的运动机能学、治疗性康乐

🤖 Good questions to ask an AI向 AI 提的好问题

Here is one thing I do outside class: [describe it]. Ask me three questions about hours, people and what I learned, then turn my answers into one resume line. Don't add anything I didn't say.
这是我课外做的一件事：[描述]。先问我三个关于时数、人数和收获的问题，再把我的回答改成一行简历。不要加任何我没说的内容。
Read my resume lines below and point out any word that sounds bigger than what I actually did. Suggest a more honest version.
读下面我的简历条目，指出哪些词听起来比我实际做的更夸张，给一个更诚实的版本。
I have about [N] free hours a week and get tired easily. Suggest two low-cost activities in BC that fit, and tell me what to check on their official pages.
我每周大约有 [N] 小时空闲，而且容易累。建议两个适合我、花费低的 BC 活动，并告诉我该在它们官网上核对什么。
How to check the answer怎么核对 AI 的回答 AI can invent organisations, ages and deadlines. Keep only lines that are true for you, and check every program on its official page before you plan around it.AI 可能会编造机构、年龄和截止日期。只保留对你真实的内容；每个项目都要在官网核实后再做计划。
