🐢 Average marks, keeps showing up🐢 成绩普通，但一直坚持

The core核心

Interest → action → reflection, with what this student already has.用这位学生已有的东西，讲出「兴趣 → 行动 → 反思」。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. On a school sports team three years, rarely a starter在校队三年，很少首发
