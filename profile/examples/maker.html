🎨 The music-and-art student🎨 爱音乐美术的学生

Spends free time drawing or making music and wants work with people — but not sure art and health can meet.空闲时画画或玩音乐，想做与人打交道的工作——但不确定艺术和健康能不能连起来。

The core核心

Interest → action → reflection, with what this student already has.用这位学生已有的东西，讲出「兴趣 → 行动 → 反思」。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Plays in a school band or sings in a choir在学校乐队或合唱团
  2. Draws posters for clubs; keeps a sketchbook给社团画海报；有一本速写本
  3. Average in sciences, strong in arts理科中等，艺术突出
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“None of this counts.” It all counts once it has a number and a sentence of reflection.「这些都不算数。」加上数字和一句反思，就都算数。
