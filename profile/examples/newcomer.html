🧭 The newcomer with three languages🧭 会三种语言的新来者

Arrived in BC not long ago; English is improving every month, and the languages already spoken are a strength nobody has named yet.来 BC 不久；英语每个月都在进步，而已经会的语言是一项还没人点明的长处。

The core核心

Interest → action → reflection, with what this student already has.用这位学生已有的东西，讲出「兴趣 → 行动 → 反思」。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Speaks Chinese and English, learning a third language at school会中文和英文，在学校学第三种语言
  2. Helps family read letters and fill in forms帮家里读信件、填表格
  3. Entered a math contest, no prize; feels behind classmates参加过数学竞赛，没得奖；觉得自己落后于同学
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“None of this counts.” It all counts once it has a number and a sentence of reflection.「这些都不算数。」加上数字和一句反思，就都算数。
