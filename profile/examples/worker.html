⏰ The weekend worker⏰ 周末打工的人

Works part-time and helps at home; there is very little free time, and every new activity feels like one too many.在打工，也帮家里做事；空闲时间很少，再加任何活动都觉得太多。

The core核心

Interest → action → reflection, with what this student already has.用这位学生已有的东西，讲出「兴趣 → 行动 → 反思」。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Part-time job about 10 h/week (example: café or grocery store)每周打工约 10 小时（例如咖啡店或超市）
  2. Helps a younger sibling with homework辅导弟弟妹妹写作业

🏔️ 4 · The big picture: phases and a calm week4 · 大图景：阶段与一个不赶的一周

① Phase 1第 1 阶段

Fall: list what the job already shows; check what each target program asks for秋季：写下打工已经说明了什么；查目标项目的要求

② Phase 2第 2 阶段

Application season: one evening a week for applications only申请季：每周只留一个晚上做申请

③ Phase 3第 3 阶段

Winter–spring: keep marks steady in the courses programs look at; no new activities冬春：把项目看重的课保持稳定；不加新活动

④ Phase 4第 4 阶段

Summer: one certificate on a free weekend; rest暑假：找一个空周末拿一个证书；休息

A normal week平常的一周

★ Must do必做
  • Homework in the two school nights with no shift没排班的两个上学日晚上写作业
  • Work shifts (about 10 h)打工（约 10 小时）
• Should do该做
  • One 45-min review block for Anatomy & Physiology一块 45 分钟的解剖生理复习
  • Sibling homework check (short)检查弟弟妹妹作业（简短）
○ Want to do想做
  • Flashcards on the bus路上刷卡片
♡ Rest (planned, not leftover)休息（排进去，不是剩下的）
  • One full evening off after the weekend shifts周末班后留一个完整的晚上休息
  • Same bedtime on school nights上学日固定上床时间
🌧 Low-energy week, exam weeks, first two weeks of a term没精神的一周、考试周、新学期头两周

Keep ★ must-do only, plus ♡ rest. Drop ○ completely and • to one item. Easing off before exams and at the start of a term is part of the plan, not falling behind.只保留 ★ 必做，加上 ♡ 休息。○ 全部停，• 只留一项。考试前和新学期开头减负，是计划的一部分，不是落后。

☀ Ordinary week普通的一周

All four columns. If a day goes badly, the minimal day counts: one must-do block and one thing for your body.四栏都有。某天状态不好，就用最小版：一块必做加一件照顾身体的事，也算完成。 Plan your own week规划你自己的一周
