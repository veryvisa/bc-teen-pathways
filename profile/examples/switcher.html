🔀 The student who changed direction🔀 中途换了方向的学生

Started out aiming at health science, then found they enjoy spreadsheets and making things work on a computer — and worries the change looks like quitting.一开始想读健康科学，后来发现自己喜欢做表格、让电脑上的东西跑起来——担心换方向看起来像放弃。

The core核心

Interest → action → reflection, with what this student already has.用这位学生已有的东西，讲出「兴趣 → 行动 → 反思」。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Did a science fair project once, no award做过一次科学展项目，没获奖
  2. Learned some coding online by themselves自己在网上学了一点编程
  3. Average marks; reliable in group work成绩中等；小组合作里很可靠
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“None of this counts.” It all counts once it has a number and a sentence of reflection.「这些都不算数。」加上数字和一句反思，就都算数。
