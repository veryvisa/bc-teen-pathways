🌱 The quiet helper🌱 安静的帮忙者

Quiet, curious about health and people, already volunteers a little — and thinks none of it counts.安静、对健康和人感兴趣，其实已经在做一点志愿——却觉得这些都不算数。

The core核心

Interest → action → reflection, with what this student already has.用这位学生已有的东西，讲出「兴趣 → 行动 → 反思」。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Average marks, very reliable; teachers trust them with small jobs成绩中等，非常可靠；老师愿意把小事交给他
  2. Helps with a children's program at a community or faith centre在社区或宗教场所的儿童活动里帮忙
  3. Tires after busy days; likes listening to things lying down忙完一天容易累；喜欢躺着听东西
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“None of this counts.” It all counts once it has a number and a sentence of reflection.「这些都不算数。」加上数字和一句反思，就都算数。

🏔️ 4 · The big picture: phases and a calm week4 · 大图景：阶段与一个不赶的一周

① Phase 1第 1 阶段

Now – winter break: write down what already exists; start an hours log现在到寒假：把已有的事写下来，开始记时数

② Phase 2第 2 阶段

Winter break: one short course (first aid), then rest寒假：一门短课（急救），然后休息

③ Phase 3第 3 阶段

Spring term: keep the same two activities; add nothing new during exams春季学期：保持原来两项活动；考试期不加新事

④ Phase 4第 4 阶段

Summer: read one health-volunteer page, write a three-sentence reflection暑假：读一个健康志愿官网，写三句反思

A normal week平常的一周

★ Must do必做
  • Homework & one review block (45 min) on school nights上学日：作业＋一块复习（45 分钟）
  • Children's program (2 h, weekend)儿童活动（2 小时，周末）
• Should do该做
  • Band practice 2×20 min乐队练习 2×20 分钟
  • One online biology lesson, lying down with notes after一节在线生物课，躺着看，看完记笔记
○ Want to do想做
  • Read a chapter of a book about how the body works读一章讲身体如何运作的书
♡ Rest (planned, not leftover)休息（排进去，不是剩下的）
  • One evening with nothing planned一个什么都不排的晚上
  • Sleep by the same time on school nights上学日固定时间睡觉
🌧 Low-energy week, exam weeks, first two weeks of a term没精神的一周、考试周、新学期头两周

Keep ★ must-do only, plus ♡ rest. Drop ○ completely and • to one item. Easing off before exams and at the start of a term is part of the plan, not falling behind.只保留 ★ 必做，加上 ♡ 休息。○ 全部停，• 只留一项。考试前和新学期开头减负，是计划的一部分，不是落后。

☀ Ordinary week普通的一周

All four columns. If a day goes badly, the minimal day counts: one must-do block and one thing for your body.四栏都有。某天状态不好，就用最小版：一块必做加一件照顾身体的事，也算完成。 Plan your own week规划你自己的一周
