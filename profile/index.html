🎯 What admissions readers look for: interest → action → reflection招生阅读者看什么：兴趣 → 行动 → 反思

BC universities read for a connected story — one interest that turns into actions and a reflection — not for how many activities you list.BC 的大学读的是一条连贯的故事——一个兴趣变成行动，再变成反思——不是数你列了多少活动。

One interest can grow into a line一个兴趣怎样长成一条线

  1. Read a book about how memory works → write three sentences on what surprised you.读一本讲记忆如何运作的书 → 写三句最让你意外的地方。
  2. Take a short listening or first-aid course → volunteer a few hours where those skills are used.上一个短的倾听或急救课 → 在用得上这些技能的地方做几小时志愿。
  3. Turn it into a small project: a one-page knowledge map or a short talk for classmates.做成一个小项目：一页知识图谱，或给同学讲一次。
  4. Write the reflection while it is fresh; it becomes a paragraph of your application later.趁记忆新鲜写下反思；它以后就是申请里的一段。

The core核心

A good profile is a few real things told clearly, with one thread running through them.好的个人档案，是几件真实的事讲清楚，并有一条贯穿的主线。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Few and deep beats many and shallow: one activity for a year looks better than ten once.少而深胜过多而浅：一项做一年，比十项各做一次好。
  2. For every activity keep three things: hours, evidence, and two or three sentences on what you learned.每项活动留三样：时数、证据、两三句学到了什么。
  3. Do not add new activities during exams or the first weeks of a new term.考试期和新学期头几周，不加新活动。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“A long list of activities impresses.” Readers remember a thread and a reflection, not a count.「活动列得越多越好」——读的人记住的是主线和反思，不是数量。

🧩 What makes it work好在哪里

One thread一条主线

The profile names one question — how can understanding the brain and mind help people? — and every entry connects to it.档案里写明一个问题——理解大脑与心灵如何帮到别人？——每一条经历都和它相连。

Behaviour, not adjectives写行为，不堆形容词

“Organized” appears as a weekly plan and an error log; “hard-working” as a practice routine kept every week.「有条理」写成每周计划和错题记录；「勤奋」写成每周坚持的练习习惯。

Others’ words他人的话

Real teachers and mentors say it better than you can. Ask them for a few sentences; the wording above is only a model.真实的老师和导师比你自己说更有力。请他们写几句；上面的措辞只是示范。

Examples are about an invented student. Copy the pattern, not the content.例子是虚构的学生。学写法，不要抄内容。

1 · Start with a verb动词开头

Built, led, organized, taught, designed, trained, performed, researched, completed.做了、带领、组织、教、设计、受训、演出、研究、完成。

2 · Add a number or a result写数字或结果

Hours, weeks, people, ranking, finished or not. Honest and small beats big and vague.时数、周数、人数、名次、完成与否。真实而小，胜过大而空。

3 · Say what you learned写学到了什么

One short clause: “learned to…”. It shows you reflect, not just attend.一个短句：「学会了……」。它说明你在反思，而不只是参加。

🍽️ Enrichment menu: what could I add?增彩活动菜单：我可以加什么？
