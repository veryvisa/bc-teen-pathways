The core核心

Planning is not a promise; it is a map you redraw every week.规划不是承诺，而是每周都可以重画的地图。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Big goal → stretches → this week → today. Only today has to be concrete.大目标 → 阶段 → 本周 → 今天。只有今天需要具体。
  2. Sleep, meals, outdoors and rest go in first; study fills what's left, and stops at a set time.睡眠、三餐、户外和休息先排；学习填进剩下的时间，并且到点就停。
  3. Tired day? Switch to the minimal day — it still counts.累的那天？切成最小版——同样算数。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“A good plan means studying every free minute.” A plan that leaves room for rest is the one you can keep for a whole term.「好计划就是把空闲时间都填满学习」——留出休息的计划，才能坚持一整个学期。
