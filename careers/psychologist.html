🔎 What the work is工作是什么

Clinical and counselling psychologists assess and treat difficulties such as anxiety, depression, learning differences and trauma, using evidence-based talk therapies; they cannot prescribe medicine. Research psychologists design studies about memory, development, emotion or social behaviour. Many people with a bachelor's in psychology work in related roles — research assistant, behaviour interventionist, HR, user research — while some continue to graduate school.临床和咨询心理学家评估并治疗焦虑、抑郁、学习差异、创伤等问题，使用有研究证据支持的谈话治疗；他们不能开药。研究型心理学家设计关于记忆、发展、情绪或社会行为的研究。很多心理学本科毕业生做相关工作——研究助理、行为干预师、人力资源、用户研究——也有人继续读研究生。

🃏 Flip to remember翻卡记一记

What does a Psychologist do, in one sentence?用一句话说心理学家做什么？

Understands how people think, feel and behave — and uses that to help them or to discover something new.理解人怎样思考、感受和行动——用它来帮助人，或发现新知识。

How long is the usual route after Grade 12?高中后通常要读多久？

About 9–10 years: a 4-year bachelor's, then graduate school; BC registration as a psychologist generally requires a doctoral degree — check CHCPBC.约 9–10 年：4 年本科，再读研究生；在 BC 注册为心理学家一般需要博士学位——以 CHCPBC 官网为准。

The most common mix-up about this job?关于这份工作最常见的误会？

“Psychologist and psychiatrist are the same.” A psychiatrist is a medical doctor; a psychologist is not.「心理学家和精神科医生一样」——精神科医生是医生，心理学家不是。
