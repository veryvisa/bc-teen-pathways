This is the brief on becoming a pharmacist in BC.

This career profile walkthrough is designed simply to let you know this job exists,

community health centers, research, industry, and government.

Expect 2,530 openings between 2025 and 2035 due to retirements, an aging population,

There's no direct high school entry.

organic chemistry, biology, biochemistry, statistics, and English,

followed by the four-year UBC Entry to Practice Doctor of Pharmacy program.

High school prerequisites are Chemistry 11 and 12, Precalculus 12,

Biology or Anatomy and Physiology 12, and English Studies 12.

Check the official page before you decide.

You're regulated by the College of Pharmacists of BC.

Finally, a typical day.

At 9, you check a new prescription against other medicines.

Giving flu shots at 1030.

By 13, you talk a worried parent through a child's antibiotic dosing.

You call a doctor about a high dose at 15,

finishing around 17 by reviewing a senior's whole medicine list to cut unnecessary pills.

You'll collaborate alongside a pharmacy technician, pharmacologist, toxicologist,

public health, inspector, epidemiology, health promotion.

The core核心

The medicines expert people can walk in and talk to, no appointment needed.大家不用预约就能走进去请教的「药物专家」。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Route: about two years of university prerequisites, then UBC's 4-year PharmD.路线：约两年大学先修课，再读 UBC 4 年药学博士。
  2. Work goes far beyond dispensing: vaccines, medication reviews, minor ailments, hospital rounds.工作远不止配药：疫苗、用药审查、常见小病、医院查房。
  3. Chemistry is the core school subject to keep strong.化学是高中最要保持好的科目。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“Pharmacists only work in stores.” Many work in hospitals, long-term care, research, industry and government.「药剂师只在药店」——很多人在医院、长期护理、科研、企业和政府工作。

🔎 What the work is工作是什么

Pharmacists make sure each person gets the right medicine and understands it: they review prescriptions for interactions and doses, counsel patients, give vaccines, manage chronic conditions with the care team, and in BC can treat some minor ailments and adapt prescriptions within their scope. Hospital pharmacists join ward rounds; others work in research, industry or policy.药剂师确保每个人拿到对的药并且真正理解它：审核处方的相互作用和剂量、给病人用药指导、接种疫苗、和医疗团队一起管理慢性病；在 BC，他们还能在执业范围内处理一些常见小病、调整处方。医院药剂师会参加病房查房；也有人在科研、企业或政策部门工作。

Outlook (WorkBC): 前景（WorkBC）：Significant openings from new job creation and retirements; growing demand as population ages; shortages in hospitals and small rural communities.新增岗位和退休带来大量空缺；人口老龄化使需求增长；医院和小型农村社区缺人。

You like chemistry and biology, explaining things kindly, and catching the detail others miss.适合喜欢化学和生物、愿意耐心讲解、能发现别人忽略的细节的人。
