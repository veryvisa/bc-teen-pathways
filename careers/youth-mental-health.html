The core核心

A youth mental-health unit is a team: child and youth care staff, social workers, psychologists, therapists and pharmacy work alongside doctors and nurses.青少年心理健康病房是一个团队：儿童与青少年关怀人员、社工、心理学家、各类治疗师和药房，与医生护士并肩工作。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Child and youth care (CYC) is the most direct route: a 2-year diploma or a 4-year bachelor's.儿童与青少年关怀（CYC）是最直接的路线：2 年文凭或 4 年本科。
  2. Youth units are run by the provincial health authority (PHSA) and regional ones such as Fraser, Island, Interior and Northern Health; community CYMH and ICY teams work outside hospitals.青少年病房由省卫生服务局（PHSA）和菲沙、岛屿、内陆、北部等地区卫生局运营；社区的 CYMH 和 ICY 团队在医院之外工作。
  3. Volunteering with youth (camps, tutoring, recreation programs) is the best first step in high school.高中阶段最好的第一步，是参加面向青少年的志愿活动（营地、辅导、康乐项目）。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“You need to be a doctor or nurse to work on a psychiatric unit.” Most of the daily support comes from other professions.「在精神科病房工作必须是医生或护士」——大部分日常支持来自其他职业。
