🔎 What the work is工作是什么

Health information professionals keep patient records accurate, coded and private; they manage health databases, ensure privacy law is followed, and prepare data that hospitals and governments use to plan. Health data analysts use statistics and code to answer questions like 'Where are wait times growing?' WorkBC notes a growing shortage, especially in rural areas.健康信息从业者保证病历准确、编码规范、隐私安全；管理健康数据库，确保遵守隐私法，整理医院和政府用来做规划的数据。健康数据分析师用统计和编程回答诸如「哪里的等候时间在变长？」这样的问题。WorkBC 指出人手短缺在加剧，农村地区尤甚。
