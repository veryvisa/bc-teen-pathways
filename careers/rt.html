that's absolutely perfect for science-minded folks who stay calm under pressure and literally keep people breathing.

🔎 What the work is工作是什么

Respiratory therapists assess breathing and lung function, run ventilators in intensive care, help at deliveries and resuscitations, give oxygen and inhaled medicines, and teach people with asthma or COPD to manage at home. It is a fast-paced hospital career that mixes physics, physiology and calm teamwork.呼吸治疗师评估呼吸和肺功能，在重症监护室操作呼吸机，参与分娩和抢救，给氧和吸入药物，并教哮喘或慢阻肺病人在家管理。这是节奏很快的医院职业，结合了物理、生理和冷静的团队协作。

Outlook (WorkBC): 前景（WorkBC）：Aging population raises demand; significant RT shortage in larger hospitals; pressure to reduce hospital stays may temper demand long term.人口老龄化推高需求；大型医院呼吸治疗师严重短缺；缩短住院时间的压力长期可能抑制需求。

You stay calm under pressure, like physics and anatomy, and want hands-on work.适合压力下保持冷静、喜欢物理和解剖、想做一线动手工作的人。
