🔎 What the work is工作是什么

Medical laboratory technologists analyse blood, tissue and other samples: they run chemistry analysers, identify bacteria, match blood for transfusions, examine cells under the microscope and check quality so results can be trusted. Doctors base many decisions on their numbers. WorkBC notes growing demand, including for genetic testing.医学检验技师分析血液、组织和其他样本：操作生化分析仪、鉴定细菌、为输血配血、在显微镜下看细胞、做质量控制，让结果值得信赖。医生的很多决定都基于他们给出的数字。WorkBC 提到需求在增长，包括基因检测。

Outlook (WorkBC): 前景（WorkBC）：Industry reports a current shortage in B.C., most acute in rural/remote areas; growing demand for clinical genetics testing.行业报告 BC 目前短缺，农村和偏远地区最严重；临床基因检测需求在增长。

You like labs, chemistry and biology, precision, and quiet focused work.适合喜欢实验室、化学和生物、讲究精确、喜欢安静专注工作的人。
