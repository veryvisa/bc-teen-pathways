🔎 What the work is工作是什么

Social workers help individuals and families through hard moments: a hospital discharge, a mental-health crisis, money or housing trouble, child safety, grief. They assess needs, make plans, connect people to services, provide counselling and advocate within systems. In BC the title 'social worker' is protected and registered with the BC College of Social Workers.社工在人们最难的时刻帮助个人和家庭：出院安排、心理危机、经济或住房困难、儿童安全、丧亲。他们评估需要、制定计划、连接服务、提供辅导，并在制度里替人争取。在 BC，「社工」这个头衔受保护，须在 BC 社工学院（BCCSW）注册。

🃏 Flip to remember翻卡记一记

What does a Social worker do, in one sentence?用一句话说社工做什么？

Connects people to what they need — housing, safety, support, services — and speaks up for them.把人和他们需要的东西连起来——住房、安全、支持、服务——并为他们发声。

How long is the usual route after Grade 12?高中后通常要读多久？

About 4 years: two years of university, then the BSW (entry at third year).约 4 年：先读两年大学，再从大三进入社工学士（BSW）。

The most common mix-up about this job?关于这份工作最常见的误会？

“Social work is only child protection.” It also covers hospitals, mental health, seniors, housing and policy.「社工就是儿童保护」——还包括医院、心理健康、长者、住房和政策。
