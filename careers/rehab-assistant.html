This is the brief on OT-PT rehabilitation assistance.

If the occupational or physical therapist is the architect who draws up the recovery plan,

the assistant is the builder who actually constructs it on the ground.

It's a fast-track healthcare career that fundamentally helps people regain their independence.

First, looking at the daily tasks, it's totally hands-on.

The therapist plans, but the assistant executes the whole thing and reports back.

We're talking about running walking practice after hip surgery, group balance classes, wheelchair transfers, or even practicing kitchen tasks.

Now, you might assume they just help injured athletes, right?

Actually, most clients are folks recovering from surgery, strokes, or managing chronic conditions.

Second, these builders are everywhere.

You'll find them in hospitals, long-term care, home care, community centers, clinics, and youth mental health units.

Well, BC has a severe shortage, especially out in rural and northern regions,

expecting 1,100 job openings by 2035, with median earnings around $51,000.

Finally, stepping into this field takes just a diploma, like Capilano University's 16-month program,

Okanagan College's two-year track, or Vancouver Community College.

It's basically a health care passport, getting you in fast while keeping doors wide open for a future master's degree.

Of course, programs and numbers change, so check the official pages before you decide.

🔎 What the work is工作是什么

Rehabilitation assistants carry out exercise and activity programs planned by occupational therapists (OTs) and physiotherapists (PTs): walking practice after surgery, strength and balance work, practising dressing or cooking, setting up equipment. They report progress back to the therapist. It is a fast route into hands-on health care, and some later continue to become OTs or PTs (both master's-level).康复助理执行职业治疗师（OT）和物理治疗师（PT）制定的运动和活动方案：术后练习走路、力量和平衡训练、练习穿衣做饭、安装辅助设备。他们把进展反馈给治疗师。这是进入一线健康工作的快速路径，也有人之后继续读成 OT 或 PT（都是硕士水平）。

Outlook (WorkBC): 前景（WorkBC）：B.C. faces a shortage; demand rising as population grows and ages; shortage most acute in rural and northern regions.BC 面临短缺；人口增长和老龄化使需求上升；农村和北部地区最缺。

You like being active, encouraging people, and seeing someone take a step they couldn't last week.适合好动、会鼓励人、喜欢看到别人做到上周还做不到的事的人。
