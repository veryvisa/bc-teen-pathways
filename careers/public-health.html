The core核心

Protects the health of whole communities — before anyone gets sick.在有人生病之前，守护整个社区的健康。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Public health works on whole communities, mostly before people get sick.公共卫生面向整个社区，主要在人生病之前起作用。
  2. Inspectors: BCIT degree + CIPHI certificate; epidemiologists often need a master's.卫生检查员：BCIT 学位＋CIPHI 证书；流行病学家常需硕士。
  3. Statistics and clear writing matter as much as biology.统计和清楚的写作与生物同样重要。

“Public health is just hospitals.” It happens in restaurants, schools, water systems and government.「公共卫生就是医院」——它发生在餐馆、学校、供水系统和政府里。

🔎 What the work is工作是什么

Public health professionals inspect restaurants, pools and drinking water, track outbreaks, study patterns of disease (epidemiology), and design programs that help people stay healthy — vaccination, healthy schools, harm reduction. They work for health authorities, the BC Centre for Disease Control, the provincial government and non-profits. Senior epidemiology roles often need a master's (MPH or MSc).公共卫生从业者检查餐馆、泳池和饮用水，追踪疫情，研究疾病的分布规律（流行病学），设计帮助人们保持健康的项目——疫苗接种、健康校园、减少伤害。他们在卫生局、BC 疾病控制中心、省政府和非营利机构工作。资深的流行病学岗位常需要硕士（MPH 或 MSc）。

You like science that helps many people at once, maps and data, and talking with the public.适合喜欢能一次帮很多人的科学、喜欢地图和数据、愿意和公众沟通的人。
