🔎 What the work is工作是什么

Child and youth care (CYC) practitioners build relationships with young people in schools, hospitals, youth centres, residential programs and community agencies. They help with emotions, routines, friendships and safety, run activity groups, and work alongside families, social workers and psychologists. WorkBC groups them with social and community service workers.儿童与青少年关怀（CYC）工作者在学校、医院、青少年中心、寄宿项目和社区机构里与年轻人建立关系。他们帮助孩子处理情绪、作息、交友和安全问题，带活动小组，并与家长、社工和心理学家一起工作。WorkBC 把他们归在「社会与社区服务工作者」类别里。

Outlook (WorkBC): 前景（WorkBC）：Broad career with many expected openings; new positions involve caring for the growing elderly population.职业面广，预计空缺多；新岗位涉及照顾日益增多的老年人口。
