The core核心

There are many ways to work in health care that are not doctor or nurse — some start after one year of college, others after graduate school.在医疗健康领域工作有很多不是医生护士的路——有的读一年学院就能开始，有的要读到研究生。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Pick a direction by what you like doing (lab, people, data, movement), not by prestige.按你喜欢做的事（实验、和人打交道、数据、运动）选方向，而不是按名气。
  2. English 12, Chemistry 11 and a Grade 12 math keep most of these doors open.英语 12、化学 11 和一门 12 年级数学能让大多数门保持开着。
  3. Many routes start with a general first year, so you can decide later.很多路线从普通大一开始，可以晚点再决定。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“If I don't get into one program, health care is closed to me.” There are short college routes, transfer routes and second chances everywhere.「这个项目进不去，健康行业就和我无缘了」——处处都有短期学院路线、转学路线和第二次机会。

🔤 Glossary术语表 · Glossary

Diploma / certificate文凭 / 证书
A college credential, usually 1–3 years (certificate: shorter).学院颁发的资格，一般 1–3 年（证书更短）。
Bachelor's degree本科学位
A university degree, usually 4 years (BA, BSc, BSW…).大学学位，一般 4 年（BA、BSc、BSW 等）。
Master's degree硕士学位
Graduate degree after a bachelor's, often 2 years (e.g., SLP, OT).本科之后的研究生学位，常为 2 年（如 SLP、OT）。
Doctorate (PhD, PsyD)博士学位
The highest degree; needed for registered psychologists and to lead research.最高学位；注册心理学家和主导研究需要。
Prerequisite先修课
A course you must finish before you can enter a program or another course.进入某个项目或下一门课之前必须完成的课。
Regulator / College监管机构 / 学院（College）
In BC health care, a 'College' licenses and disciplines a profession — it is not a school.在 BC 医疗领域，「College」负责发执照、管纪律——不是学校。
Registration / licence注册 / 执照
Permission from the regulator to use a protected title and practise.监管机构许可你使用受保护头衔并执业。
NOC国家职业分类
Canada's National Occupational Classification code that WorkBC uses to group jobs.加拿大国家职业分类代码，WorkBC 用它给职业分组。
Median salary收入中位数
The middle value: half earn more, half earn less. Starting pay is usually lower.中间值：一半人高于它，一半低于它。起薪通常更低。
Practicum / clinical placement实习 / 临床实习
Supervised work in a real workplace, built into many health programs.在真实单位里有督导的实习，很多健康项目都包含。
Post-baccalaureate学士后
A program you take after you already have a bachelor's degree.已有本科学位之后再读的项目。
Transfer credit转学分
Courses from one BC school counted at another (see the BC Transfer Guide).在一所 BC 学校读的课被另一所承认（见 BC 转学指南）。
