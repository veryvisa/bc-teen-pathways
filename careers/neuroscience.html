🔎 What the work is工作是什么

Neuroscientists study how neurons signal, how memories form, how the brain develops and what goes wrong in conditions like Parkinson's or stroke. They use microscopes, brain imaging, electrical recordings, genetics and computer models. A BSc leads to research-assistant, lab-technician, data and science-communication roles; leading your own lab usually means a PhD.神经科学家研究神经元怎样传递信号、记忆怎样形成、大脑怎样发育，以及帕金森病、中风等情况下哪里出了问题。他们用显微镜、脑成像、电生理记录、遗传学和计算机模型。本科可以做研究助理、实验室技术员、数据和科普类工作；要带自己的实验室，通常需要博士。

You like biology plus a bit of coding and physics, and you enjoy puzzles that take months.适合喜欢生物、也愿意学点编程和物理、享受花几个月解一道谜的人。
