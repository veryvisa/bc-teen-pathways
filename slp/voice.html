The core核心

Voice = breath × vocal-fold vibration × resonance; protect it by reducing collision force, train it by getting more sound for less effort.声音＝呼吸供气 × 声带振动 × 口鼻共鸣；保护嗓子的核心是减少声带碰撞的力量，练嗓的核心是用更少的力气得到更响更亮的声音。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Your vocal folds vibrate a hundred or two hundred times a second or more. Shouting, forceful throat clearing and long stretches of whispering all add to the impact or tension.声带每秒振动一两百次以上，喊叫、用力清嗓、长时间耳语都会加重碰撞或紧张。
  2. Semi-occluded vocal tract practice (straw phonation, lip trills, humming) uses back-pressure in the mouth so the vocal folds vibrate with less effort.半闭合声道（吸管发声、唇颤音、哼鸣）利用口腔里的回压让声带更省力地振动。
  3. If hoarseness lasts more than about 3–4 weeks, or comes with pain or trouble breathing, see a doctor rather than practising on your own.声音嘶哑持续超过约 3–4 周、伴随疼痛或呼吸困难，要看医生，不靠自练。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

Thinking whispering "protects" your voice. Forceful whispering also strains the throat. Reading a voice hygiene list without changing how you actually use your voice has limited effect (Vermeulen et al. 2022).以为耳语能「保护」嗓子——用力的耳语同样会让喉部紧张；只读一份嗓音卫生清单而不改变实际用嗓习惯，效果有限（Vermeulen et al. 2022）。

🗺️ The map of this area这个领域的图谱

Voice is its own area of speech-language pathology. Teens go through voice change and often use their voice a lot on the sports field, in choir and in debate. Voice care and semi-occluded vocal tract practice are low-risk and can be done at home.

嗓音是 SLP 的独立领域。青少年经历变声，又常在球场、合唱、辩论中大量用嗓；嗓音保健与半闭合声道练习风险低、可在家做。

💡 Why it works — the core ideas为什么这样练——核心原理

Where voice comes from声音从哪来

The lungs push out air. In the larynx, two vocal folds open and close very quickly, like two lips, and make a buzzing basic sound. The throat, mouth and nose shape it into your own tone.

肺推出气流，喉里的两片声带像两片嘴唇一样快速开合，产生「嗡嗡」的原声；喉咙、口腔、鼻腔把它塑造成你的音色。

📎 Sources来源: NIH: How Does the Human Body Produce Voice and Speech? (video)

Why a straw can train your voice吸管为什么能练嗓

When your lips are nearly closed (or hold a straw), air pressure builds in the vocal tract. That pressure helps the vocal folds vibrate more easily and lightly. This is called semi-occluded vocal tract practice.

嘴唇几乎合上（或含一根吸管），声道里的气压升高，反过来帮声带更容易、更轻地振动——这叫半闭合声道。

📎 Sources来源: Titze IR (2006). Voice training and therapy with a semi-occluded vocal tract: rationale and scientific underpinnings. Journal of Speech, Language, and Hearing Research 49(2).

Knowing and doing are different things保健知识和保健行为是两回事

Research shows that giving out a voice hygiene leaflet alone has limited effect, and it works better combined with direct voice practice. Drinking enough water helps the voice to some degree, but the strength of evidence varies.

研究显示只发一份嗓音卫生手册效果有限，结合直接的发声练习效果更好；补水对嗓音有一定好处但证据强弱不一。

📎 Sources来源: Roy N, Grey SD, Simon M, et al. (2001). An evaluation of the effects of two treatment approaches for teachers with voice disorders: a prospective randomized clinical trial. Journal of Speech, Language, and Hearing Research 44(2). · Vermeulen R, et al. (2022). Journal of Voice 33(1).

Voice change is a normal process变声是正常过程

In puberty the larynx grows, and both boys' and girls' voices change (more clearly in boys). Your pitch may be unsteady or crack for a while. That is development and does not need to be "corrected".

青春期喉部长大，男生女生的声音都会变（男生更明显），可能有一段时间音高不稳、破音；这是发育，不需要「矫正」。

📎 Sources来源: ASHA: Voice Disorders (public)

Vocal hygiene as behaviour change把嗓音卫生变成行为

Evidence证据 B · At least one controlled trial至少一项对照试验支持Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Pick the 2 habits from the list you do most (for example shouting, frequent throat clearing) and keep a behaviour log for a week. Each time you want to clear your throat, take a small sip of water or swallow gently instead.

从清单里挑 2 条最常犯的（例如喊叫、频繁清嗓）做一周行为记录：每次想清嗓就改为喝一小口水或轻轻吞咽。

  1. Read the NIDCD voice-care checklist读 NIDCD 嗓音保护清单
  2. Choose 2 items as your goals选 2 条做目标
  3. Count them each day with the web counter用网页计数器记每天次数
  4. Compare after one week一周后对比

⏱️ How much练多少 Keep the log with you every day, one round per week每天随身记录，一周一轮

Why this evidence level?为什么是这个证据等级？

Randomised trials with teachers and other professional voice users show that voice hygiene education alone is not as good as direct voice therapy. Systematic reviews say it works better combined with other methods. A systematic review supports hydration as helpful for some acoustic measures.

对教师等职业用嗓者的随机试验显示，单纯嗓音卫生教育不如直接的发声治疗；系统综述认为与其他方法合用更有效。补水有系统综述支持对部分声学指标有益。

Semi-occluded vocal tract exercises (straw phonation, lip trills)半闭合声道练习（吸管发声、唇颤音）

Evidence证据 B · At least one controlled trial至少一项对照试验支持Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Hold an ordinary straw in your mouth and gently say "oo" at a comfortable pitch, then do slides from low to high. A lip trill is making the lips flutter ("brrr") while you voice.

含住一根普通吸管，用舒服的音高轻轻发「呜——」，再做从低到高的滑音；唇颤音是让嘴唇「嘟噜噜」振动着发声。

  1. Put the straw in your mouth and seal your lips around it吸管放进嘴里，嘴唇包紧
  2. Hold a steady sound for 5 seconds, 5 times平稳发声 5 秒 × 5 次
  3. Do pitch slides, 5 times音高滑动 5 次
  4. Take the straw out and say "mm-hmm" and a few words, noticing the same easy feeling拿掉吸管说「mm-hmm」和几个词，体会同样省力的感觉

⏱️ How much练多少 2–3 times a day, about 5 minutes each每天 2–3 次，每次约 5 分钟

⚠️ If you feel dizzy, stop and rest. If you feel pain or hoarseness gets worse, stop practising and see a doctor.头晕就停下休息；有疼痛或嘶哑加重就停练并就医。
Why this evidence level?为什么是这个证据等级？

Two randomised controlled trials (comparing two SOVT programmes; and an SOVT treatment procedure for people with behavioural voice disorders) showed improved voice measures. For the theory see Titze 2006. Most participants were adults or people with voice problems.

两项随机对照试验（两种 SOVT 方案对比；行为性嗓音障碍者的 SOVT 治疗程序）显示嗓音指标改善；理论依据见 Titze 2006。研究对象多为成人或有嗓音问题者。

📎 Sources来源: Titze IR (2006). Voice training and therapy with a semi-occluded vocal tract: rationale and scientific underpinnings. Journal of Speech, Language, and Hearing Research 49(2). · Kapsner-Smith MR, Hunter EJ, Kirkham K, Cox K, Titze IR (2015). A randomized controlled trial of two semi-occluded vocal tract voice therapy protocols. Journal of Speech, Language, and Hearing Research 58(3). · Guzman M, Bertucci D, Pacheco C, Leiva C (2020). Effectiveness of a physiologic voice therapy program based on different semioccluded vocal tract exercises in subjects with behavioral dysphonia: a randomized controlled trial. Journal of Communication Disorders 87. · Columbia Center for Voice and Swallowing: SOVT exercises (video)

Evidence comes from studies led by professionals. Very little research has tested learning this on your own.这条证据来自专业人员带领的研究；自己在家练的效果，研究很少。

Resonant voice practice共鸣嗓音练习

Evidence证据 C · Expert consensus or uncontrolled studies专家共识或无对照研究Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Use "mm-hmm" (like the hum you make when you agree) to find a light buzzing feeling around the lips and the bridge of the nose. Then carry that feeling into words that start with m, into phrases, and into everyday speech.

用「嗯—嗯」（像同意时的哼声）找到嘴唇和鼻梁周围轻微的振动感，再把这种感觉带进 m 开头的词、短语和日常说话。

  1. Hum "mm-hmm" 10 times and find the buzz in the mask area of the face哼「mm-hmm」10 次，找面罩区振动
  2. Words that start with m: me, my, more...m 开头的词：me、my、more…
  3. A short sentence: My mom makes muffins短句：My mom makes muffins
  4. Read a passage aloud and keep the buzzing feeling朗读一段，保持振动感

⏱️ How much练多少 5–10 minutes a day每天 5–10 分钟

Why this evidence level?为什么是这个证据等级？

系统综述显示共鸣嗓音治疗对嗓音质量有改善，但研究数量和质量有限；有教师群体的对照研究支持。

🫶 The feelings side心理这一面

  • Voice is closely tied to your sense of identity. During voice change, or when someone says your voice sounds bad, it is easy to not want to speak. Think of your voice as an instrument you can train, not a personality label.声音和身份感紧密相连，变声期或被评价「声音难听」时容易不想开口；把声音看作可以训练的乐器，而不是性格标签。
  • When you are nervous the muscles in the throat tighten too. Speaking anxiety and voice fatigue can make each other worse, and breathing and resonance practice can help with both.紧张时喉部肌肉也会收紧，说话焦虑和嗓音疲劳可能互相加重；呼吸与共鸣练习对两者都有帮助。

📎 Sources来源: ASHA: Voice Disorders (public)

自己练是一条真实可行的路；想找受过训练的人听一听，也完全正常——不需要符合下面任何一条才可以去问，卡在一步上就足够了。特别值得去问问的情况：

  • Hoarseness or a changed voice lasts about 3–4 weeks or more声音嘶哑或改变持续约 3–4 周以上

  • Your voice suddenly disappears声音突然消失
