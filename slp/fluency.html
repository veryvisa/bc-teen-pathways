The core核心

Stuttering is a timing disruption in speech plus the tension and avoidance that grow around it; practice aims for easier speech and less avoidance, not perfect fluency.口吃是说话的「时间与节奏」偶尔卡住，加上身体紧张和想躲开的心理；练习的目标是说得更轻松、躲得更少，而不是永远不卡。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Stuttering has three layers: the visible bumps in speech (repeating, stretching, blocking), body tension and extra movements, and the unseen feelings and avoidance. This is the "iceberg model" (Sheehan).口吃的三层：看得见的卡顿（重复、拉长、阻塞）、身体紧张与附加动作、看不见的感受与回避——就是「冰山模型」（Sheehan）。
  2. There are two main schools of method. Fluency shaping changes how you speak so that bumps happen less. Stuttering modification means getting through a bump in a calmer way. Modern approaches often combine both with a psychological focus.两大方法流派：流利塑形（改变说话方式，让卡顿少发生）与口吃修正（卡住时用更轻松的方式过去）；现代做法常把两者与心理取向结合。
  3. Avoidance (swapping words, staying quiet, pretending not to know) keeps anxiety going over time, so cutting down avoidance is an important goal in itself.回避（换词、不发言、假装不知道）会让焦虑长期维持，减少回避本身就是重要目标。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

Treating "no bumps at all" as the measure of success. The harder you try to control your speech, the tighter you get. In research, people who stutter also say stuttering is much more than the bumps (Tichenor & Yaruss 2019).把「完全不卡」当成功标准，结果越用力控制越紧张；研究里口吃者自己也认为口吃远不止卡顿（Tichenor & Yaruss 2019）。

💡 Why it works — the core ideas为什么这样练——核心原理

Typical disfluency is not the same as stuttering正常不流畅和口吃不一样

Everyone says "um" and "like", or restarts half a sentence. Stuttering more often means repeating part of a word (b-b-ball), stretching a sound, or a "block" where no sound comes out at all, and it usually comes with tension.

人人都会说「嗯、那个」、重说半句。口吃更常见的是词的一部分反复（b-b-ball）、声音拉长、或者完全发不出声的「阻塞」，而且常伴随紧张感。

📎 Sources来源: ASHA consumer: Stuttering and Cluttering

Stuttering is not caused by nerves口吃不是紧张「造成」的

Research points to genetics and differences in the brain's speech-motor network. Feelings and situations can make it more or less frequent, but they are not the root cause. So "just relax" is not accurate advice.

研究指向遗传和大脑言语运动网络的差异，情绪和场合会让它变多或变少，但不是根源。所以「放松一点就好了」并不准确。

📎 Sources来源: NIDCD: Stuttering · Smith A, Weber C (2017). How stuttering develops: the multifactorial dynamic pathways theory. Journal of Speech, Language, and Hearing Research 60(9).

The iceberg model冰山模型

Above the water are the bumps other people can hear. Below the water are shame, fear and avoidance. For many people the part below is much bigger than the part above, so in self-study you practise both parts.

水面上是别人听得到的卡顿，水面下是羞耻、害怕、回避。很多人水面下比水面上大得多；自学时两部分都要练。

📎 Sources来源: Stuttering Foundation: Self-Therapy for the Stutterer · Tichenor SE, Yaruss JS (2019). Stuttering as defined by adults who stutter. Journal of Speech, Language, and Hearing Research 62(12).

The two routes can be used together两条路线可以合起来用

Fluency shaping is like driving in a smoother way. Stuttering modification is like learning how to steady the car when it skids. Systematic reviews show evidence for both, but mostly from programmes guided by a therapist. The self-study versions have not been tested on their own.

流利塑形像「换一种更顺的开车方式」，口吃修正像「打滑时怎么稳住」。两者的系统综述都有证据，但多来自有治疗师指导的项目；自学版效果没有被单独检验过。

The teen years are an important window青春期是重要窗口

Many children who start stuttering before school stop on their own. For many people whose stuttering continues into the teen years, it doesn't simply fade — which is why learning to speak more easily and to avoid less is worth starting now, rather than waiting.

学龄前开始口吃的孩子，有不少会自然好转；而到青少年仍在的，很多人不会自己消失——所以与其等，不如现在就开始练习说得更轻松、躲得更少。

📎 Sources来源: Yairi E, Ambrose N (2013). Epidemiology of stuttering: 21st century advances. Journal of Fluency Disorders 38(2).

What about cluttering (talking too fast)?迅吃（说得太快）是怎么回事？

Cluttering is a different fluency pattern: speech that is too fast or uneven, with syllables squashed or dropped, so listeners lose the thread. People who clutter often don't notice it themselves. Slowing down in short chunks, with clear pauses between meaning groups (the rate and phrasing method below), is the usual starting point.

迅吃是另一种流利度模式：说得太快或快慢不均，音节被挤在一起或吞掉，听的人跟不上。迅吃的人自己常常察觉不到。按意群分成短块、块与块之间清楚地停一下（下面的「放慢与按意群停顿」）是常见的起点。

📎 Sources来源: ASHA consumer: Stuttering and Cluttering

Identification & self-observation识别与自我观察

Evidence证据 C · Expert consensus or uncontrolled studies专家共识或无对照研究Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Don't change anything yet, just look. Record 2 minutes of natural speech. When you play it back, count which kind of bump happens most, where your body is working hard, and where you swapped a word.

先不改，只看：录一段 2 分钟的自然说话，回放时数一数哪种卡顿最多、身体哪里在用力、你在哪里换了词。

  1. Record 30 seconds to 2 minutes (half reading aloud, half free talking) — 30 seconds is a fine first try录 30 秒到 2 分钟（一半朗读、一半自由说）——第一次录 30 秒就很好
  2. Play it back and tick repetitions, stretches and blocks on the observation sheet (PDF handbook) or note them in the log below回放，在观察表（PDF 手册里）上勾出重复、拉长、阻塞，或记在下面的记录里
  3. Note where the tension is (lips, jaw, throat, breathing)标出紧张部位（嘴唇、下巴、喉咙、呼吸）
  4. Write down how many times you swapped a word or avoided something this time写下这次换词或回避了几次

⏱️ How much练多少 5 minutes a day for 1–2 weeks每天 5 分钟，连续 1–2 周

⚠️ Observe, don't criticise. If listening back feels very hard, start with just 30 seconds.只观察、不批评；如果回放让你很难受，先只听 30 秒。

This is the first stage of stuttering modification. Self-Therapy for the Stutterer and the Van Riper approach both start here. It comes from clinical tradition and expert consensus, and there are no separate controlled studies of it.

口吃修正法的第一阶段，Self-Therapy for the Stutterer 与 Van Riper 体系都从这里开始；属于临床传统与专家共识，缺单独的对照研究。

Evidence comes from studies led by professionals. Very little research has tested learning this on your own.这条证据来自专业人员带领的研究；自己在家练的效果，研究很少。

Desensitisation & voluntary stuttering减敏与自愿口吃

Evidence证据 C · Expert consensus or uncontrolled studies专家共识或无对照研究Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

In a safe setting, stutter on purpose in an easy way by repeating or stretching one sound (for example "I-I-I want..."). This takes away the scary power of a bump and also reduces the word-swapping you do to dodge it.

在安全的场合故意轻松地重复或拉长一个音（比如「我-我-我想要…」），让「卡顿」失去吓人的力量，也减少为了躲它而换词。

  1. First do it 5 times alone in front of a mirror先独自对镜子做 5 次
  2. Then do it into a recorder and listen. It is not as bad as you feared再对录音机做，听听其实没那么糟
  3. Do it once with someone you trust对一个信任的人做 1 次
  4. Do it once in a low-risk place (asking directions, ordering food)在低风险场合（问路、点餐）做 1 次
  5. After each time, give yourself a 0–10 tension score每次之后打一个 0–10 的紧张分

⏱️ How much练多少 3–5 times a day, moving up the ladder week by week每天 3–5 次，按阶梯逐周往上

🔗 American Institute for Stuttering: What is Voluntary Stuttering?

⚠️ Voluntary stuttering should be easy and under your control. It is not an imitation of your worst bumps. If a step feels too hard, go back one step.自愿口吃要「轻松、可控」，不是模仿最严重的卡顿；觉得太难就退回上一级。
Why this evidence level?为什么是这个证据等级？

Studies from the point of view of people who stutter support it (Byrd et al. 2016 surveyed their views on voluntary stuttering), and it is part of most combined programmes. There are no separate randomised controlled trials.

有研究从口吃者视角支持它（Byrd et al. 2016 调查了口吃者对自愿口吃的看法），是多数综合项目的组成部分；缺单独的随机对照试验。

📎 Sources来源: Byrd CT, Gkalitsiou Z, Donaher J, Stergiou E (2016). The client's perspective on voluntary stuttering. American Journal of Speech-Language Pathology 25(3). · American Institute for Stuttering: What is Voluntary Stuttering? (video)

Rate control & phrasing放慢与按意群停顿

Evidence证据 C · Expert consensus or uncontrolled studies专家共识或无对照研究Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Split a sentence into chunks by meaning, pause and take a breath between chunks, and say each chunk a little more slowly. This is especially useful for cluttering (speaking too fast and swallowing sounds).

按意思把一句话切成几块，在块与块之间停一下、吸一口气，每块用略慢的速度说完。对迅吃（说太快、吞音）尤其有用。

  1. Underline the meaning groups in a text with /在文本上用 / 标出意群
  2. Read along with the web metronome, one syllable per beat跟网页节拍器每拍一个音节读
  3. Take away the metronome and keep only the pauses去掉节拍器只保留停顿
  4. Give a 1-minute introduction while watching a timer对着计时器做 1 分钟介绍

⏱️ How much练多少 5–10 minutes a day每天 5–10 分钟

CBT and ACT for speech-related anxiety认知行为与接纳承诺取向（处理说话焦虑）

Evidence证据 B · At least one controlled trial至少一项对照试验支持Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Find thoughts like "If I get stuck, everyone will look down on me" and test them against evidence. Learn to speak up even while feeling nervous, and choose what to do by your own values (wanting to make friends, wanting to speak in class).

找出「我一卡大家就会看不起我」这类想法，用证据检验；学会带着紧张照样开口，按自己的价值（想交朋友、想发言）决定做什么。

  1. Write down a speaking situation you are afraid of and your prediction写下一个害怕的说话场景和预测
  2. List the evidence for and against that prediction列出支持与反对这个预测的证据
  3. Design a small experiment to test it设计一个小实验去检验
  4. Afterwards, compare your prediction with what really happened做完后对比预测与真实结果
  5. ACT: say the thought as "I notice I'm having the thought that..."ACT：把念头说成「我注意到我有一个念头说…」

⏱️ How much练多少 2–3 times a week, 15 minutes each每周 2–3 次，每次 15 分钟

⚠️ If anxiety is affecting school, sleep or eating, please read "When to seek help" in this area.如果焦虑影响到上学、睡眠或吃饭，请看本领域「何时找专业人士」。

A controlled trial of a CBT package for adults who stutter (with exposure and cognitive restructuring) showed less anxiety and avoidance, but stuttering frequency did not always go down. A small ACT trial showed better psychosocial outcomes.

口吃成人的 CBT 包（含暴露与认知重构）对照试验显示焦虑与回避下降，但口吃频率不一定下降；ACT 小样本试验显示心理社会结果改善。

Fluency-shaping targets: easy onset, light contact, continuous phonation流利塑形：轻起音、轻接触、连续发声

Evidence证据 B · At least one controlled trial至少一项对照试验支持Practise with care — read the note可以自己练，但先看提醒

For words that start with a vowel, begin the sound gently with a little breath. For words that start with a consonant, let the lips and tongue touch lightly and move on. Don't break the breath between words, so they flow as if joined together.

元音开头的词用轻柔、带一点气的方式起声；辅音开头的词让嘴唇和舌头轻轻碰到就走；词与词之间不断气、像连在一起。

  1. Start with single words (apple, only, open)先在单个词上练（apple、only、open）
  2. Move to short sentences再到短句
  3. Read sentences slowly with the metronome below — start at the Slow preset (70 BPM), one syllable per beat跟着下面的节拍器慢慢读句子——从「慢」档（70 BPM）开始，每拍一个音节
  4. Gradually speed up to close to natural speed逐步加快到接近自然速度
  5. Compare recordings for naturalness: give your own naturalness a score from 1 to 9录音对比自然度：给自己的自然度打 1–9 分

Stuttering modification: cancellation, pull-out, preparatory set口吃修正：取消、拉出、预备组

Evidence证据 C · Expert consensus or uncontrolled studies专家共识或无对照研究Practise with care — read the note可以自己练，但先看提醒

After a bump, pause and say it again in an easy way (cancellation). In the middle of a bump, slowly relax, stretch and slide out of it (pull-out). When you sense a bump coming, start the sound in an easy way ahead of time (preparatory set).

卡住后停一下再用轻松的方式重说一遍（取消）；卡住当中慢慢放松、拉长、滑出来（拉出）；预感要卡时提前用轻松的方式起这个音（预备组）。

  1. Practise cancellation first: find a bump in a recording and say it again in an easier version先练「取消」：在录音里找到卡顿处，重说一遍更轻松的版本
  2. Then pull-out: make a light bump on purpose, then slide out slowly再练「拉出」：故意制造一个轻的卡顿，再慢慢滑出
