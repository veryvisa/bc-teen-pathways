The core核心

Language has five layers—sounds, word structure, sentences, meaning, use; clear expression rests on deep vocabulary and story structure, comprehension on building a mental model as you listen.语言有五层：声音、词的构造、句子、意思、使用；表达清楚靠的是词汇深度与叙事结构，理解靠的是边听边建「心里的模型」。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Meaning (what words mean and how they relate) and grammar (how sentences are built) keep growing through the teen years. Subject vocabulary matters most.语义（词义与词与词的关系）和句法（句子结构）是青少年阶段继续发展的部分，学科词汇尤其重要。
  2. Story structure (setting, problem, attempt, outcome, feeling) is a handy frame for organising what you say out loud.叙事结构（背景—问题—尝试—结果—感受）是组织口头表达的通用框架。
  3. Language ability is not the same as intelligence. People with DLD can be very smart.「语言能力」不等于聪明程度；DLD 的人可以非常聪明。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

Memorising a word list with only the Chinese meaning. Deep vocabulary means knowing examples, non-examples, similar words, word roots and how the word works in different settings.背单词表只记中文意思；深度词汇需要知道例子、反例、近义词、词根和在不同语境里的用法。

🗺️ The map of this area这个领域的图谱

Language (not speech) is the second big area of speech-language pathology. Developmental language disorder (DLD) affects about 7% of school-age children (Norbury et al. 2016), yet it is rarely talked about. Even with no disorder at all, getting your thoughts across clearly is a skill every teenager can use.

语言（不是言语）是 SLP 的第二大块。发育性语言障碍（DLD）约占学龄儿童的 7%（Norbury et al. 2016），却很少被听说；即使没有障碍，「把想法组织清楚」也是每个青少年都用得上的能力。

💡 Why it works — the core ideas为什么这样练——核心原理

Speech and language are two different things言语和语言是两回事

Speech is how smoothly and clearly you say things. Language is whether you understand, and how well you organise what you mean. Someone can pronounce every sound perfectly and still find organising ideas hard, and the reverse can be true too.

「说得顺不顺、音准不准」是言语；「懂不懂、说的内容组织得好不好」是语言。一个人可以发音完美但组织困难，反过来也一样。

📎 Sources来源: ASHA: What Is Speech? What Is Language?

DLD is commonDLD 很常见

An international Delphi consensus (CATALISE) uses the name developmental language disorder for language difficulties that have no clear cause and keep affecting daily life. Population studies estimate about 1 child in every 14.

国际 Delphi 共识（CATALISE）把没有明确原因、持续影响生活的语言困难叫做发育性语言障碍；人群研究估计约每 14 个孩子里有 1 个。

Phase 2 of CATALISE: a multinational and multidisciplinary Delphi consensus study of problems with language development: terminology. Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry 58(10). · Norbury CF, Gooch D, Wray C, et al. (2016). The impact of nonverbal ability on prevalence and clinical presentation of language disorder: evidence from a population study. Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry 57(11).

Story practice has solid evidence叙事练习有扎实的证据

Several systematic reviews and meta-analyses show that narrative work improves storytelling and understanding in school-age children. In UK secondary schools, one randomised controlled trial found that narrative and vocabulary training for teenagers worked.

多篇系统综述与元分析显示叙事干预能改善学龄儿童讲故事与理解；在英国中学里，一项随机对照试验显示针对青少年的叙事与词汇训练有效。

International Journal of Language & Communication Disorders 54(4). · Spencer TD, Petersen DB (2020). Narrative intervention: principles to practice. Teen vocabulary needs to be taught deeply青少年词汇要「教得深」

Systematic reviews and classroom trials with teenagers who have language difficulties support three things: teaching subject words directly, using them again and again in different settings, and adding word roots and endings. This works better than reading definitions alone.

面向语言障碍青少年的系统综述与课堂试验支持：明确教学科词汇、反复在不同语境中使用、加入词根词缀，比只看定义有效。

Journal of Speech, Language, and Hearing Research 62(8).

Story-grammar retell故事语法复述

Evidence证据 A · Systematic reviews support it有系统综述支持Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Watch a 3–5 minute clip or read a short story. Sort it into a six-box chart: character, setting, problem, attempt, outcome, feeling. Then retell it with the chart, retell it again without the chart, and record yourself.

看一个 3–5 分钟的短片或读一个短故事，用「人物—背景—问题—尝试—结果—感受」六格图整理，再不看图复述一遍并录音。

  1. Pick your material选材料
  2. Fill in the six-box chart填六格图
  3. Retell using the chart看图复述
  4. Retell without the chart and record it不看图复述并录音
  5. Check the chart to see which box you left out对照六格图检查漏了哪格

⏱️ How much练多少 3 times a week, 10 minutes each每周 3 次，每次 10 分钟

Narrative work is supported by systematic reviews and meta-analyses (mostly with school-age children). A randomised trial with UK secondary students supports the teen version. In the studies, specialists or trained school staff led the sessions.

叙事干预有系统综述与元分析支持（主要是学龄儿童）；英国中学生随机对照试验支持青少年版本。研究中由专业人员或经过培训的教职员带领。

Personal-narrative practice讲自己的经历

Evidence证据 C · Expert consensus or uncontrolled studies专家共识或无对照研究Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Use the same frame to tell about one real small event (a weekend, a mistake, a time you helped someone). Practise setting the scene first, then the problem, then the outcome and what you thought.

用同一个框架讲一件真实的小事（周末、一次失误、一次帮助别人），重点练「先交代背景，再讲问题，最后说结果和想法」。

  1. Write 3 keywords as prompts写 3 个关键词提示
  2. Tell it aloud for 1 minute and record it1 分钟口述并录音
  3. Play it back and check the order and the linking words (then, because, so, however)回放检查顺序与连接词（then, because, so, however）
  4. Tell it a second time with improvements第二遍改进

⏱️ How much练多少 2–3 times a week每周 2–3 次

⚠️ Only tell things you are happy to share. Keep your recordings on your own device.只讲自己愿意分享的事；练习录音保存在自己设备上。

Robust vocabulary (word maps, morphology)深度词汇：词义图与词根

Evidence证据 B · At least one controlled trial至少一项对照试验支持Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Each week choose 5 subject words. For each one, make a word map: the meaning in your own words, an example, a non-example, similar and opposite words, the word root or ending, and which class it belongs to.

每周选 5 个学科词，给每个词做一张词义图：定义（用自己的话）、例子、反例、近义/反义词、词根词缀、能用在哪门课。

  1. Choose 5 words (from your textbook)选 5 个词（来自课本）
  2. Make the word map做词义图
  3. Make 2 sentences with each word and say them aloud每个词造 2 个句子并说出来
  4. Review on days 2, 4 and 7 (spaced review)第 2、4、7 天复习（间隔重复）

⏱️ How much练多少 5 words a week, 5 minutes of review a day每周 5 词，每天 5 分钟复习

Why this evidence level?为什么是这个证据等级？

Systematic reviews and classroom comparison studies with teenagers who have language difficulties support clear, deep vocabulary teaching. A meta-analysis supports teaching word parts (roots and endings) as helpful for reading and writing.

面向语言障碍青少年的系统综述与课堂对照研究支持明确、深度的词汇教学；形态学（词根词缀）教学有元分析支持对读写有益。

Listen-and-summarise听后总结

Evidence证据 C · Expert consensus or uncontrolled studies专家共识或无对照研究Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Listen to a 2–3 minute explanation or podcast. Pause, then spend 30 seconds saying the main idea and 2 supporting details. Record yourself and compare with the original.

听一段 2–3 分钟的讲解或播客，暂停后用 30 秒说出主要观点和 2 个支持细节，录音后与原文对照。

  1. Listen once without taking notes听一遍不记笔记
  2. Give a 30-second spoken summary and record it30 秒口头总结并录音
  3. Listen again to check再听一遍核对
  4. Add the points you missed and say it again补上漏掉的要点重说

⏱️ How much练多少 3 times a week每周 3 次

Why this evidence level?为什么是这个证据等级？

A scoping review of spoken-comprehension work found many different methods and limited evidence overall. Summarising has stronger evidence in reading (see the language and literacy area).

口语理解干预的范围综述发现方法多样、证据整体有限；总结策略在阅读领域的证据更强（见读写领域）。

Comprehension monitoring & clarification理解监控与澄清提问

Evidence证据 C · Expert consensus or uncontrolled studies专家共识或无对照研究Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

While listening, quietly ask yourself, "What did I just miss?" Practise three clarifying lines: "Do you mean ...?", "Can you give an example?" and "What does this word mean?"

听讲时在心里问「我刚才哪里没懂？」，练习三种澄清句：「你是说…吗？」「能举个例子吗？」「这个词是什么意思？」。

  1. Listen to an explanation and mark where you got lost听一段讲解，标出没懂的地方
  2. Write one clarifying question for each spot为每处写一个澄清问题
  3. Actually ask one in class or in conversation在课堂或对话里实际问一次

⏱️ How much练多少 2 times a week每周 2 次

Why this evidence level?为什么是这个证据等级？

This is part of comprehension strategy work. The evidence comes mostly from reading-comprehension research and clinical consensus.

属于理解策略的组成部分，证据多来自阅读理解研究与临床共识。

🫶 The feelings side心理这一面

  • Language difficulty is often mistaken for shyness or not caring, and over time it can wear down self-esteem. Focus your practice on small wins like "I can explain one thing clearly".语言困难常被误读成「害羞」「不认真」，长期下来会影响自尊；把练习聚焦在「我能讲清楚一件事」的小成功上。
  • DLD often comes together with anxiety and social difficulty. When that happens, support for both feelings and language may be needed.DLD 与焦虑、社交困难同时出现的比例较高，必要时心理与语言两方面都需要支持。

Practising on your own is a real option. It is also completely normal to want someone trained to listen — you don't need any of the reasons below to ask; one stuck step is reason enough. It may be especially worth asking if:

自己练是一条真实可行的路；想找受过训练的人听一听，也完全正常——不需要符合下面任何一条才可以去问，卡在一步上就足够了。特别值得去问问的情况：

  • You often cannot keep up with explanations or instructions in class, even though your hearing is normal长期跟不上课堂讲解或指令，而听力正常
  • Organising what you say or write is very hard and affects marks in several subjects组织口头或书面表达非常吃力，影响多门课成绩
  • People often say your answers do not match the question常被说「答非所问」
  • When learning two languages, you are well behind your peers in both (being weaker in just one language does not count)学两种语言时两种都明显落后于同龄人（单一语言较弱不算）
