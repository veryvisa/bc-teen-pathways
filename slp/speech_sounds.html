🔤 Speech sounds: articulation & phonology🔤 语音与构音

The core核心

Every speech sound is a place × manner × voicing gesture; practise by first hearing the contrast, then producing it reliably in harder and harder contexts.每个音都是「在哪里、怎么、带不带声」三个动作的组合；练发音的关键是先听得出差别，再在越来越难的语境里稳定地做出来。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Articulation means the movement is not quite accurate. Phonology means the sound rules are being used the wrong way. The two are practised differently.构音（articulation）是动作做不准；音系（phonology）是声音规则用错，两者练法不同。
  2. The International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) sorts every sound by place, manner and voicing. It is your map for self-study.国际音标（IPA）把每个音按部位、方式、清浊分类，是自学的地图。
  3. A motor-learning principle: practice that is spread out, mixed up and gradually gets less feedback lasts longer than one big burst (Maas et al. 2008).运动学习原则：练得分散、随机、逐步减少反馈，比一次猛练效果更持久（Maas et al. 2008）。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

Doing mouth "muscle exercises" such as blowing and licking the lips, and expecting clearer speech. A Cochrane review found no evidence that they improve speech (L. & Gibbon 2015).做吹气、舔嘴唇等「口腔肌肉操」以为能改善发音——Cochrane 综述没有找到它们改善言语的证据（L. & Gibbon 2015）。

🗺️ The map of this area这个领域的图谱

The speech sound system is one of the core areas of speech-language pathology. A common thing in the teen years is a leftover error on a single sound (such as English /r/, /s/ or /θ/). Understanding phonetics also helps when you learn a second language.

言语声音系统是 SLP 的核心领域之一。青少年常见的是个别音（如英语 /r/、/s/、/θ/）的残留错误；理解语音学还能帮助学第二语言。

💡 Why it works — the core ideas为什么这样练——核心原理

Three questions pin down a consonant三个问题定位一个辅音

Where is the airflow blocked (both lips, behind the top teeth, the soft palate...)? How is it blocked (fully stopped, a narrow gap for friction, or out through the nose)? Do the vocal folds vibrate? For example, /s/ and /z/ differ only in the last one.

气流在哪里被挡（双唇、齿龈、软腭…）、怎么挡（完全堵住、留缝摩擦、从鼻子出）、声带振不振动。比如 /s/ 和 /z/ 只差最后一项。

📎 Sources来源: Crash Course Linguistics #8 Phonetics – Consonants

Why minimal pairs work最小对立为什么有效

"Sip" and "ship" differ by one sound, and the meaning changes. When you put your own tricky sound into pairs like these, your brain is forced to notice the difference. Listen first, then speak.

「sip/ship」只差一个音，意思就变了。把自己发错的音放进这样的词对里，大脑会被迫注意那个区别——先听辨，再发音。

📎 Sources来源: Baker E, McLeod S (2011). Evidence-based practice for children with speech sound disorders: Part 1 narrative review. Language, Speech, and Hearing Services in Schools 42(2).

How you schedule practice matters more than how much you do练习的安排比练习量更重要

Short sessions many times, words mixed in random order, and less immediate feedback over time all make the movement last longer. That is the idea behind the web practice with random questions and spaced review.

短时间多次、把不同词打乱练、逐步减少立即反馈，学到的动作更持久。这就是网页练习「随机出题、间隔复习」的原理。

📎 Sources来源: Maas E, Robin DA, Austermann Hula SN, et al. (2008). Principles of motor learning in treatment of motor speech disorders. American Journal of Speech-Language Pathology 17(3).

Feedback you can see看得见的反馈

For sounds like /r/, where it is hard to feel where the tongue is, visual biofeedback from a spectrogram or ultrasound has worked in research, but it mostly needs equipment and a professional. You can use Praat to look at a spectrogram as a rough guide.

对 /r/ 这类难以感觉到舌头位置的音，看声谱或超声的「视觉生物反馈」在研究里有效，但多数需要设备和专业人员。普通人可用 Praat 看声谱作粗略参考。

📎 Sources来源: McAllister Byun T, Hitchcock ER (2012). Investigating the use of traditional and spectral biofeedback approaches to intervention for /r/ misarticulation. American Journal of Speech-Language Pathology 21(3). · Sugden E, Lloyd S, Lam J, Cleland J (2019). Systematic review of ultrasound visual biofeedback in intervention for speech sound disorders. International Journal of Language & Communication Disorders 54(5).

A good first session: pick one method, try only its first step once or twice, and stop. That is a complete session. Feeling nervous while practising is expected — it is not a sign that it isn't working.好的第一次：选一种方法，只把第一步试一两次，然后停下。这就是完整的一次练习。练的时候紧张是正常的——不说明它没用。

Minimal-pair practice (perception → production)最小对立练习（先听后说）

Evidence证据 C · Expert consensus or uncontrolled studies专家共识或无对照研究Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Pick a set of words that differ only in your target sound (for example sip/ship, right/light). First listen to a recording and decide which word it is. Then say it yourself and record it, and let the page play your recordings back in random order to quiz you.

选一组只差你目标音的词（例如 sip/ship、right/light），先听录音判断是哪一个，再自己说并录下来，让网页随机播放你的录音来考你自己。

  1. Choose 10 pairs of words挑 10 对词
  2. Listening quiz: 20 questions, and move on only at 90% correct or more听辨测验：听 20 题，正确率 ≥90% 再往下
  3. Speaking: say each pair 3 times and record说：每对说 3 遍并录音
  4. Play back and self-rate: right / not sure / wrong回放自评：对/不确定/错
  5. Put the correct words into short sentences把正确的词放进短句

⏱️ How much练多少 10 minutes a day, split into 2 sessions每天 10 分钟，分 2 次

⚠️ If you cannot get above 80% in the listening quiz for two weeks in a row, it may be linked to hearing, so get your hearing checked first.如果连续两周听辨都过不了 80%，可能和听力有关，先查听力。
Why this evidence level?为什么是这个证据等级？

Systematic and narrative reviews show minimal pairs are among the best-supported phonology approaches (the research is mostly with children). The self-study version for teens has not been studied directly.

系统与叙述综述显示最小对立法是证据最多的音系干预之一（研究对象以儿童为主）。青少年自学版本未被直接研究。

Two separate checks graded this differently (A and C). We show the more cautious one, C.

两次独立判级结果不同（A 与 C），这里显示更保守的 C。

📎 Sources来源: Baker E, McLeod S (2011). Evidence-based practice for children with speech sound disorders: Part 1 narrative review. Language, Speech, and Hearing Services in Schools 42(2). · Weiner F (1981). Treatment of phonological disability using the method of meaningful minimal contrast: two case studies. Journal of Speech and Hearing Disorders 46(1).

Evidence comes from studies led by professionals. Very little research has tested learning this on your own.这条证据来自专业人员带领的研究；自己在家练的效果，研究很少。

Traditional articulation approach with a complexity hierarchy传统构音法：从单音到对话的阶梯

Evidence证据 C · Expert consensus or uncontrolled studies专家共识或无对照研究Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Use a mirror and articulation animations (Seeing Speech / eNunciate) to find the right position. Make the sound correctly on its own, then move through syllables, the start, middle and end of words, phrases, sentences and conversation.

用镜子、发音动画（Seeing Speech/eNunciate）找到正确位置，单独发准这个音，再进音节、词首/词中/词尾、短语、句子、对话。

  1. Watch the animation and copy it in the mirror看动画并对镜模仿
  2. Do the single sound 10 times all correct before moving up单音 10 次全对再进下一级
  3. Use 20 random words at each level每级用 20 个随机词
  4. Record a conversation sample once a week每周录一次对话样本

⏱️ How much练多少 10 minutes a day, 2–7 days per level每天 10 分钟，每级 2–7 天

Why this evidence level?为什么是这个证据等级？

It is the most commonly used clinical method. Overall evidence is moderate and mostly from studies with children. It works better when combined with motor-learning principles.

临床最常用的方法，整体证据中等、多为儿童研究；与运动学习原则结合时效果更好。

📎 Sources来源: Baker E, McLeod S (2011). Evidence-based practice for children with speech sound disorders: Part 1 narrative review. Language, Speech, and Hearing Services in Schools 42(2). · Maas E, Robin DA, Austermann Hula SN, et al. (2008). Principles of motor learning in treatment of motor speech disorders. American Journal of Speech-Language Pathology 17(3). · Seeing Speech (University of Glasgow)

Evidence comes from studies led by professionals. Very little research has tested learning this on your own.这条证据来自专业人员带领的研究；自己在家练的效果，研究很少。

Self-monitoring with recordings & spectrogram录音自评与声谱参考

Evidence证据 C · Expert consensus or uncontrolled studies专家共识或无对照研究Practise with care — read the note可以自己练，但先看提醒

Listen to your own recording together with a model. If you like, use Praat to look at how two sounds differ on a spectrogram (for example, /s/ has higher-frequency noise than /ʃ/).

把自己的录音和示范放在一起听；进阶的可以用 Praat 看两个音的声谱差别（例如 /s/ 的高频噪声比 /ʃ/ 更高）。

  1. Record 10 target words录 10 个目标词
  2. Compare with model A/B与示范 A/B 对比
  3. Open the spectrogram in Praat (optional)用 Praat 打开看声谱（可选）
  4. Write down your self-rated accuracy记下自评准确率

⏱️ How much练多少 2–3 times a week每周 2–3 次

⚠️ A spectrogram is only a rough guide. The same sound looks different for each person, so don't try to match the model exactly.声谱只是参考，同一个音每个人的声谱会不同，不要追求和示范「一模一样」。
Why this evidence level?为什么是这个证据等级？

Visual biofeedback (spectrogram or ultrasound) is supported by controlled studies and systematic reviews for leftover /r/ errors. The self-study version with free software has not been studied.

视觉生物反馈（声谱/超声）对 /r/ 残留错误有对照研究与系统综述支持；用免费软件的自学版本未被研究过。

两次独立判级结果不同（B 与 C），这里显示更保守的 C。

A minimal-pair listening quiz. It plays a model sound (or your own recorded word) in random order as a two-choice question with instant feedback, and wrong answers go into a spaced-review queue.

🌱 When it's worth asking a professional什么时候值得找专业人士

Practising on your own is a real option. It is also completely normal to want someone trained to listen — you don't need any of the reasons below to ask; one stuck step is reason enough. It may be especially worth asking if:

自己练是一条真实可行的路；想找受过训练的人听一听，也完全正常——不需要符合下面任何一条才可以去问，卡在一步上就足够了。特别值得去问问的情况：

  • People often can't understand what you say别人经常听不懂你说的话
  • After 4–6 weeks of self-study the target sound has hardly changed自学 4–6 周后目标音仍几乎没有变化
  • It comes with hearing loss or ear problems伴随听力下降、耳朵问题
  • You have pain when speaking, or your tongue or lips cannot move freely说话时有疼痛、舌头或嘴唇活动受限
  • Your speech makes you avoid speaking in class or joining social events发音问题让你回避上课发言或社交
