The core核心

Anxiety itself is not dangerous; avoidance and safety behaviours keep it going. Small graded steps, checked against your predictions, teach the brain that it is safer than it feels.紧张本身不危险；让焦虑越变越大的是回避和「安全行为」。一小步一小步地去做、做完对照预测与结果，大脑会重新学会「这没那么可怕」。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Anxiety keeps itself going in a loop: you predict disaster, you avoid or use safety behaviours, you never get the chance to find out the prediction was wrong, and you fear it even more.焦虑的维持循环：预测灾难→回避或用安全行为→没机会发现预测是错的→更怕。
  2. The point of exposure is not to wait until you feel calm. It is to test your prediction and create surprises (inhibitory learning).暴露的关键不是「忍到不紧张」，而是检验预测、制造意外（抑制学习）。
  3. ACT: you do not have to get rid of nervousness before you act. You can do what matters to you while feeling nervous.ACT：不必先消除紧张才行动；带着紧张去做对自己重要的事。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

Thinking the coping habits are the problem — or that you have to drop them all at once. Memorising every word, avoiding eye contact, talking fast or leaving early are very common ways of coping. They make you feel a little better but keep the nerves going (McManus et al. 2008). The practice is to drop one small one at a time and notice what happens. (Short cue cards with key points are fine — it's word-for-word scripts that keep the fear going.)把这些应对习惯当成「错」，或者以为要一下子全部放下。背下每个字、不看人、说得飞快、提前离开，都是很常见的应对方式；它们让人好受一点，却让紧张维持下去（McManus et al. 2008）。练习是一次放下一个小的，看看会发生什么。（写要点的小提示卡没问题——让恐惧维持的是逐字背稿。）

💡 Why it works — the core ideas为什么这样练——核心原理

Why avoiding makes fear grow为什么越躲越怕

Each time you avoid something, the nervousness drops right away, and your brain records avoiding as the thing that saved you. But it never gets a chance to learn that things would have been fine without it.

每次躲开，紧张马上下降，大脑把「躲」记成救命的办法；但它从来没机会发现「其实不躲也没事」。

📎 Sources来源: Craske MG, Treanor M, Conway CC, Zbozinek T, Vervliet B (2014). Maximizing exposure therapy: an inhibitory learning approach. Behaviour Research and Therapy 58.

The safety-behaviour trap安全行为的陷阱

In studies, people with social anxiety who deliberately used safety behaviours in a conversation (looking away, having what to say prepared) felt more anxious and made a worse impression on others. Dropping those behaviours did the opposite.

研究里让社交焦虑的人在对话中刻意用安全行为（避开目光、提前准备好要说的话），焦虑和别人对他们的印象都更差；放下安全行为则相反。

📎 Sources来源: McManus F, Sacadura C, Clark DM (2008). Why social anxiety persists: an experimental investigation of the role of safety behaviours as a maintaining factor. Journal of Behavior Therapy and Experimental Psychiatry 39(2).

Teens can get better at public speaking anxiety青少年的公开说话焦虑能练好

Experiments with teens who are anxious about public speaking show that even one structured exposure session can lower anxiety — a start, not a finish. A meta-analysis shows that psychological approaches work well overall for fear of public speaking.

针对青少年公开说话焦虑的实验显示，一次结构化的暴露练习就能降低焦虑——这是开始，不是结束。元分析显示心理干预对公开说话恐惧整体有效。

📎 Sources来源: Plaisted H, Waite P, Creswell C (2022). Optimising exposure for adolescents with public speaking anxiety: affect labelling or positive coping statements? Behaviour Research and Therapy 148. · Ebrahimi OV, Pallesen S, Kenter RMF, Nordgreen T (2019). Psychological interventions for the fear of public speaking: a meta-analysis. Frontiers in Psychology 10.

Both CBT and ACT have evidenceCBT 与 ACT 都有证据

A Cochrane review supports CBT for anxiety in children and teens, and a meta-analysis suggests ACT can help with anxiety and related difficulties. The same ideas can be used for anxiety about speaking.

Cochrane 综述支持 CBT 用于儿童青少年焦虑；ACT 的元分析显示它对焦虑及相关困扰有帮助。说话相关的焦虑可以用同样的原理。

📎 Sources来源: James AC, Reardon T, Soler A, James G, Creswell C (2020). Cognitive behavioural therapy for anxiety disorders in children and adolescents. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. · A-Tjak JGL, Davis ML, Morina N, et al. (2015). A meta-analysis of the efficacy of acceptance and commitment therapy for clinically relevant mental and physical health problems. Psychotherapy and Psychosomatics 84(1).

Nervous can also mean excited紧张也可以是兴奋

In one set of experiments, people who told themselves "I am excited" before a performance, rather than "I need to calm down", did a little better and felt better. A racing heart can mean either nervous or excited.

在一组实验里，上台前对自己说「我很兴奋」而不是「我要冷静」的人，表现略好、感受也更好——心跳加速本来就既可以是紧张也可以是兴奋。

📎 Sources来源: Brooks AW (2014). Get excited: reappraising pre-performance anxiety as excitement. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General 143(3).

Graded exposure with a fear ladder恐惧阶梯与逐级暴露

Evidence证据 A · Systematic reviews support it有系统综述支持Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Write 8–10 speaking situations from easy to hard (talk to a mirror, record a video, talk to family, ask a question in class, give a group presentation). Start in the low-to-middle range and repeat each step until your prediction turns out wrong, then move up.

写 8–10 个从容易到困难的说话场景（对镜子说→录视频→对家人→课堂提问→小组报告），从中低档开始，每一级重复到预测被推翻再往上。

  1. Write the ladder and give each step a predicted nervousness score写阶梯并给每级预测紧张分
  2. Pick one at 3–4 to start with选一个 3–4 分的开始
  3. Before you do it, write down what you predict will happen做之前写下预测会发生什么
  4. Afterwards, write down what actually happened做完写实际发生了什么
  5. Do each step 2–3 times before moving up同一级做 2–3 次再升级

⏱️ How much练多少 3–4 times a week每周 3–4 次

⚠️ Do not start with the hardest step. You can step back to an easier one at any time.不要一开始就挑最难的；任何一级都可以退回。
Why this evidence level?为什么是这个证据等级？

Exposure is the core ingredient of CBT for anxiety, supported by systematic reviews and meta-analyses. Experiments on teens with public speaking anxiety support the effect of one structured exposure session. One small randomized trial with teens compared exposure alone with exposure plus anxiety-management skills.

暴露是 CBT 治疗焦虑的核心成分，有系统综述与元分析支持；青少年公开说话焦虑的实验研究支持一次结构化暴露的效果；一项青少年随机微试验比较了「只做暴露」与「暴露＋焦虑管理技巧」。

Behavioural experiments (prediction testing)行为实验：检验预测

Evidence证据 B · At least one controlled trial至少一项对照试验支持Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Turn a worry into a prediction you can test ("If I pause for 3 seconds, everyone will laugh"), design a small experiment to try it, and compare afterwards.

把一个担心写成可检验的预测（「我停顿 3 秒大家会笑」），设计一次实验去试，事后对照。

  1. Write the prediction and how much you believe it, 0–100%写预测与相信程度 0–100%
  2. Design the experiment设计实验
  3. Do it执行
  4. Record what happened and how much you believe the prediction now记录结果与新的相信程度

⏱️ How much练多少 1–2 times a week每周 1–2 次

Why this evidence level?为什么是这个证据等级？

Cognitive restructuring and behavioural experiments are core parts of CBT, with evidence in CBT reviews. For speaking anxiety they are often used together with exposure.

认知重构与行为实验是 CBT 的核心组成，证据见 CBT 综述；在说话焦虑中常与暴露合用。

📎 Sources来源: James AC, Reardon T, Soler A, James G, Creswell C (2020). Cognitive behavioural therapy for anxiety disorders in children and adolescents. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. · Menzies RG, O'Brian S, Onslow M, et al. (2008). An experimental clinical trial of a cognitive-behavior therapy package for chronic stuttering. Dropping safety behaviours放下安全行为

Evidence证据 C · Expert consensus or uncontrolled studies专家共识或无对照研究Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

List your safety behaviours when you present or speak up (memorizing the whole script, looking down, talking fast, always holding something). In each practice, drop just one and see what happens.

列出自己上台或发言时的安全行为（背全稿、低头、说很快、手里一直拿东西），每次练习只放下其中一个，观察结果。

  1. Make the list列清单
  2. Drop one behaviour each time每次放下一个
  3. Record your anxiety and how it went记录焦虑与效果

ACT: defusion and values-based actionACT：解离与按价值行动

Evidence证据 B · At least one controlled trial至少一项对照试验支持Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Change "I'm going to mess up" into "I notice I'm having the thought that I'll mess up." Write down why you want to speak (to share something, to make friends), then do it while feeling nervous.

把「我会搞砸」改成「我注意到我有一个『我会搞砸』的念头」；写下自己为什么想发言（想分享、想交朋友），带着紧张去做那件事。

  1. Write 3 values (what you care about)写 3 个价值（我在乎什么）
  2. Pair each value with one small speaking action每个价值配一个说话小行动
  3. Practise the defusion sentence练解离句
  4. Afterwards, note "I did something that fits my values"做完记录「我做到了与价值一致的事」

⏱️ How much练多少 5 minutes a day每天 5 分钟

ACT 元分析支持其有效；口吃成人 ACT 研究显示心理社会结果改善。

Arousal reappraisal重新解读紧张

Evidence证据 B · At least one controlled trial至少一项对照试验支持Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

When your heart is racing before you speak, tell yourself "I'm excited" or "My body is helping me get ready" instead of forcing yourself to calm down.

上台前心跳快时，对自己说「我很兴奋」或「身体在帮我准备」，而不是强迫自己冷静。

  1. Say it once in the minute before you start开场前 1 分钟说一次
  2. Afterwards, note how it felt事后记录感受

Slow breathing for physical arousal (adjunct)慢呼吸（辅助）

Evidence证据 D · Tradition or theory, little direct evidence临床传统或理论，缺直接证据Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

🌱 When it's worth asking a professional什么时候值得找专业人士

Practising on your own is a real option. It is also completely normal to want someone trained to listen — you don't need any of the reasons below to ask; one stuck step is reason enough. It may be especially worth asking if:

自己练是一条真实可行的路；想找受过训练的人听一听，也完全正常——不需要符合下面任何一条才可以去问，卡在一步上就足够了。特别值得去问问的情况：

  • You skip school, refuse exams or avoid activities because you are afraid of speaking因为害怕说话而逃学、拒绝参加考试或活动
  • Panic attacks (racing heart, trouble breathing, feeling out of control) keep happening恐慌发作（心慌、喘不上气、觉得要失控）反复出现
  • You feel low, sleep badly or cannot eat for more than two weeks持续两周以上情绪低落、睡不好、吃不下

🔤 Glossary术语表 · Glossary

Safety behaviour安全行为
Something done to feel safer (scripts, avoiding eye contact) that keeps the fear going.为了感觉安全而做的事（背稿、不看人），却让害怕维持下去。
Exposure暴露
Stepping into a feared situation on purpose, in small steps, to learn it is manageable.有意、小步地进入害怕的情境，学到它其实应付得来。
Behavioural experiment行为实验
Testing a prediction (“they'll laugh”) by trying it and recording what really happens.通过真的去做并记录结果，来检验一个预测（「他们会笑我」）。
Defusion解离
Noticing a thought as a thought (“I'm having the thought that…”) instead of a fact.把想法当成想法来看（「我注意到我有一个想法……」），而不是当成事实。
Reappraisal重评
Reading a racing heart as excitement or readiness rather than danger.把心跳加速理解为兴奋或准备好了，而不是危险。
