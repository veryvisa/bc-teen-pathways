The core核心

An accent is a trace of knowing more than one language, not an error; if you choose to work on it, aim for being easy to understand, not accent-free.口音是你会说不止一种语言的痕迹，不是错误；如果想调整，目标是「别人容易听懂」，而不是「听不出口音」。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. How strong an accent sounds, how well people understand you, and how tiring you are to listen to are three different things (Munro & Derwing 1995).口音浓不浓、别人听不听得懂、别人听得累不累是三个不同的维度（Munro & Derwing 1995）。
  2. Stress, rhythm and intonation (the bigger patterns of speech) often matter more for being understood than individual vowels and consonants.重音、节奏、语调（超音段）对「好懂」的影响常常大于个别元音辅音。
  3. Learning two languages does not cause a language disorder. It is normal for a bilingual person to be temporarily weaker in one language, or to mix the two.学两种语言不会造成语言障碍；双语者暂时某一语言较弱、混用两种语言都是正常现象。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

Spending a lot of time trying to "get rid of the accent" on small sounds that make little difference, while ignoring stress and pausing.为了「消除口音」花大量时间在影响不大的细节音上，忽略了重音和停顿。

🗺️ The map of this area这个领域的图谱

This is a newer domain. Many teens in BC speak more than one language, and an accent or learning two languages is often mistaken for a speech or language problem. ASHA is clear that an accent is not a disorder. At the same time, research on accent change by Derwing and Munro (Canadian researchers) gives evidence-based ways to practise being easy to understand, and you can do these on your own.

新增领域，理由：BC 有大量多语青少年，「口音」和「学两种语言」常被误当成言语语言问题。ASHA 明确口音不是障碍；同时口音调整的研究（Derwing & Munro，加拿大学者）提供了以「好懂」为目标的循证练法，可以自学。

💡 Why it works — the core ideas为什么这样练——核心原理

An accent can be fully understandable有口音也可以完全好懂

Canadian researchers found that speech that listeners rated as strongly accented was often still understood 100%. How strong an accent sounds and how easy it is to understand are only partly related.

加拿大学者的研究发现，听者觉得口音很重的话，常常仍然百分之百被听懂；口音度和可懂度只有部分相关。

📎 Sources来源: Munro MJ, Derwing TM (1995). Foreign accent, comprehensibility, and intelligibility in the speech of second language learners. Language Learning 45(1). · Derwing TM, Munro MJ (2005). Second language accent and pronunciation teaching: a research-based approach. TESOL Quarterly 39(3).

Pronunciation teaching helps, if you pick the right target发音教学有用，但要选对目标

Reviews show that focused pronunciation teaching improves how easily you are understood, especially for the bigger patterns like stress and rhythm.

综述显示有针对性的发音教学能提高可懂度，尤其是重音、节奏等超音段。

📎 Sources来源: Thomson RI, Derwing TM (2015). The effectiveness of L2 pronunciation instruction: a narrative review. Applied Linguistics 36(3). · Derwing TM, Munro MJ (2005). Second language accent and pronunciation teaching: a research-based approach. TESOL Quarterly 39(3).

What ASHA saysASHA 的立场

An accent is not a speech or language disorder. Accent modification is a service people choose for themselves.

口音不是言语或语言障碍；口音调整是个人选择的服务。

📎 Sources来源: ASHA Practice Portal: Accent Modification · ASHA: Learning Two Languages

Stress, rhythm and thought groups重音、节奏与意群

Evidence证据 B · At least one controlled trial至少一项对照试验支持Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Take a short passage and mark the stress in each word with several syllables and the key word in each thought group. Read along with a model, then record your own version and compare.

给一段课文标出每个多音节词的重音和每个意群的中心词，跟示范读，再录自己的版本对比。

  1. Underline the stress and the thought groups标重音与意群
  2. Read along with the model 3 times跟示范读 3 遍
  3. Record yourself and compare录音对比
  4. Read the same passage to a friend and ask which parts were hard to follow把同一段讲给朋友听，问哪里不好懂

⏱️ How much练多少 10 minutes a day每天 10 分钟

Why this evidence level?为什么是这个证据等级？

Narrative reviews and classroom studies support teaching stress, rhythm and intonation to help being understood.

叙述综述与课堂研究支持超音段教学对可懂度有益。

📎 Sources来源: Thomson RI, Derwing TM (2015). The effectiveness of L2 pronunciation instruction: a narrative review. Applied Linguistics 36(3). · Derwing TM, Munro MJ (2005). Second language accent and pronunciation teaching: a research-based approach. TESOL Quarterly 39(3).

Evidence comes from studies led by professionals. Very little research has tested learning this on your own.这条证据来自专业人员带领的研究；自己在家练的效果，研究很少。

Shadowing with recordings影子跟读

Evidence证据 C · Expert consensus or uncontrolled studies专家共识或无对照研究Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Play 30 seconds of clear English (for example, a Crash Course clip) and speak half a beat behind it, copying the rhythm and intonation. Record yourself and compare.

播放一段 30 秒的清晰英语（例如 Crash Course 片段），延迟半拍跟着说，模仿节奏和语调，录音对比。

  1. Choose a 30-second clip选 30 秒片段
  2. Listen to it twice先听 2 遍
  3. Shadow it 3 times跟读 3 遍
  4. Compare your recording with the original, A/B录音与原声 A/B 对比

⏱️ How much练多少 5–10 minutes a day每天 5–10 分钟

Why this evidence level?为什么是这个证据等级？

Widely used in second-language pronunciation teaching, with some research support, though the quality varies.

在第二语言发音教学中广泛使用，有一些研究支持，但质量不一。

📎 Sources来源: Thomson RI, Derwing TM (2015). The effectiveness of L2 pronunciation instruction: a narrative review. Applied Linguistics 36(3).

Evidence comes from studies led by professionals. Very little research has tested learning this on your own.这条证据来自专业人员带领的研究；自己在家练的效果，研究很少。

High-impact sound contrasts高影响的音段对立

Evidence证据 C · Expert consensus or uncontrolled studies专家共识或无对照研究Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Pick the sound contrasts that change meaning most (for example /l/–/r/, or /i/–/ɪ/ as in ship–sheep) and practise them with minimal pairs (see the speech sounds domain).

挑影响意思最多的对立（例如 /l/–/r/、/i/–/ɪ/ 如 ship–sheep），用最小对立练习（见语音与构音领域）。

  1. Pick 2 contrasts挑 2 组对立
  2. Take a listening test听辨测验
  3. Say the words and record them发音并录音

Why this evidence level?为什么是这个证据等级？

This uses the same minimal-pair idea as phonology work. Second-language research supports choosing sounds based on how much they affect being understood.

与音系最小对立原理相同；第二语言研究支持以可懂度为目标选择音段。

🚫 Not recommended — and why不推荐的做法——以及原因

  • Aiming for "no accent at all"以「完全听不出口音」为目标 — Research shows this is rarely reached after the teenage years and is not needed to be understood. It tends to lead to frustration.研究显示这在青春期后很少达到，也不是好懂的前提；容易带来挫败感。

    📎 Sources来源: Derwing TM, Munro MJ (2005). Second language accent and pronunciation teaching: a research-based approach. TESOL Quarterly 39(3).

  • Stopping use of your home language because you worry about your accent因为担心口音而停用家里的语言 — Being bilingual does not cause a language disorder, and keeping your home language helps both identity and learning.双语不会造成语言障碍，保持家庭语言对身份认同和学习都有益。

    📎 Sources来源: ASHA: Learning Two Languages

🫶 The feelings side心理这一面

  • Being teased or imitated for your accent can make you speak up less. Setting your goal as "easy to understand" rather than "sounding like a native speaker" can clearly lower the pressure.因为口音被嘲笑或模仿的经历会让人少开口；把目标定在「好懂」而不是「像母语者」，能明显减轻压力。

📎 Sources来源: Derwing TM, Munro MJ (2005). Second language accent and pronunciation teaching: a research-based approach. TESOL Quarterly 39(3).
