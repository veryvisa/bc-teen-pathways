The core核心

Conversation is a joint project—taking turns, staying on topic, repairing misunderstandings; practise for mutual understanding, not scripts.对话是两个人合作完成的：轮流说、跟着话题走、发现误会时修补；练习的目标是让双方都更容易懂对方，而不是背台词。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Pragmatics has three parts: using language to reach a goal (asking, saying no, comforting), adjusting how you say things to the person and the place, and following conversation rules (taking turns, staying on topic, repairing).语用三件事：用语言达到目的（请求、拒绝、安慰）、按对象和场合调整说法、遵守对话规则（轮替、话题、修补）。
  2. The "double empathy problem": communication difficulty goes both ways. People with different communication styles often misread each other, while people with the same style often talk smoothly together (Milton 2012; Crompton et al. 2020).「双重共情问题」：沟通困难是双方的，不同沟通风格的人之间互相误读，同风格的人之间往往沟通顺畅（Milton 2012；Crompton et al. 2020）。
  3. Repair strategies (asking someone to say it again, rephrasing, checking you understood) are the most useful and most practisable skills.修补策略（请对方重说、换种说法、确认理解）是最实用、最可练的技能。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

Mistaking shyness, nerves or cultural difference for a "social communication disorder", or pushing down your own way of communicating just to "look normal", which gets very tiring over time.把内向、紧张或文化差异误认为「社交沟通障碍」；或者为了「看起来正常」硬压抑自己的沟通方式，长期会很累。

🗺️ The map of this area这个领域的图谱

Pragmatics (using language to get things done, adjusting what you say to the person, and following the rules of conversation) is its own area of speech-language pathology. Teen social life gets more complicated, and conversation skills can be learned and practised. This area takes a neurodiversity-respecting view: the goal is for people to understand each other, not for anyone to pretend to be someone else.

语用（用语言做事、按对象调整、守对话规则）是 SLP 的独立领域；青少年社交场景变复杂，对话技巧既能学也能练。本领域采用尊重神经多样性的立场：目标是互相理解，而不是让人「装得像别人」。

💡 Why it works — the core ideas为什么这样练——核心原理

Conversation has hidden rules对话有隐形规则

The philosopher Grice suggested four habits: say just enough, say what is true, say what is relevant, and say it clearly. Many misunderstandings happen because two people have different ideas of "just enough".

哲学家格莱斯提出：说得刚好够、说真话、说相关的、说清楚。很多误会来自双方对「刚好够」的理解不同。

📎 Sources来源: ASHA: Social Communication (public)

Repair is part of everyday conversation修补是对话的日常

Conversation-analysis research finds that in real talk, a repair like "What?" or "I mean ..." comes up every few minutes. Repair is not failure. It is part of how conversation normally works.

会话分析研究发现，真实对话里每隔几分钟就会出现一次「什么？」「我是说…」这样的修补——修补不是失败，而是对话正常运作的一部分。

📎 Sources来源: Stokoe E. Talk: The Science of Conversation (2018)

Understanding goes both ways双向的理解

Misreading between autistic and non-autistic people goes in both directions, and information passes between autistic people just as well as between non-autistic people. So practice covers both "how I express myself" and "how I explain what I need".

自闭者与非自闭者之间的误读是双向的；自闭者之间传递信息的效率并不比非自闭者之间差。所以练习既包括「我怎么表达」，也包括「我怎么说明自己的需要」。

📎 Sources来源: Milton DEM (2012). On the ontological status of autism: the 'double empathy problem'. Disability & Society 27(6). · Crompton CJ, Ropar D, Evans-Williams CV, Flynn EG, Fletcher-Watson S (2020). Autistic peer-to-peer information transfer is highly effective. Autism 24(7).

Turn-taking & question practice轮替与提问练习

Evidence证据 C · Expert consensus or uncontrolled studies专家共识或无对照研究Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Have a 5-minute practice conversation with someone you trust. Say 1–3 sentences each turn, then hand the turn over, and pick up the other person's topic with an open question (how, what, why).

和信任的人做 5 分钟对话练习：每轮说 1–3 句就把话轮交出去，用开放式问题（how/what/why）接住对方的话题。

  1. Prepare 3 open questions准备 3 个开放式问题
  2. Talk for 5 minutes and record it (with the other person's permission)5 分钟对话并录音（征得对方同意）
  3. Play it back: count who spoke more, whether anyone interrupted, and whether there were silences回放：数一数谁说得多、有没有打断、有没有冷场
  4. Change just one thing next time下一次只改一个地方

⏱️ How much练多少 2 times a week每周 2 次

⚠️ Always get the other person's permission before recording. You can also skip recording and just recall it afterwards.录音前一定征得对方同意；不录也可以，只事后回忆。

Repair & clarification strategies修补与澄清

Evidence证据 B · At least one controlled trial至少一项对照试验支持Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Practise three repairs: asking the other person to clarify ("Do you mean ...?"), fixing your own words ("Let me say that another way ..."), and checking understanding ("So what you mean is ..."). First write the lines on cards, then use each one once in a real conversation.

练三种修补：请对方澄清（「你是说…？」）、自我修补（「我换个说法…」）、确认理解（「所以你的意思是…」），先写句子卡，再在真实对话里各用一次。

  1. Copy out 6 repair cards抄 6 张修补句卡
  2. Say them in front of a mirror对镜说
  3. Use each kind once in a day在一天里每种用 1 次
  4. Note how the other person reacted记录对方反应

⏱️ How much练多少 1–3 times a day每天 1–3 次

Topic management话题管理

Evidence证据 C · Expert consensus or uncontrolled studies专家共识或无对照研究Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Practise changing topics with bridging lines like "Speaking of that, it reminds me ...". When the other person's answers get shorter, try handing the topic over to them.

练习用「说到这个，我想起…」这类过渡句转换话题；发现对方回答变短时，试着把话题交给对方。

  1. Watch an interview clip and mark where the topic changes看一段访谈视频，标出话题转换点
  2. Copy two bridging lines模仿两种过渡句
  3. Use one in a real conversation对话中实际用一次

Explaining your communication needs说明自己的沟通需要

Evidence证据 D · Tradition or theory, little direct evidence临床传统或理论，缺直接证据Fine to practise on your own可以自己练

Write a small card called "How I communicate best". For example: "I need a few seconds to think before I answer" or "Text messages are easier for me". Share it with a teacher or friend when it feels right.

写一张「我怎么沟通最舒服」的小卡：例如「我需要几秒钟想一下再回答」「文字消息对我更容易」，在合适时告诉老师或朋友。

  1. Write 3 of your own communication preferences写 3 条自己的沟通偏好
  2. Practise one short sentence to explain them练习一句简短的说明
  3. Choose one safe person and say it once选一个安全的对象说一次

Structured social-skills steps (PEERS-style, informational)结构化社交步骤（PEERS 式，作参考）

🚫 Not recommended — and why不推荐的做法——以及原因

  • Memorising "universal scripts" to get through every situation靠背「万能话术」应付所有场合 — A script cannot respond to what the other person really does, and it makes talk feel stiff. Practising general skills like turn-taking and repair works better.脚本无法应对对方的真实反应，也会让对话显得机械；更有效的是练轮替与修补这类通用技能。

    📎 Sources来源: Gerber S, Brice A, Capone N, Fujiki M, Timler G (2012). Language use in social interactions of school-age children with language impairments: an evidence-based systematic review of treatment. Language, Speech, and Hearing Services in Schools 43(2).

  • Training whose goal is to remove a communication style以「消除」某种沟通风格为目标的训练 — Aiming to suppress your natural way of communicating adds emotional strain. The current mainstream approach is mutual understanding and self-advocacy.以压抑自然沟通方式为目标会带来心理负担；当前主流取向是互相理解与自我倡导。

    📎 Sources来源: Milton DEM (2012). On the ontological status of autism: the 'double empathy problem'. Disability & Society 27(6).
