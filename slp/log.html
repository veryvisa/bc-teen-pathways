The core核心

Looking back over weeks — not days — is how you see progress.按周、而不是按天回头看，才看得到进步。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Compare recordings of the same text, a week or two apart.对比隔一两周录的同一段文字。
  2. Export a CSV or print a copy to keep.导出 CSV 或打印一份留存。
  3. Recordings live in this browser only; save the ones you want as files.录音只在这个浏览器里；想留的请另存为文件。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“A bad day means it isn't working.” Speech changes slowly and wobbles; the trend over weeks is what counts.「状态差的一天说明没用。」言语变化慢、会有起伏；看的是几周的走向。
