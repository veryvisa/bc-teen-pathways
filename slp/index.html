Speech & language言语与语言

The core核心

Speech is how we make the sounds; language is how we understand and organise meaning. There are nine areas, and you can start with any of them.言语管怎么发出声音，语言管怎么理解和组织意思；一共九个领域，你可以从任何一个开始。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. The map of nine areas shows how hearing, words, sounds, voice and feelings connect.九个领域的图谱，展示听、词句、语音、嗓音和感受怎么连在一起。
  2. “Where to start” turns what you notice in your own words into a place to begin reading.「从哪开始」把你自己的感受变成一个开始阅读的地方。
  3. Ten minutes a day, recorded and compared over weeks, is how you see change.每天 10 分钟，录下来、隔几周对比，就能看到变化。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“If I practise on my own I don't need anyone to check.” Self-study really helps — but it is not a diagnosis, and it is fine to ask a professional at any point.「自己练就不需要任何人看了。」自学确实有用——但它不是诊断，任何时候去问专业人士都没问题。

🧩 The nine areas九个领域

🌊

Fluency & stuttering流利度与口吃

Stuttering is a timing disruption in speech plus the tension and avoidance that grow around it; practice aims for easier speech and less avoidance, not perfect fluency.

口吃是说话的「时间与节奏」偶尔卡住，加上身体紧张和想躲开的心理；练习的目标是说得更轻松、躲得更少，而不是永远不卡。

8 methods种方法🔤

Speech sounds: articulation & phonology语音与构音

Every speech sound is a place × manner × voicing gesture; practise by first hearing the contrast, then producing it reliably in harder and harder contexts.

每个音都是「在哪里、怎么、带不带声」三个动作的组合；练发音的关键是先听得出差别，再在越来越难的语境里稳定地做出来。

3 methods种方法🎵

Voice & resonance嗓音与共鸣

Voice = breath × vocal-fold vibration × resonance; protect it by reducing collision force, train it by getting more sound for less effort.

声音＝呼吸供气 × 声带振动 × 口鼻共鸣；保护嗓子的核心是减少声带碰撞的力量，练嗓的核心是用更少的力气得到更响更亮的声音。

4 methods种方法💬

Spoken language: understanding & expression口语理解与表达

Language has five layers—sounds, word structure, sentences, meaning, use; clear expression rests on deep vocabulary and story structure, comprehension on building a mental model as you listen.

语言有五层：声音、词的构造、句子、意思、使用；表达清楚靠的是词汇深度与叙事结构，理解靠的是边听边建「心里的模型」。

5 methods种方法🤝

Social communication & pragmatics社交沟通与语用

Conversation is a joint project—taking turns, staying on topic, repairing misunderstandings; practise for mutual understanding, not scripts.

对话是两个人合作完成的：轮流说、跟着话题走、发现误会时修补；练习的目标是让双方都更容易懂对方，而不是背台词。

5 methods种方法📖

Language & literacy读写与语言

Reading comprehension ≈ decoding × language comprehension. When older students find reading hard, it is usually the second part — and that part can be trained with summarising, questioning, word parts and writing strategies.

阅读理解≈认字解码 × 语言理解。高年级学生觉得读不懂时，通常卡在后一项——而这一项可以靠总结、提问、词根和写作策略练出来。

4 methods种方法👂

Auditory processing & listening听觉加工与倾听

Hearing is the ear; understanding is the brain. In noisy, fast settings, environment changes, pre-teaching vocabulary and active-listening strategies help.

「听见」靠耳朵，「听懂」靠大脑；在嘈杂、快速的环境里听懂别人，可以通过改善环境、预习词汇和主动倾听策略来帮忙。

4 methods种方法🫶

Speaking anxiety & avoidance (cross-cutting)说话焦虑与回避（贯穿各领域）

Anxiety itself is not dangerous; avoidance and safety behaviours keep it going. Small graded steps, checked against your predictions, teach the brain that it is safer than it feels.

紧张本身不危险；让焦虑越变越大的是回避和「安全行为」。一小步一小步地去做、做完对照预测与结果，大脑会重新学会「这没那么可怕」。

6 methods种方法🌏

Multilingualism & accent (a difference, not a disorder)多语与口音（是差异，不是障碍）

An accent is a trace of knowing more than one language, not an error; if you choose to work on it, aim for being easy to understand, not accent-free.

口音是你会说不止一种语言的痕迹，不是错误；如果想调整，目标是「别人容易听懂」，而不是「听不出口音」。

3 methods种方法

🔬 How to read the evidence letters怎么看证据字母

  • A Systematic reviews support it有系统综述支持
  • B At least one controlled trial至少一项对照试验支持
  • C Expert consensus or uncontrolled studies专家共识或无对照研究
  • D Tradition or theory, little direct evidence临床传统或理论，缺直接证据

Each level comes from the method's own sources. Where the evidence could be read two ways, the page shows the more cautious level.

每个等级都来自该方法自己的研究来源。证据可以有两种读法时，页面显示更保守的那一级。

⚠️ The most important caveat最重要的一条提醒

Almost all of this evidence comes from studies where a trained professional guided the practice, often with children or adults rather than teenagers. Whether the same methods work as well when a teenager practises alone at home has barely been studied. So “evidence A” means the method works in research — not that the self-study version is proven.

这里几乎所有证据都来自有专业人员指导的研究，样本多是儿童或成人，而不是青少年。青少年在家自己练是否同样有效，几乎没有被研究过。所以「证据 A」的意思是这个方法在研究里有效——不是说自学版本已被证明。

Areas this self-study site leaves out — and why本站自学部分不包括的领域——以及原因

  • Swallowing吞咽 — A real part of SLP work, but it carries medical risks (food or drink going into the airway), so it is not for self-practice.这是 SLP 工作的重要部分，但有误吸等医疗风险，不适合自己练。
  • Communication after a stroke or brain injury中风或脑损伤后的沟通障碍 — Mostly affects adults and needs a medical team — covered as a career topic.多见于成人，需要医疗团队——在职业路线里介绍。
  • Hearing loss and hearing aids听力损失与助听 — This is audiology. The Listening page points you to a hearing check first.这属于听力学。「听与倾听」页会先请你做听力检查。
  • Augmentative and alternative communication (AAC)辅助与替代沟通（AAC） — Very important for people who need it, but choosing and setting it up needs a professional assessment.对需要的人非常重要，但选择和配置需要专业评估。

In university, the areas on this site map onto courses in phonetics, language development, speech science, fluency, voice and audiology.

在大学里，本站这些领域对应语音学、语言发展、言语科学、流利度、嗓音和听力学等课程。

🤖 Good questions to ask an AI向 AI 提的好问题

Explain the difference between speech and language in plain words, with two everyday examples of each.
用通俗的话解释言语和语言的区别，各举两个日常例子。
I want to practise one speech or language skill for 10 minutes a day for 4 weeks. Help me make a simple plan with a way to record progress — ask me which skill first.
我想每天花 10 分钟、连续 4 周练一项言语或语言技能。帮我做一个简单计划，包括记录进步的方法——先问我想练哪一项。
What is the difference between “this method has evidence” and “the self-study version has evidence”? Use an example.
「这个方法有证据」和「自学版本有证据」有什么区别？举个例子。
How to check the answer怎么核对 AI 的回答 Check any claim against ASHA, NIDCD or the area pages here. If an AI names a study, look up its DOI — AI tools sometimes invent references or mix up authors. Never paste personal or health details into an AI chat.把任何说法对照 ASHA、NIDCD 或本站各领域页核一核。AI 提到的研究要查 DOI——AI 有时会编造文献或张冠李戴。不要把个人信息或健康信息粘贴进 AI 对话。
🎧 Audio is on the way音频制作中 For each area: a deep-dive episode, short practise-with-me guides, and a slow lying-down review. They will play here, with speed control and a transcript.每个领域：一集深讲、几集「跟我练」短集、一集慢节奏的躺着听复习。做好后在这里播放，可调速、有逐字稿。
