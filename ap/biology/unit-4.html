4.1 Cell Communication细胞通讯1 Concept Explanation1 概念解释

A fictional hormone is released into the blood and reaches every tissue, but only liver cells change their activity. Which statement best explains this?

一种虚构激素释放入血并到达所有组织，但只有肝细胞改变活动。哪项最能解释？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Cells signal in three ranges: direct contact, local signals such as paracrine and synaptic signals, and long-distance hormones. Every cell may be exposed to a signal, but only cells with the matching receptor respond.

细胞的信号有三种范围：直接接触、局部信号（如旁分泌和突触信号）和远距离激素。信号可能到达所有细胞，但只有带匹配受体的细胞才会反应。

4.2 Introduction to Signal Transduction信号转导导论1 Concept Explanation1 概念解释

A receptor activates a G protein, which activates adenylyl cyclase. The enzyme converts ATP to cAMP. Which molecule is the second messenger in this pathway?

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Signal transduction has three stages: reception (ligand binds receptor), transduction (relay inside the cell) and response (a change in cell activity). The ligand usually stays outside; the receptor changes shape and passes the message on.

信号转导分三个阶段：接收（配体结合受体）、转导（细胞内的传递）和反应（细胞活动的改变）。配体通常留在细胞外；受体改变形状并传递信息。

Claim论点

cAMP is the second messenger.

cAMP是第二信使。

Evidence证据

cAMP is made inside the cell after receptor activation.

cAMP在受体激活后于细胞内产生。

Reasoning推理

Second messengers are small molecules made or released inside the cell that spread the signal.

第二信使是在细胞内产生或释放、传播信号的小分子。

4.3 Signal Transduction Pathways信号转导通路6 Argumentation6 论证

A student claims that a phosphorylation cascade amplifies a signal. Which evidence best supports the claim?

一位学生声称磷酸化级联会放大信号。哪项证据最能支持该说法？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

In many pathways a receptor activates a G protein, then an enzyme makes a second messenger such as cAMP, which activates kinases that phosphorylate other proteins in a cascade. Each step multiplies the signal (amplification), and phosphatases and enzymes that break down cAMP switch it off.

在许多通路中，受体激活G蛋白，随后酶产生cAMP等第二信使，再激活使其他蛋白磷酸化的激酶，形成级联。每一步都使信号倍增（放大），磷酸酶和分解cAMP的酶使其关闭。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

The numbers rise at each step.

每一步的分子数量都增加。

Evidence证据

A few receptors lead to a very large number of products.

少数受体产生极大量产物。

Reasoning推理

Each enzyme in a cascade activates many copies of the next, multiplying the signal.

级联中每个酶激活许多下游分子，使信号成倍放大。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Describes where the pathway begins, not how many molecules it affects.描述的是通路的起点，而不是影响了多少分子。
B. Evidence for reversibility, not amplification.这是可逆性的证据，不是放大的证据。
C. Evidence for receptor and pathway differences, not for amplification.这是受体和通路差异的证据，不是放大的证据。

4.4 Feedback反馈6 Argumentation6 论证

In a fictional mammal, warming above the set point triggers nerves to sweat glands, which cool the body. A nerve injury blocks the signal to the sweat glands. Predict body temperature on a hot day.

在一种虚构哺乳动物中，体温高于设定点会触发通向汗腺的神经使身体降温。神经损伤阻断了通向汗腺的信号。预测炎热天气下的体温。

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Negative feedback reduces the original change and keeps a variable near a set point, as insulin does for blood glucose. Positive feedback increases the change until something ends it, as oxytocin does in childbirth.

负反馈减弱最初的变化，使变量保持在设定点附近，如胰岛素调节血糖。正反馈使变化不断加强直到某事件终止，如分娩时的催产素。

Claim论点

Without the effector, the temperature is not corrected.

没有效应器，温度得不到纠正。

Evidence证据

The nerve to the sweat glands is blocked.

通向汗腺的神经被阻断。

Reasoning推理

Negative feedback needs every link to return the variable to the set point.

负反馈需要每个环节都在才能使变量回到设定点。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
B. Reverses the effect of losing the response.把失去反应的影响弄反了。
C. Assumes a loop works even if a link is broken.以为反馈环即使有环节断了也能工作。

In an asynchronous, steadily growing culture with a 20-hour cell cycle, a 400-cell sample has 28 cells in mitosis and the rest in interphase. About how long does mitosis last?

在非同步、稳定增长、周期为20小时的培养物中，取400个细胞，28个处于有丝分裂，其余在间期。有丝分裂大约持续多久？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

The cell cycle has interphase (G1, S, G2) and the mitotic phase (prophase, metaphase, anaphase, telophase, then cytokinesis). DNA is copied in S phase, and sister chromatids separate in anaphase.

细胞周期包括间期（G1、S、G2）和分裂期（前期、中期、后期、末期，然后是胞质分裂）。DNA在S期复制，姐妹染色单体在后期分离。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

28/400 × 20 h = 1.4 h.

28/400 × 20小时 = 1.4小时。

Evidence证据

7% of the cells were in mitosis.

7%的细胞处于有丝分裂。

Reasoning推理

In an asynchronous culture the fraction of cells in a stage estimates the fraction of the cycle spent there.

在非同步培养物中，处于某阶段的细胞比例可估计周期中花在该阶段的时间比例。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Divides the cycle length by 28 instead of using the fraction of cells.用周期长度除以28，而没有用细胞比例。
C. Uses 28 divided by 400 times 200, an arithmetic slip.算成28除以400再乘200，是计算失误。
D. Calculates the time spent in interphase instead of mitosis.算出的是间期时间，而不是有丝分裂时间。

Loss of p53 removes a brake on the cycle.

失去p53相当于去掉周期的刹车。

Evidence证据

Normal p53 halts the cycle or triggers apoptosis after DNA damage.

正常p53在DNA损伤后使周期停止或触发凋亡。

Reasoning推理

Without a working checkpoint, damaged cells keep dividing and pass on mutations, which raises cancer risk.

检查点失效时，受损细胞继续分裂并传递突变，增加癌症风险。

The core核心

Cells receive a signal, relay and amplify it through a pathway, and respond; the same logic of signals and feedback decides when a cell divides.细胞接收信号，经通路传递并放大后作出反应；同样的信号与反馈逻辑决定细胞何时分裂。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Reception, transduction, response: a ligand binds a receptor, a relay (often kinases and second messengers such as cAMP) amplifies it, and the cell changes what it does.接收、转导、反应：配体结合受体，通过中继（常为激酶和cAMP等第二信使）放大，细胞随之改变行为。
  2. Negative feedback brings a variable back toward a set point; positive feedback pushes it further in the same direction until something ends it.负反馈使变量回到设定点；正反馈让变量朝同一方向越变越快，直到某事件使其结束。
  3. Cyclin-CDK complexes and checkpoints decide whether a cell moves through the cycle; broken control, for example a faulty tumour suppressor, can lead to cancer.周期蛋白-CDK复合物和检查点决定细胞能否继续周期；控制失效（如抑癌基因出错）可导致癌症。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

Myth: the signal molecule itself travels into the cell and does the work. Fix: most ligands stay outside; the receptor changes shape and relays the message, so the cell responds only if it has the right receptor.常见误区：以为信号分子本身进入细胞完成工作。纠正：多数配体留在细胞外，受体改变形状来传递信息；只有带有对应受体的细胞才会反应。

The official topics, in plain words官方主题，讲成大白话

4.1 Cell Communication细胞通讯

Cells signal by contact, locally, or over long distances.细胞通过直接接触、局部或远距离方式传递信号。

Cells signal in three ranges: direct contact, local signals such as paracrine and synaptic signals, and long-distance hormones. Every cell may be exposed to a signal, but only cells with the matching receptor respond.

细胞的信号有三种范围：直接接触、局部信号（如旁分泌和突触信号）和远距离激素。信号可能到达所有细胞，但只有带匹配受体的细胞才会反应。

4.2 Introduction to Signal Transduction信号转导导论

A ligand binds a receptor; the signal is relayed into a response.配体结合受体，信号被传递并引发反应。

Signal transduction has three stages: reception (ligand binds receptor), transduction (relay inside the cell) and response (a change in cell activity). The ligand usually stays outside; the receptor changes shape and passes the message on.

信号转导分三个阶段：接收（配体结合受体）、转导（细胞内的传递）和反应（细胞活动的改变）。配体通常留在细胞外；受体改变形状并传递信息。

4.3 Signal Transduction Pathways信号转导通路

Cascades of phosphorylation and second messengers amplify signals.磷酸化级联与第二信使放大信号。

In many pathways a receptor activates a G protein, then an enzyme makes a second messenger such as cAMP, which activates kinases that phosphorylate other proteins in a cascade. Each step multiplies the signal (amplification), and phosphatases and enzymes that break down cAMP switch it off.

在许多通路中，受体激活G蛋白，随后酶产生cAMP等第二信使，再激活使其他蛋白磷酸化的激酶，形成级联。每一步都使信号倍增（放大），磷酸酶和分解cAMP的酶使其关闭。

4.4 Feedback反馈

Negative feedback keeps balance; positive feedback amplifies change.负反馈维持平衡；正反馈放大变化。

Negative feedback reduces the original change and keeps a variable near a set point, as insulin does for blood glucose. Positive feedback increases the change until something ends it, as oxytocin does in childbirth.

负反馈减弱最初的变化，使变量保持在设定点附近，如胰岛素调节血糖。正反馈使变化不断加强直到某事件终止，如分娩时的催产素。

4.5 Cell Cycle细胞周期

Interphase prepares and copies; mitosis and cytokinesis divide.间期准备并复制；有丝分裂与胞质分裂完成分裂。

The cell cycle has interphase (G1, S, G2) and the mitotic phase (prophase, metaphase, anaphase, telophase, then cytokinesis). DNA is copied in S phase, and sister chromatids separate in anaphase.

细胞周期包括间期（G1、S、G2）和分裂期（前期、中期、后期、末期，然后是胞质分裂）。DNA在S期复制，姐妹染色单体在后期分离。

4.6 Regulation of Cell Cycle细胞周期的调控

Cyclins, CDKs and checkpoints control division; cancer is lost control.周期蛋白、CDK与检查点控制分裂；癌症是控制失效。

Cyclin-CDK complexes and checkpoints (G1, G2, spindle) decide whether the cycle continues. Loss of control, such as a stuck-on growth gene or a missing tumour suppressor like p53, lets damaged cells divide and can cause cancer.

周期蛋白-CDK复合物和检查点（G1、G2、纺锤体）决定周期是否继续。控制失效，如生长基因持续开启或缺少p53等抑癌基因，会让受损细胞继续分裂并可能导致癌症。
