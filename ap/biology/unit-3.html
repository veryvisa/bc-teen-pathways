3.1 Enzymes酶4 Representing and Describing Data4 数据表示与描述

A student graphs reaction rate against substrate concentration for a fictional enzyme with and without inhibitor N. Without N, the curve levels off at 50 units. With N, the curve levels off at 20 units, even at the highest substrate concentration. Which description of the graph is correct?

一名学生为某虚构酶画出反应速率与底物浓度的关系曲线，分别是有无抑制剂 N 两种情况。无 N 时曲线在 50 单位处趋于平稳；有 N 时，即使底物浓度最高，曲线也只在 20 单位处趋于平稳。哪项对图像的描述正确？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Most enzymes are proteins (some RNA molecules, called ribozymes, also catalyse reactions). The active site binds its specific substrate(s) through shape and chemistry, and often shifts slightly to grip them more closely (induced fit), which lowers the activation energy so the reaction happens faster. The enzyme is not used up and does not change the reaction's overall ΔG.

大多数酶是蛋白质（也有一些 RNA 分子，称为核酶（ribozyme），能催化反应）。活性位点凭借形状和化学性质结合特定底物，并常常稍微变形以更紧密地抓住底物（诱导契合，induced fit），从而降低活化能，使反应加快。酶不会被消耗，也不改变反应的总 ΔG。

Claim论点

N lowers the maximum rate, which fits a noncompetitive inhibitor.

N 降低了最大速率，符合非竞争性抑制剂的特点。

Evidence证据

The inhibited curve plateaus at 20 units instead of 50, even at the highest substrate level.

受抑制的曲线在 20 单位而不是 50 单位处达到平台，即使底物浓度最高也一样。

Reasoning推理

A plateau means every working enzyme is saturated; a lower plateau means fewer enzymes are working, which happens when the inhibitor binds outside the active site and changes its shape.

平台意味着所有能工作的酶都已饱和；平台更低说明能工作的酶更少，这正是抑制剂结合在活性位点以外并改变其形状时的结果。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Applies the competitive-inhibitor pattern; the data show the plateau stays at 20.套用了竞争性抑制的规律；但数据显示平台一直停在 20。
C. Reads the inhibited curve as above the control; an inhibitor lowers the rate.把受抑制的曲线读成高于对照；抑制剂会降低速率。
D. Ignores the plateau the stem describes; enzymes become saturated.忽略了题干描述的平台；酶会达到饱和。

3.1 Enzymes酶1 Concept Explanation1 概念解释

A fictional enzyme, ketase, converts molecule K into product L. Molecule M has a shape very similar to K. When M is added, the reaction slows, but adding much more K brings the rate back near its maximum. Which explanation fits best?

一种虚构的酶「酮酶」把分子 K 转化为产物 L。分子 M 的形状与 K 非常相似。加入 M 后反应变慢，但再加入大量 K 后，速率又接近最大值。哪种解释最合理？

Most enzymes are proteins (some RNA molecules, called ribozymes, also catalyse reactions). The active site binds its specific substrate(s) through shape and chemistry, and often shifts slightly to grip them more closely (induced fit), which lowers the activation energy so the reaction happens faster. The enzyme is not used up and does not change the reaction's overall ΔG.

Claim论点

M is a competitive inhibitor of ketase.

M 是酮酶的竞争性抑制剂。

Evidence证据

M resembles K, and a high concentration of K restores the rate.

M 与 K 形状相似，而高浓度的 K 能恢复反应速率。

Reasoning推理

A competitive inhibitor occupies the active site; when substrate greatly outnumbers it, substrate wins most binding events, so the maximum rate can still be reached.

竞争性抑制剂占据活性位点；当底物数量远多于抑制剂时，大多数结合机会被底物占据，所以仍能达到最大速率。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Thinks any inhibitor changes shape; but a shape change at another site would not be reversed by adding more substrate.以为所有抑制剂都改变酶的形状；但在其他位点引起的形状改变，不会因为加入更多底物而被逆转。
B. Mixes up inhibitors with catalysts; a molecule that slows the reaction is not lowering activation energy.把抑制剂和催化剂混淆了；让反应变慢的分子并没有降低活化能。
D. Thinks inhibition means denaturing; denaturation is not reversed by adding substrate.以为抑制就是变性；变性不会因为加入底物而恢复。

3.2 Environmental Impacts on Enzyme Function环境对酶功能的影响6 Argumentation6 论证

A student heats a sample of a fictional enzyme from a cold-water fish to 55 °C for 10 minutes, then cools it to 10 °C, its usual temperature. The enzyme now shows almost no activity. Which claim is best supported?

一名学生把一种来自冷水鱼的虚构酶加热到 55 °C 保持 10 分钟，再冷却到它平时所处的 10 °C。此时酶几乎没有活性。哪个结论最有依据？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Below the optimum temperature, warming speeds collisions; above it, weak bonds break and the enzyme denatures, often for good. pH changes the charges on R-groups, so each enzyme has an optimum pH. Competitive inhibitors block the active site and can be outcompeted by more substrate; noncompetitive inhibitors bind elsewhere and lower the maximum rate.

低于最适温度时，升温加快分子碰撞；高于最适温度时，弱键断裂，酶变性，而且往往不可逆。pH 改变 R 基团的电荷，所以每种酶都有最适 pH。竞争性抑制剂占据活性位点，可被更多底物“挤走”；非竞争性抑制剂结合在别处，使最大速率降低。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

The enzyme was denatured by the heat and did not refold when cooled.

酶被高温变性，冷却后也没有恢复原来的折叠。

Evidence证据

Activity was lost after heating even when the enzyme was returned to its normal working temperature.

即使回到正常工作温度，加热后的酶仍然失去了活性。

Reasoning推理

High temperature disrupts the hydrogen bonds and other weak interactions in tertiary structure; the active site loses its shape, and the change is often not reversible.

高温破坏三级结构中的氢键和其他弱相互作用，活性位点失去原有形状，而这种改变常常不可逆。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
B. Thinks low temperature alone explains it; the enzyme normally works at 10 °C, so cold cannot be the cause.以为单靠低温就能解释；但这种酶平时就在 10 °C 工作，所以低温不是原因。
C. Thinks heat removes molecules; the stem gives no evidence that substrate was lost.以为加热会去掉分子；题干没有任何证据表明底物丢失。
D. Thinks denaturing changes the amino-acid sequence; heat breaks weak bonds, not peptide bonds.以为变性会改变氨基酸序列；加热破坏的是弱键，而不是肽键。

A student measured product formed by an enzyme at 30 °C: 0 µmol at 0 min, 12 µmol at 4 min and 24 µmol at 8 min. At 40 °C the same enzyme formed 40 µmol in 8 min. By what percentage did the average rate increase from 30 °C to 40 °C?

一名学生测量某酶在 30 °C 下生成的产物：0 分钟时 0 µmol，4 分钟时 12 µmol，8 分钟时 24 µmol。在 40 °C 下，同一种酶 8 分钟生成 40 µmol。从 30 °C 到 40 °C，平均速率提高了百分之多少？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Below the optimum temperature, warming speeds collisions; above it, weak bonds break and the enzyme denatures, often for good. pH changes the charges on R-groups, so each enzyme has an optimum pH. Competitive inhibitors block the active site and can be outcompeted by more substrate; noncompetitive inhibitors bind elsewhere and lower the maximum rate.

低于最适温度时，升温加快分子碰撞；高于最适温度时，弱键断裂，酶变性，而且往往不可逆。pH 改变 R 基团的电荷，所以每种酶都有最适 pH。竞争性抑制剂占据活性位点，可被更多底物“挤走”；非竞争性抑制剂结合在别处，使最大速率降低。

Claim论点

The rate rose by about 67%.

速率大约提高了 67%。

Evidence证据

At 30 °C the rate is 24/8 = 3 µmol/min; at 40 °C it is 40/8 = 5 µmol/min.

30 °C 时速率为 24/8 = 3 µmol/min；40 °C 时为 40/8 = 5 µmol/min。

Reasoning推理

Percent change = (new − old)/old × 100 = (5 − 3)/3 × 100 ≈ 67%; below the optimum, warmer temperatures raise collision frequency.

百分比变化 = (新值 − 旧值)/旧值 × 100 = (5 − 3)/3 × 100 ≈ 67%；在最适温度以下，升温会增加分子碰撞频率。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Divides by the new value (2/5); percent change uses the original value as the base.用新值作分母（2/5）；百分比变化应以原值为基数。
B. Compares the two totals as a ratio minus one, then rounds wrongly; 5/3 − 1 is 0.67, not 0.60.用两者之比减一但取整出错；5/3 − 1 等于 0.67，不是 0.60。
D. Subtracts the totals (40 − 24 = 16) and reads that as a percentage; a difference is not a percent change.把两个总量相减（40 − 24 = 16）当作百分比；差值不等于百分比变化。

3.3 Cellular Energy细胞能量2 Visual Representations2 图示分析

An energy diagram for a fictional reaction shows reactants at 60 kJ, a peak at 110 kJ without enzyme, a peak at 80 kJ with enzyme, and products at 20 kJ. What does the drop from 60 kJ to 20 kJ represent?

某虚构反应的能量图显示：反应物在 60 kJ，无酶时峰值为 110 kJ，有酶时峰值为 80 kJ，产物在 20 kJ。从 60 kJ 降到 20 kJ 代表什么？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Cells need a constant input of energy because every energy change loses some as heat. ATP hydrolysis is exergonic, and cells couple it to endergonic work, often by passing a phosphate to another molecule. The ΔG values of coupled reactions add up; if the total is negative, the process can proceed.

因为每次能量转化都有一部分以热的形式散失，细胞需要持续的能量输入。ATP 水解是放能的，细胞把它与吸能过程偶联，常见方式是把磷酸基团转给另一个分子。偶联反应的 ΔG 可以相加；总和为负，过程就能进行。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

The drop is ΔG, which is −40 kJ for both paths.

这段下降就是 ΔG，两条路径都是 −40 kJ。

Evidence证据

Reactants start at 60 kJ and products end at 20 kJ whether or not the enzyme is present.

无论有没有酶，反应物都从 60 kJ 开始，产物都在 20 kJ 结束。

Reasoning推理

ΔG depends only on the energy of reactants and products; an enzyme lowers the peak (activation energy) but leaves the start and end points unchanged.

ΔG 只取决于反应物和产物的能量；酶降低的是峰值（活化能），起点和终点不变。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
B. Mixes up two parts of the diagram; the saving is the difference between the peaks (30 kJ).混淆了图上的两个部分；节省的是两个峰值之差（30 kJ）。
C. Thinks enzymes supply energy; they only lower the barrier.以为酶提供能量；酶只是降低能垒。
D. Reads activation energy as start to end; it is start to peak (20 kJ with enzyme).把活化能读成起点到终点；活化能是起点到峰值（有酶时为 20 kJ）。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

On a fictional energy diagram, reactants sit at 50 kJ, the uncatalysed peak at 120 kJ, the catalysed peak at 90 kJ and products at 70 kJ. Which is true?

Products (70) are above reactants (50), so ΔG = +20 kJ; the enzyme lowers the peak by 30 kJ but cannot change ΔG.

3.4 Photosynthesis光合作用4 Representing and Describing Data4 数据表示与描述

A student measures O2 released by a fictional water plant at five light levels: 2, 5, 8, 9 and 9 mL/h as light rises. Which statement best describes the data?

一名学生测量某虚构水生植物在五个光照水平下释放的 O2：随光照增强依次为 2、5、8、9、9 mL/h。哪项最能描述这些数据？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

In the thylakoid membranes, light excites electrons, water is split (releasing O2), and an electron transport chain pumps H+ into the thylakoid so ATP synthase can make ATP; the electrons end on NADPH. In the stroma, the enzyme rubisco attaches CO2 to RuBP (carbon fixation), and ATP and NADPH then reduce the products to G3P, the three-carbon sugar used to build glucose and other molecules. The two stages depend on each other.

在类囊体膜上，光激发电子，水被分解（释放 O2），电子传递链把 H+ 泵入类囊体腔，ATP 合酶借此合成 ATP；电子最终交给 NADPH。在叶绿体基质中，rubisco 把 CO2 连接到 RuBP 上（碳固定），随后 ATP 和 NADPH 把产物还原为 G3P——一种三碳糖，用来合成葡萄糖和其他分子。两个阶段相互依赖。

Claim论点

The rate rises and then plateaus, so at high light another factor limits photosynthesis.

速率先上升后达到平台，说明强光下有其他因素限制光合作用。

Evidence证据

The values climb from 2 to 9 mL/h but stop changing at the last two light levels.

数值从 2 升到 9 mL/h，但在最后两个光照水平不再变化。

Reasoning推理

Once the light reactions run fast enough, something else, such as CO2 supply or Calvin cycle enzymes, becomes the limiting factor.

当光反应足够快时，其他因素（如 CO2 供应或卡尔文循环中的酶）就成为限制因素。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Ignores the plateau in the last two values.忽略了最后两个数值形成的平台。
B. Reads a plateau as a fall; the values stay at 9 mL/h.把平台读成下降；数值保持在 9 mL/h。
C. Overlooks the clear rise from 2 to 9 mL/h.忽略了从 2 到 9 mL/h 的明显上升。

3.1 Enzymes酶

The active site binds specific substrates by shape and chemistry, lowering activation energy.活性位点凭借形状和化学性质结合特定底物，从而降低活化能。

Most enzymes are proteins (some RNA molecules, called ribozymes, also catalyse reactions). The active site binds its specific substrate(s) through shape and chemistry, and often shifts slightly to grip them more closely (induced fit), which lowers the activation energy so the reaction happens faster. The enzyme is not used up and does not change the reaction's overall ΔG.

大多数酶是蛋白质（也有一些 RNA 分子，称为核酶（ribozyme），能催化反应）。活性位点凭借形状和化学性质结合特定底物，并常常稍微变形以更紧密地抓住底物（诱导契合，induced fit），从而降低活化能，使反应加快。酶不会被消耗，也不改变反应的总 ΔG。

3.2 Environmental Impacts on Enzyme Function环境对酶功能的影响

Temperature, pH and inhibitors change the active site or access to it.温度、pH 和抑制剂会改变活性位点，或改变底物能否进入活性位点。

Below the optimum temperature, warming speeds collisions; above it, weak bonds break and the enzyme denatures, often for good. pH changes the charges on R-groups, so each enzyme has an optimum pH. Competitive inhibitors block the active site and can be outcompeted by more substrate; noncompetitive inhibitors bind elsewhere and lower the maximum rate.

低于最适温度时，升温加快分子碰撞；高于最适温度时，弱键断裂，酶变性，而且往往不可逆。pH 改变 R 基团的电荷，所以每种酶都有最适 pH。竞争性抑制剂占据活性位点，可被更多底物“挤走”；非竞争性抑制剂结合在别处，使最大速率降低。

3.3 Cellular Energy细胞能量

Cells couple exergonic ATP hydrolysis to endergonic work.细胞把放能的 ATP 水解与吸能的反应和做功偶联起来。

Cells need a constant input of energy because every energy change loses some as heat. ATP hydrolysis is exergonic, and cells couple it to endergonic work, often by passing a phosphate to another molecule. The ΔG values of coupled reactions add up; if the total is negative, the process can proceed.

因为每次能量转化都有一部分以热的形式散失，细胞需要持续的能量输入。ATP 水解是放能的，细胞把它与吸能过程偶联，常见方式是把磷酸基团转给另一个分子。偶联反应的 ΔG 可以相加；总和为负，过程就能进行。

3.4 Photosynthesis光合作用

Light reactions make ATP and NADPH; the Calvin cycle uses them to fix CO2.光反应产生 ATP 和 NADPH；卡尔文循环用它们固定 CO2。

In the thylakoid membranes, light excites electrons, water is split (releasing O2), and an electron transport chain pumps H+ into the thylakoid so ATP synthase can make ATP; the electrons end on NADPH. In the stroma, the enzyme rubisco attaches CO2 to RuBP (carbon fixation), and ATP and NADPH then reduce the products to G3P, the three-carbon sugar used to build glucose and other molecules. The two stages depend on each other.

在类囊体膜上，光激发电子，水被分解（释放 O2），电子传递链把 H+ 泵入类囊体腔，ATP 合酶借此合成 ATP；电子最终交给 NADPH。在叶绿体基质中，rubisco 把 CO2 连接到 RuBP 上（碳固定），随后 ATP 和 NADPH 把产物还原为 G3P——一种三碳糖，用来合成葡萄糖和其他分子。两个阶段相互依赖。

3.5 Cellular Respiration细胞呼吸

Electrons from food fall to oxygen; the proton gradient they build powers ATP synthase.食物中的电子最终交给氧；电子传递建立的质子梯度驱动 ATP 合酶。

Glycolysis in the cytosol splits glucose into pyruvate for 2 ATP and some NADH; pyruvate oxidation and the Krebs cycle in the matrix release CO2 and make more NADH, and the Krebs cycle also makes FADH2. The electron transport chain passes those electrons to oxygen and pumps H+, and ATP synthase uses the H+ gradient to make most of the ATP. Without oxygen, fermentation regenerates NAD+ so glycolysis can continue.

糖酵解在细胞质基质中把葡萄糖分解为丙酮酸，产生 2 个 ATP 和一些 NADH；丙酮酸氧化和克雷布斯循环在线粒体基质中释放 CO2 并产生更多 NADH，克雷布斯循环还产生 FADH2。电子传递链把这些电子交给氧并泵出 H+，ATP 合酶利用 H+ 梯度产生大部分 ATP。没有氧时，发酵再生 NAD+，使糖酵解得以继续。

🃏 Five concept cards五张概念卡

What does an enzyme change about a reaction, and what does it not change?酶改变了反应的什么？又没有改变什么？

It lowers the activation energy, so the reaction goes faster. It does not change ΔG or which direction is favoured.它降低活化能，使反应更快；它不改变 ΔG，也不改变反应倾向的方向。

Competitive vs noncompetitive inhibitor竞争性抑制剂 vs 非竞争性抑制剂

A competitive inhibitor binds the active site, so more substrate can outcompete it. A noncompetitive inhibitor binds elsewhere and changes the enzyme's shape, so more substrate does not restore the maximum rate.竞争性抑制剂结合活性位点，加入更多底物可以与之竞争；非竞争性抑制剂结合在别处并改变酶的形状，加更多底物也恢复不了最大速率。

Energy coupling能量偶联（energy coupling）

Cells pair the exergonic hydrolysis of ATP with an endergonic process, often by transferring a phosphate to a molecule so it becomes less stable and more reactive.细胞把放能的 ATP 水解与吸能过程配对，常见方式是把一个磷酸基团转给某个分子，使它更不稳定、更容易反应。

Where are the light reactions and the Calvin cycle?光反应和卡尔文循环分别在哪里进行？

Light reactions: thylakoid membranes (H+ builds up in the thylakoid space). Calvin cycle: the stroma, using ATP and NADPH to fix CO2.光反应：类囊体膜（H+ 在类囊体腔内积累）。卡尔文循环：叶绿体基质（stroma），利用 ATP 和 NADPH 固定 CO2。

Why does a cell ferment?细胞为什么要进行发酵？

To turn NADH back into NAD+ when the electron transport chain cannot run, so glycolysis can continue making 2 ATP per glucose.当电子传递链无法运转时，把 NADH 变回 NAD+，使糖酵解能继续进行，每个葡萄糖产生 2 个 ATP。
