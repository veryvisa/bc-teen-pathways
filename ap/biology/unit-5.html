A student's sketch shows a cell in which homologous chromosomes are paired as tetrads and lined up together across the middle of the cell. Which stage does the sketch show?

某学生画的示意图中，同源染色体两两配对成四分体（tetrad），并一起排列在细胞中央。图中是哪个时期？

减数分裂是在一次 DNA 复制后连续两次分裂。减数第一次分裂分开同源染色体对，使染色体数减半；第二次分裂分开姐妹染色单体，形成四个单倍体细胞。

Claim论点

The sketch shows metaphase I.

Evidence证据

The chromosomes are lined up as homologous pairs (tetrads), not as single chromosomes.

染色体以同源染色体对（四分体）排列，而不是单条排列。

Reasoning推理

Only meiosis I pairs homologs; in mitosis and metaphase II, chromosomes line up one by one.

B. Focuses on 'meiosis' and ignores that the chromosomes are lined up, not moving apart.只看到「减数分裂」，忽略了染色体在排列而不是分离。

A cell from a fictional fern has a diploid number of 2n = 12. At the end of meiosis I, what does each daughter cell contain?

某虚构蕨类的细胞二倍体数为 2n = 12。减数第一次分裂（meiosis I）结束时，每个子细胞含有什么？

减数分裂是在一次 DNA 复制后连续两次分裂。减数第一次分裂分开同源染色体对，使染色体数减半；第二次分裂分开姐妹染色单体，形成四个单倍体细胞。

Claim论点

Each cell has 6 chromosomes, and each is still made of two sister chromatids.

每个细胞有 6 条染色体，每条仍由两条姐妹染色单体组成。

Evidence证据

2n = 12 means 6 homologous pairs; meiosis I separates the pairs.

2n = 12 表示有 6 对同源染色体；减数第一次分裂把同源染色体分开。

C. Mixes up the two divisions: chromatids separate in meiosis II, not meiosis I.把两次分裂混淆了：染色单体在减数第二次分裂才分开，而不是第一次。
D. Remembers the chromatids are still joined but forgets the number is halved.记得染色单体仍相连，却忘了染色体数已减半。

5.2 Meiosis and Genetic Diversity减数分裂与遗传多样性6 Argumentation6 论证

A student tracks one chromosome pair through a single meiosis. Of the four gametes, two have the normal number, one has an extra copy (n+1) and one is missing that chromosome (n-1). Which claim do these data best support?

某学生追踪一对染色体经过一次减数分裂。四个配子中，两个数目正常，一个多一条（n+1），一个少一条（n-1）。这些数据最支持哪个结论？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Crossing over in prophase I swaps segments between homologs, and independent assortment at metaphase I shuffles whole chromosomes. Random fertilization then combines two unique gametes, so siblings differ.

前期 I 的交叉互换在同源染色体间交换片段，中期 I 的自由组合打乱整条染色体。随机受精再把两个独特的配子结合，所以兄弟姐妹各不相同。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

Nondisjunction happened in meiosis II.

不分离发生在减数第二次分裂（meiosis II）。

Evidence证据

Half the gametes are normal; only one n+1 and one n-1 gamete formed.

一半配子正常；只有一个 n+1 和一个 n-1 配子。

Reasoning推理

If homologs fail to separate in meiosis I, all four gametes are abnormal. Failure of sister chromatids to separate in one meiosis II cell affects only that cell's two gametes.

若同源染色体在第一次分裂不分离，四个配子全部异常。若只有一个细胞在第二次分裂时姐妹染色单体不分离，只影响该细胞产生的两个配子。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Knows nondisjunction causes n+1 and n-1 but forgets that a meiosis I error makes all four gametes abnormal.知道不分离会产生 n+1 和 n-1，却忘了第一次分裂出错时四个配子全部异常。
B. Links any odd result to crossing over; crossing over changes allele combinations, not chromosome number.把任何异常都归于交叉互换；交叉互换改变的是等位基因组合，不是染色体数目。
D. Without replication, meiosis could not produce two normal gametes and one extra copy.若没有复制，减数分裂不可能产生两个正常配子和一个多一条的配子。

一次减数分裂的四个配子全部异常：两个 n+1，两个 n−1。不分离发生在何时？

If homologs fail to separate in meiosis I, both meiosis II cells are already abnormal, so all four gametes are affected.

5.2 Meiosis and Genetic Diversity减数分裂与遗传多样性5 Statistical Tests and Data Analysis5 统计检验与数据分析

A fictional newt has 2n = 8. Ignoring crossing over, how many genetically different combinations of chromosomes can independent assortment produce in its gametes?

某虚构蝾螈 2n = 8。不考虑交叉互换（crossing over），自由组合（independent assortment）能在配子中产生多少种不同的染色体组合？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Crossing over in prophase I swaps segments between homologs, and independent assortment at metaphase I shuffles whole chromosomes. Random fertilization then combines two unique gametes, so siblings differ.

前期 I 的交叉互换在同源染色体间交换片段，中期 I 的自由组合打乱整条染色体。随机受精再把两个独特的配子结合，所以兄弟姐妹各不相同。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

Independent assortment can produce 16 combinations.

自由组合可产生 16 种组合。

Evidence证据

2n = 8 gives n = 4 homologous pairs.

2n = 8 表示 n = 4 对同源染色体。

Reasoning推理

Each pair lines up in one of two orientations at metaphase I, so the number of combinations is 2^n = 2^4 = 16.

每对同源染色体在中期 I 有两种排列方向，所以组合数为 2^n = 2^4 = 16。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
B. Uses 2n itself instead of 2 raised to the number of pairs.直接用 2n 的值，而不是 2 的「对数」次方。
C. Counts the pairs but forgets each pair has two orientations that multiply.数了对数，却忘了每对的两种方向要相乘。
D. Squares 8 or counts zygote combinations from two parents instead of gametes from one.把 8 平方，或算成两个亲本形成合子的组合，而不是一个个体的配子。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

A fictional plant has 2n = 6. Ignoring crossing over, how many chromosome combinations can its gametes have?

某虚构植物 2n = 6。不考虑交叉互换，其配子可有多少种染色体组合？

n = 3 pairs, so 2^3 = 8 combinations.

n = 3 对，所以 2^3 = 8 种组合。

5.3 Mendelian Genetics孟德尔遗传5 Statistical Tests and Data Analysis5 统计检验与数据分析

In a fictional plant, three genes assort independently, and A, B and C are completely dominant. If an AaBbCc plant self-pollinates, what fraction of offspring is expected to show the dominant A trait, the recessive b trait and the dominant C trait?

某虚构植物中，三个基因自由组合，A、B、C 均为完全显性。AaBbCc 自交，后代中表现 A 显性、b 隐性、C 显性性状的比例预计是多少？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Each gamete gets one allele of each gene (segregation), and genes on different chromosomes sort independently. Use the product rule for 'and', the sum rule for 'or', and chi-square to test data against a predicted ratio.

每个配子只得到每个基因的一个等位基因（分离定律），不同染色体上的基因独立分配。「且」用乘法法则，「或」用加法法则，再用卡方检验数据是否符合预测比例。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

The expected fraction is 9/64.

预计比例为 9/64。

Evidence证据

Each gene is a separate Aa x Aa cross: P(dominant) = 3/4 and P(recessive) = 1/4.

每个基因都是一个 Aa × Aa 杂交：显性概率 3/4，隐性概率 1/4。

Reasoning推理

For independent events use the product rule: 3/4 x 1/4 x 3/4 = 9/64.

独立事件用乘法法则：3/4 × 1/4 × 3/4 = 9/64。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Uses 1/4 for a dominant phenotype, as if only AA counted.把显性表型概率当成 1/4，好像只算 AA。
B. Multiplies 3/4 three times, treating the b trait as dominant.三次都乘 3/4，把 b 性状当成显性。
C. Adds or drops one gene instead of multiplying all three.漏掉或加错一个基因，而不是三个相乘。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

Two fictional peas AaBb x AaBb are crossed; the genes assort independently. What fraction of offspring is expected to be aa and show the dominant B trait?

虚构豌豆 AaBb × AaBb 杂交，两基因自由组合。预计后代中 aa 且表现 B 显性的比例是多少？

P(aa) x P(B_) = 1/4 x 3/4 = 3/16.

按乘法法则：P(aa) × P(B_) = 1/4 × 3/4 = 3/16。

5.3 Mendelian Genetics孟德尔遗传3 Questions and Methods3 问题与方法

A breeder has a fictional beetle with shiny wings, the dominant trait. She wants to know whether it is homozygous or heterozygous. Which cross gives the clearest answer?

某饲养者有一只翅膀有光泽（显性性状）的虚构甲虫，想知道它是纯合子还是杂合子。哪种杂交最能给出明确答案？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Each gamete gets one allele of each gene (segregation), and genes on different chromosomes sort independently. Use the product rule for 'and', the sum rule for 'or', and chi-square to test data against a predicted ratio.

每个配子只得到每个基因的一个等位基因（分离定律），不同染色体上的基因独立分配。「且」用乘法法则，「或」用加法法则，再用卡方检验数据是否符合预测比例。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

A testcross with a homozygous recessive (dull) beetle answers the question.

与隐性纯合（无光泽）甲虫进行测交（testcross）能回答这个问题。

Evidence证据

Dull wings is the recessive phenotype, so a dull beetle must be homozygous recessive.

无光泽是隐性表型，所以无光泽甲虫一定是隐性纯合子。

Reasoning推理

The recessive parent gives only recessive alleles, so offspring phenotypes reveal the unknown parent's alleles: any dull offspring means it is heterozygous.

隐性亲本只提供隐性等位基因，所以后代表型直接显示未知亲本的等位基因：只要出现无光泽后代，它就是杂合子。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
B. A shiny partner could be AA or Aa, so the result cannot be read clearly.有光泽的配偶可能是 AA 或 Aa，结果无法明确解读。
C. The offspring's genotypes are also unknown, so this adds uncertainty.后代的基因型也未知，只会增加不确定性。
D. Changing the environment may change phenotype but does not reveal genotype.改变环境可能改变表型，但不能揭示基因型。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

A purple-flowered plant (purple dominant) crossed with a white plant gives 52 purple and 48 white offspring. What is the purple parent's genotype?

紫花植株（紫色为显性）与白花植株杂交，得到 52 紫、48 白。紫花亲本的基因型是什么？

White offspring mean the purple parent gave a recessive allele, and the roughly 1:1 ratio fits a heterozygote in a testcross.

出现白花说明紫花亲本提供了隐性等位基因，约 1:1 的比例符合测交中的杂合子。

5.4 Non-Mendelian Genetics非孟德尔遗传6 Argumentation6 论证

In a fictional plant, leaf striping is passed on through chloroplast genes. Which result from reciprocal crosses best supports this claim?

某虚构植物中，叶片条纹通过叶绿体基因遗传。正反交（reciprocal cross）的哪种结果最支持这一说法？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Linked genes travel together unless crossing over separates them, and X-linked recessive traits show more in males. Incomplete dominance, codominance, pleiotropy (one gene affecting several traits) and organelle genes also break Mendel's simple ratios; many traits are also polygenic, shaped by several genes.

连锁基因一起遗传，除非交叉互换把它们分开；X 连锁隐性性状在雄性中更常见。不完全显性、共显性、基因多效性（pleiotropy，一个基因影响多个性状）和细胞器基因也会打破孟德尔的简单比例；此外许多性状是多基因性状，受多个基因影响。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

Offspring matching only the seed (maternal) parent supports chloroplast inheritance.

后代只与种子（母本）亲本一致，支持叶绿体遗传。

Evidence证据

The phenotype follows the parent that provides the egg, whichever direction the cross goes.

无论杂交方向如何，表型都跟随提供卵细胞的亲本。

Reasoning推理

In most plants, chloroplasts come from the egg's cytoplasm, so organelle genes are inherited maternally and do not follow Mendelian ratios.

在大多数植物中，叶绿体来自卵细胞的细胞质，因此细胞器基因由母本遗传，不符合孟德尔比例。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. A 3:1 ratio is the signature of a nuclear gene, not an organelle gene.3:1 是核基因的特征，而不是细胞器基因。
B. Expects a sex-linked pattern; a chloroplast gene follows the seed parent, not the offspring's sex.以为是伴性模式；叶绿体基因跟随种子亲本，而不是后代的性别。
C. Reverses the direction: pollen contributes very little cytoplasm in most plants.方向弄反了：大多数植物中花粉几乎不提供细胞质。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

In a fictional mouse, a trait appears in all offspring of affected mothers and in no offspring of affected fathers with unaffected mothers. Which explanation fits?

某虚构小鼠中，患病母亲的所有后代都有该性状，而患病父亲与正常母亲的后代都没有。哪种解释最合理？

Inheritance only through the mother, to all offspring of both sexes, is the pattern of mitochondrial genes, which come with the egg.

只经母亲传递、且传给所有雌雄后代，是线粒体基因的模式，因为线粒体随卵细胞传递。

5.5 Environmental Effects on Phenotype环境对表型的影响4 Representing and Describing Data4 数据表示与描述

Cuttings from one fictional shrub (genetically identical) were grown at 15 C and at 30 C. At 15 C, 90% of new leaves were purple; at 30 C, 10% were purple. Which statement best describes these data?

从同一株虚构灌木取下的插条（遗传上相同）分别种在 15°C 和 30°C。15°C 时 90% 的新叶为紫色；30°C 时为 10%。哪一句最准确地描述了这些数据？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Phenotype comes from genotype and environment together. The environment can affect gene expression and the activity of gene products, changing phenotype without changing genotype.

表型由基因型和环境共同决定。环境可以影响基因表达以及基因产物的活性，从而在不改变基因型的情况下改变表型。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

Purple leaves were more common at 15 C than at 30 C: temperature affects phenotype even though the genotype is unchanged (phenotypic plasticity).

15°C 时紫色叶比 30°C 时更常见：即使基因型不变，温度也会影响表型（表型可塑性）。

Evidence证据

90% purple at 15 C versus 10% at 30 C in genetically identical cuttings.

遗传上相同的插条在 15°C 时 90% 紫色，30°C 时 10%。

Reasoning推理

Because genotype was held constant, the difference shows that temperature affects phenotype even though the genotype is unchanged (phenotypic plasticity).

由于基因型保持不变，差异说明即使基因型不变，温度也会影响表型（表型可塑性）。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
B. Thinks the environment changes the DNA; the clones keep the same genes.以为环境改变了 DNA；克隆植株的基因相同。
C. Ignores the large difference between 90% and 10%.忽略了 90% 与 10% 的巨大差别。
D. Forgets that identical genotypes gave different phenotypes here.忘了这里相同基因型产生了不同表型。

The core核心

Meiosis shuffles and halves chromosomes, and that physical behaviour is what makes inheritance predictable by probability.减数分裂打乱并减半染色体，正是这种物理行为让遗传可以用概率预测。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Meiosis I separates homologous pairs; meiosis II separates sister chromatids.减数第一次分裂分开同源染色体对；第二次分裂分开姐妹染色单体。
  2. Use the product rule for 'and', the sum rule for 'or', and chi-square to test whether data fit a predicted ratio.「且」用乘法法则，「或」用加法法则，用卡方检验判断数据是否符合预测比例。

5.1 Meiosis减数分裂

Meiosis halves chromosome number, making haploid gametes from diploid cells.减数分裂使染色体数减半，由二倍体细胞产生单倍体配子。

Meiosis is two divisions after one round of DNA replication. Meiosis I separates homologous pairs and halves the chromosome number; meiosis II separates sister chromatids, giving four haploid cells.

减数分裂是在一次 DNA 复制后连续两次分裂。减数第一次分裂分开同源染色体对，使染色体数减半；第二次分裂分开姐妹染色单体，形成四个单倍体细胞。

5.2 Meiosis and Genetic Diversity减数分裂与遗传多样性

Crossing over, independent assortment and fertilization create new allele combinations.交叉互换、自由组合和受精产生新的等位基因组合。

Crossing over in prophase I swaps segments between homologs, and independent assortment at metaphase I shuffles whole chromosomes. Random fertilization then combines two unique gametes, so siblings differ.

前期 I 的交叉互换在同源染色体间交换片段，中期 I 的自由组合打乱整条染色体。随机受精再把两个独特的配子结合，所以兄弟姐妹各不相同。

5.3 Mendelian Genetics孟德尔遗传

Segregation and independent assortment let probability rules predict offspring ratios.分离定律与自由组合定律让概率法则可以预测后代比例。

Each gamete gets one allele of each gene (segregation), and genes on different chromosomes sort independently. Use the product rule for 'and', the sum rule for 'or', and chi-square to test data against a predicted ratio.

每个配子只得到每个基因的一个等位基因（分离定律），不同染色体上的基因独立分配。「且」用乘法法则，「或」用加法法则，再用卡方检验数据是否符合预测比例。

5.4 Non-Mendelian Genetics非孟德尔遗传

Linkage, sex linkage, dominance variations and organelle genes change the expected ratios.连锁、伴性、显性变化和细胞器基因会改变预期比例。

Linked genes travel together unless crossing over separates them, and X-linked recessive traits show more in males. Incomplete dominance, codominance, pleiotropy (one gene affecting several traits) and organelle genes also break Mendel's simple ratios; many traits are also polygenic, shaped by several genes.

连锁基因一起遗传，除非交叉互换把它们分开；X 连锁隐性性状在雄性中更常见。不完全显性、共显性、基因多效性（pleiotropy，一个基因影响多个性状）和细胞器基因也会打破孟德尔的简单比例；此外许多性状是多基因性状，受多个基因影响。

5.5 Environmental Effects on Phenotype环境对表型的影响

One genotype can produce different phenotypes in different environments.同一基因型在不同环境中可表现出不同表型。

Phenotype comes from genotype and environment together. The environment can affect gene expression and the activity of gene products, changing phenotype without changing genotype.

表型由基因型和环境共同决定。环境可以影响基因表达以及基因产物的活性，从而在不改变基因型的情况下改变表型。
