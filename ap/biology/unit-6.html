6.1 DNA and RNA StructureDNA 与 RNA 的结构5 Statistical Tests and Data Analysis5 统计检验与数据分析

A student analyzes double-stranded DNA from a fictional fungus and finds that 22% of the bases are adenine. What percentage of the bases are guanine?

一名学生分析某虚构真菌的双链 DNA，发现 22% 的碱基是腺嘌呤。鸟嘌呤占碱基的百分之多少？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

DNA is a double helix of two antiparallel strands: one runs 5' to 3', the other 3' to 5'. Bases pair by hydrogen bonds, A with T and G with C, so one strand predicts the other. RNA is usually single-stranded, uses the sugar ribose, and has uracil instead of thymine.

DNA 是由两条反向平行的链组成的双螺旋：一条 5'→3'，另一条 3'→5'。碱基通过氢键配对，A 配 T，G 配 C，所以知道一条链就能推出另一条。RNA 通常是单链，含核糖，并用尿嘧啶代替胸腺嘧啶。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

Guanine makes up 28% of the bases.

鸟嘌呤占碱基的 28%。

Evidence证据

A = 22%, so T = 22%; A + T = 44%, leaving 56% for G + C.

A = 22%，所以 T = 22%；A + T = 44%，剩下 56% 是 G + C。

Reasoning推理

In double-stranded DNA, A pairs with T and G pairs with C, so A = T and G = C; G is half of 56%, which is 28%.

在双链 DNA 中 A 与 T 配对、G 与 C 配对，所以 A = T、G = C；G 是 56% 的一半，即 28%。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Thinks every base is equally common; only paired bases are equal.以为每种碱基数量都一样；只有互相配对的碱基数量才相等。
B. Stops at G + C; that 56% must be split between G and C.停在了 G + C 这一步；这 56% 需要在 G 和 C 之间平分。
C. Subtracts A from 100% only; T also has to be removed.只用 100% 减去 A；还需要减去 T。

Double-stranded DNA from a fictional virus is 35% cytosine. What percentage is thymine?

某虚构病毒的双链 DNA 中胞嘧啶占 35%。胸腺嘧啶占百分之多少？

C = G = 35%, so C + G = 70%; A + T = 30%, and T is half of that, 15%.

C = G = 35%，所以 C + G = 70%；A + T = 30%，T 为其一半，即 15%。

6.2 DNA ReplicationDNA 复制6 Argumentation6 论证

A student grows fictional bacteria for many generations with heavy nitrogen (15N), then moves them to light nitrogen (14N). DNA is separated by density. Which result after two rounds of replication in 14N best supports semiconservative replication over conservative replication?

一名学生让虚构细菌在重氮（15N）中繁殖多代，再转入轻氮（14N）中。按密度分离 DNA。在 14N 中复制两轮后，哪个结果最能支持半保留复制而不是全保留复制？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Replication is semiconservative: helicase opens the helix, and each old strand becomes a template for a new one. DNA polymerase needs an RNA primer and can add nucleotides only to a 3' end, so one strand (leading) is made continuously and the other (lagging) in Okazaki fragments. Ligase joins the fragments.

复制是半保留的：解旋酶打开双螺旋，每条旧链都作为新链的模板。DNA 聚合酶需要 RNA 引物，而且只能在 3' 端添加核苷酸，所以一条链（前导链）连续合成，另一条（后随链）以冈崎片段的形式合成，再由连接酶连接。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

A 50:50 mix of hybrid and light DNA, with no heavy band, supports semiconservative replication.

杂合 DNA 与轻 DNA 各占一半、没有重带，支持半保留复制。

Evidence证据

After round one every molecule is hybrid; in round two each hybrid gives one hybrid and one light molecule.

第一轮后每个分子都是杂合的；第二轮中每个杂合分子产生一个杂合分子和一个轻分子。

Reasoning推理

In semiconservative replication each new molecule keeps one parental strand, so the original heavy strands stay paired with light strands and no fully heavy molecule remains.

在半保留复制中，每个新分子保留一条亲代链，所以原来的重链始终与轻链配对，不会再有完全重的分子。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Gives the conservative-model prediction, in which the original heavy double helix stays intact.给出的是全保留模型的预测，即原来的重双螺旋保持完整。
B. Gives the result after one round; a second round adds light-only molecules.给出的是复制一轮后的结果；第二轮会出现只含轻链的分子。
D. Imagines the heavy helix surviving with no mixing; that matches neither model after two rounds.设想重双螺旋原样保留、完全不混合；复制两轮后这与两种模型都不符。

Fictional cells copy their DNA once in a medium with a labelled base, then once more without it. If replication is semiconservative, what fraction of DNA molecules still contain a labelled strand?

某虚构细胞先在含标记碱基的培养基中复制一次 DNA，再在无标记的培养基中复制一次。若为半保留复制，有多少比例的 DNA 分子仍含一条被标记的链？

After round one every molecule has one labelled new strand; in round two each splits into one labelled-plus-new molecule and one unlabelled molecule, so half carry a label.

第一轮后每个分子都有一条被标记的新链；第二轮中每个分子分成一个含标记链的分子和一个不含标记的分子，所以一半带标记。

6.3 Transcription and RNA Processing转录与 RNA 加工1 Concept Explanation1 概念解释

A fictional gene in a mouse has four exons. In liver cells its mature mRNA contains exons 1, 2, 3 and 4; in brain cells it contains exons 1, 2 and 4. Which process best explains how one gene gives these two mRNAs?

小鼠的某个虚构基因有四个外显子。在肝细胞中，成熟 mRNA 含外显子 1、2、3、4；在脑细胞中含外显子 1、2、4。哪个过程最能解释一个基因为何产生这两种 mRNA？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

RNA polymerase reads the template strand of a gene and builds RNA 5' to 3'. In eukaryotes the pre-mRNA gets a 5' cap and a poly-A tail, and introns are spliced out; alternative splicing lets one gene make several mRNAs. Only then does the mRNA leave the nucleus.

RNA 聚合酶读取基因的模板链，沿 5'→3' 合成 RNA。在真核生物中，前体 mRNA 加上 5' 帽和 poly-A 尾，内含子被剪掉；可变剪接让一个基因产生多种 mRNA。之后 mRNA 才离开细胞核。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

Alternative splicing produces the two mRNAs.

可变剪接产生了这两种 mRNA。

Evidence证据

Both mRNAs come from the same gene, but exon 3 is kept in one cell type and removed in the other.

两种 mRNA 来自同一个基因，但外显子 3 在一种细胞中被保留，在另一种中被去除。

Reasoning推理

During RNA processing, the spliceosome removes introns and can also include or skip particular exons, so one gene can code for more than one protein.

在 RNA 加工过程中，剪接体去除内含子，也可以保留或跳过特定外显子，所以一个基因可以编码不止一种蛋白质。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Knows the tail is part of processing, but the tail does not add or remove exons.知道加尾属于加工过程，但尾巴不会增加或去除外显子。
B. Thinks cells change their genes; brain and liver cells have the same DNA.以为细胞会改变自身的基因；脑细胞和肝细胞的 DNA 相同。
C. Looks at translation, but the mRNAs already differ before translation begins.把目光放在翻译上，但两种 mRNA 在翻译开始前就已经不同了。

A fictional fruit fly gene makes a long protein in males and a shorter one in females from the same pre-mRNA. Which process explains this?

某虚构果蝇基因由同一种前体 mRNA，在雄性中产生长蛋白，在雌性中产生短蛋白。哪个过程能解释？

Different splicing of one pre-mRNA can keep or remove exons, giving different proteins from one gene.

同一种前体 mRNA 的不同剪接方式可以保留或去除外显子，使一个基因产生不同蛋白质。

6.4 Translation翻译5 Statistical Tests and Data Analysis5 统计检验与数据分析

In a fictional mature mRNA, the region from the first base of the start codon to the last base of the stop codon is 903 nucleotides long. How many amino acids are in the polypeptide it codes for?

某虚构成熟 mRNA 中，从起始密码子第一个碱基到终止密码子最后一个碱基共 903 个核苷酸。它编码的多肽含多少个氨基酸？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

A ribosome reads mRNA three bases at a time, starting at AUG. Each tRNA has an anticodon that pairs with a codon and carries the matching amino acid, which the ribosome joins to the growing chain. A stop codon ends translation without adding an amino acid.

核糖体从 AUG 开始，每次读取 mRNA 上的三个碱基。每个 tRNA 都有与密码子配对的反密码子，并携带对应的氨基酸，核糖体把它连到延长中的肽链上。终止密码子使翻译结束，不添加氨基酸。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

The polypeptide has 300 amino acids.

该多肽含 300 个氨基酸。

Evidence证据

903 nucleotides ÷ 3 = 301 codons, and the last one is a stop codon.

903 个核苷酸 ÷ 3 = 301 个密码子，最后一个是终止密码子。

Reasoning推理

Each codon of three bases specifies one amino acid, except stop codons, which signal release factors to end translation and add no amino acid.

每个由三个碱基组成的密码子对应一个氨基酸，但终止密码子除外：它让释放因子结束翻译，不添加氨基酸。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
B. Counts every codon, forgetting that the stop codon codes for no amino acid.把每个密码子都算上了，忘了终止密码子不编码氨基酸。
C. Thinks each nucleotide codes for one amino acid; a codon is three bases.以为每个核苷酸编码一个氨基酸；一个密码子是三个碱基。
D. Multiplies by three instead of dividing.乘以 3 而不是除以 3。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

A fictional protein has 150 amino acids. How many nucleotides, including the stop codon, are in its coding region on the mature mRNA?

某虚构蛋白质含 150 个氨基酸。在成熟 mRNA 上，其编码区（包括终止密码子）有多少个核苷酸？

150 amino acids need 150 codons plus one stop codon: 151 × 3 = 453 nucleotides.

150 个氨基酸需要 150 个密码子，再加一个终止密码子：151 × 3 = 453 个核苷酸。

6.5 Regulation of Gene Expression基因表达的调控6 Argumentation6 论证

Fictional E. coli strain M has a mutation in the lac operator. With no lactose and no glucose present, strain M makes high levels of the lac enzymes, while normal cells make almost none. Which claim best explains strain M?

虚构大肠杆菌菌株 M 的 lac 操纵基因（operator）发生突变。在既无乳糖也无葡萄糖时，菌株 M 产生大量 lac 酶，而正常细胞几乎不产生。哪个结论最能解释菌株 M？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Bacteria group related genes into operons controlled by a repressor at the operator: lac is inducible (allolactose, a form of lactose, binds and inactivates the repressor) and trp is repressible (tryptophan activates it). Eukaryotes control genes with transcription factors and with chromatin packing; acetylated histones loosen DNA and raise transcription, while DNA methylation usually lowers it.

细菌把相关基因组成操纵子，由结合在操纵基因上的阻遏蛋白控制：lac 是可诱导型（异乳糖——乳糖的一种形式——结合阻遏蛋白并使其失活），trp 是可阻遏型（色氨酸激活阻遏蛋白）。真核生物通过转录因子和染色质包装控制基因；组蛋白乙酰化使 DNA 松散、转录增加，而 DNA 甲基化通常降低转录。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

The operator mutation stops the repressor from binding, so the operon is always on.

操纵基因的突变使阻遏蛋白无法结合，所以操纵子一直处于开启状态。

Evidence证据

Strain M expresses lac genes even without lactose, the normal inducer.

即使没有乳糖这一正常诱导物，菌株 M 也表达 lac 基因。

Reasoning推理

Normally the repressor binds the operator and blocks RNA polymerase; lactose (as allolactose) removes it. If the operator's sequence changes so the repressor cannot bind, transcription happens regardless of lactose.

正常情况下阻遏蛋白结合操纵基因、阻挡 RNA 聚合酶；乳糖（以异乳糖形式）使其脱离。如果操纵基因序列改变、阻遏蛋白无法结合，无论有无乳糖都会发生转录。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Needs lactose to be present; the stem says there is none.这需要有乳糖存在；但题干说没有乳糖。
B. Predicts the opposite result; a polymerase that cannot bind would give no enzymes.预测了相反的结果；聚合酶无法结合，就不会产生酶。
D. Mixes up the role of glucose; glucose lowers lac expression, and no glucose is present.混淆了葡萄糖的作用；葡萄糖会降低 lac 表达，而且这里没有葡萄糖。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

A fictional E. coli mutant makes a lac repressor that cannot bind allolactose. With lactose present and no glucose, what happens to the lac genes?

某虚构大肠杆菌突变体产生的 lac 阻遏蛋白不能结合异乳糖。在有乳糖、无葡萄糖时，lac 基因会怎样？

The inducer cannot remove this repressor, so it stays on the operator and blocks transcription even when lactose is present.

诱导物无法使这种阻遏蛋白脱离，所以它一直结合在操纵基因上，即使有乳糖也阻断转录。

6.6 Gene Expression and Cell Specialization基因表达与细胞分化4 Representing and Describing Data4 数据表示与描述

In a fictional worm embryo, a student records whether transcription factors A and B are present and whether gene X is expressed. Cell 1: A only, X off. Cell 2: B only, X off. Cell 3: A and B, X on. Cell 4: neither, X off. Which statement describes the data?

在某虚构蠕虫胚胎中，学生记录转录因子 A 和 B 是否存在，以及基因 X 是否表达。细胞 1：只有 A，X 不表达。细胞 2：只有 B，X 不表达。细胞 3：A 和 B 都有，X 表达。细胞 4：都没有，X 不表达。哪项描述了这些数据？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Almost all cells in a multicellular organism share one genome. They become different because each cell type has its own mix of transcription factors, which turns on a different set of genes. Signals from neighbouring cells help decide which factors a cell makes.

多细胞生物中几乎所有细胞都有相同的基因组。细胞之所以变得不同，是因为每种细胞都有自己的转录因子组合，开启不同的一组基因。来自相邻细胞的信号帮助决定细胞产生哪些转录因子。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

Gene X needs both factors to be expressed.

基因 X 需要两种因子才能表达。

Evidence证据

X is on only in cell 3, the only cell with both A and B.

X 只在细胞 3 中表达，而只有细胞 3 同时含 A 和 B。

Reasoning推理

Many eukaryotic genes need a particular combination of transcription factors bound to their regulatory regions, which is how a few factors can specify many cell types.

许多真核基因需要特定组合的转录因子结合在调控区才能表达，正因如此，少数几种因子就能决定许多种细胞类型。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Cell 1 has A but X is off, so A alone is not enough.细胞 1 有 A 但 X 不表达，所以单有 A 不够。
B. Cell 2 has B but X is off, so B alone is not enough.细胞 2 有 B 但 X 不表达，所以单有 B 不够。
D. Overlooks the three cells where X is off.忽略了 X 不表达的三个细胞。

In a fictional plant flower, gene P is on in cells with factors C and D, off with C only, off with D only. A cell type has C, D and E. Predict gene P.

在某虚构植物花中，基因 P 在同时有因子 C 和 D 的细胞中表达，只有 C 或只有 D 时都不表达。某种细胞含 C、D 和 E。预测基因 P。

The data show P needs C and D together; nothing shows E blocks it, so the best prediction is on.

数据显示 P 需要 C 和 D 同时存在；没有证据表明 E 会阻断它，所以最合理的预测是表达。

6.7 Mutations突变1 Concept Explanation1 概念解释

Two mutants of a fictional plant gene are sequenced. Mutant 1's protein differs from normal at one amino acid. Mutant 2's protein matches normal for 11 amino acids, then every amino acid differs and the protein ends early. Which change best explains mutant 2?

对某虚构植物基因的两个突变体测序。突变体 1 的蛋白质只有一个氨基酸与正常不同。突变体 2 的蛋白质前 11 个氨基酸正常，此后每个氨基酸都不同，并且提前终止。哪种变化最能解释突变体 2？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

A mutation is a change in DNA sequence. A substitution may be silent, change one amino acid (missense) or create a stop codon (nonsense); an insertion or deletion within a coding sequence that is not a multiple of three shifts the reading frame downstream. Mutations are random, and they supply the variation that natural selection acts on.

突变是 DNA 序列的改变。碱基替换可能是沉默的、改变一个氨基酸（错义）或产生终止密码子（无义）；编码序列中不是 3 的倍数的插入或缺失会使下游的阅读框移位。突变是随机的，为自然选择提供变异。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

Mutant 2 has a frameshift mutation near codon 12.

突变体 2 在第 12 个密码子附近发生了移码突变。

Evidence证据

Every amino acid after position 11 changes, and the protein stops early.

第 11 位之后的每个氨基酸都改变了，蛋白质也提前终止。

Reasoning推理

Codons are read in groups of three from a fixed start; inserting one base shifts every downstream codon, changing the amino acids and often creating an early stop codon.

密码子从固定起点每三个碱基一组读取；插入一个碱基会使下游所有密码子移位，改变氨基酸，并常常提前出现终止密码子。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Explains the early end but not the changed amino acids after position 11.解释了提前终止，但解释不了第 11 位之后氨基酸的改变。
B. Introns are removed before translation, so this would not change the protein's sequence.内含子在翻译前就被去除，所以这不会改变蛋白质序列。
C. A silent change leaves the amino acid the same, so the protein would look normal.沉默突变不改变氨基酸，所以蛋白质看起来是正常的。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

A deletion removes three bases (one whole codon) from the middle of a fictional gene. What is the most likely effect?

某虚构基因中部缺失了三个碱基（正好一个密码子）。最可能的影响是什么？

Removing a multiple of three keeps the reading frame, so the protein loses one amino acid but downstream codons are read normally.

缺失 3 的倍数个碱基不改变阅读框，所以蛋白质少一个氨基酸，下游密码子照常读取。

6.8 Biotechnology生物技术3 Questions and Methods3 问题与方法

A student mixes fictional bacteria with a plasmid carrying an ampicillin-resistance gene and spreads them on an ampicillin plate; many colonies grow. Which additional plate best shows that this growth is due to taking up the plasmid?

一名学生把虚构细菌与携带氨苄青霉素抗性基因的质粒混合，再涂布到含氨苄青霉素的平板上，长出许多菌落。再加哪一块平板，最能说明这些菌落是因为吸收了质粒才生长的？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

PCR copies a chosen DNA region by repeated cycles of heating to separate strands, cooling so primers bind, and extension by a heat-stable polymerase. Gel electrophoresis pulls negatively charged DNA toward the positive end, so small fragments travel farther; DNA sequencing reads the order of bases. In bacterial transformation, cells take up a plasmid, often one carrying an antibiotic-resistance gene, so only transformed cells grow on that antibiotic.

6.1 DNA and RNA StructureDNA 与 RNA 的结构

Antiparallel strands pair A with T (or U) and G with C.反向平行的两条链中，A 与 T（或 U）配对，G 与 C 配对。

DNA is a double helix of two antiparallel strands: one runs 5' to 3', the other 3' to 5'. Bases pair by hydrogen bonds, A with T and G with C, so one strand predicts the other. RNA is usually single-stranded, uses the sugar ribose, and has uracil instead of thymine.

DNA 是由两条反向平行的链组成的双螺旋：一条 5'→3'，另一条 3'→5'。碱基通过氢键配对，A 配 T，G 配 C，所以知道一条链就能推出另一条。RNA 通常是单链，含核糖，并用尿嘧啶代替胸腺嘧啶。

6.2 DNA ReplicationDNA 复制

Each new double helix keeps one old strand; polymerase builds only 5' to 3'.每个新的双螺旋保留一条旧链；聚合酶只能沿 5'→3' 方向合成。

Replication is semiconservative: helicase opens the helix, and each old strand becomes a template for a new one. DNA polymerase needs an RNA primer and can add nucleotides only to a 3' end, so one strand (leading) is made continuously and the other (lagging) in Okazaki fragments. Ligase joins the fragments.

复制是半保留的：解旋酶打开双螺旋，每条旧链都作为新链的模板。DNA 聚合酶需要 RNA 引物，而且只能在 3' 端添加核苷酸，所以一条链（前导链）连续合成，另一条（后随链）以冈崎片段的形式合成，再由连接酶连接。

6.3 Transcription and RNA Processing转录与 RNA 加工

RNA polymerase copies a gene; eukaryotes add a cap and tail and splice out introns.RNA 聚合酶转录基因；真核生物加上帽子和尾巴，并剪掉内含子。

RNA polymerase reads the template strand of a gene and builds RNA 5' to 3'. In eukaryotes the pre-mRNA gets a 5' cap and a poly-A tail, and introns are spliced out; alternative splicing lets one gene make several mRNAs. Only then does the mRNA leave the nucleus.

RNA 聚合酶读取基因的模板链，沿 5'→3' 合成 RNA。在真核生物中，前体 mRNA 加上 5' 帽和 poly-A 尾，内含子被剪掉；可变剪接让一个基因产生多种 mRNA。之后 mRNA 才离开细胞核。

6.4 Translation翻译

Ribosomes read mRNA codons; tRNA anticodons bring the matching amino acids.核糖体读取 mRNA 密码子；tRNA 的反密码子带来对应的氨基酸。

A ribosome reads mRNA three bases at a time, starting at AUG. Each tRNA has an anticodon that pairs with a codon and carries the matching amino acid, which the ribosome joins to the growing chain. A stop codon ends translation without adding an amino acid.

核糖体从 AUG 开始，每次读取 mRNA 上的三个碱基。每个 tRNA 都有与密码子配对的反密码子，并携带对应的氨基酸，核糖体把它连到延长中的肽链上。终止密码子使翻译结束，不添加氨基酸。

6.5 Regulation of Gene Expression基因表达的调控

Operons, transcription factors and chromatin state decide which genes are expressed.操纵子、转录因子和染色质状态决定哪些基因被表达。

Bacteria group related genes into operons controlled by a repressor at the operator: lac is inducible (allolactose, a form of lactose, binds and inactivates the repressor) and trp is repressible (tryptophan activates it). Eukaryotes control genes with transcription factors and with chromatin packing; acetylated histones loosen DNA and raise transcription, while DNA methylation usually lowers it.

6.6 Gene Expression and Cell Specialization基因表达与细胞分化

Same genome, different active genes, so cells become different types.基因组相同，但被激活的基因不同，所以细胞变成不同类型。

Almost all cells in a multicellular organism share one genome. They become different because each cell type has its own mix of transcription factors, which turns on a different set of genes. Signals from neighbouring cells help decide which factors a cell makes.

多细胞生物中几乎所有细胞都有相同的基因组。细胞之所以变得不同，是因为每种细胞都有自己的转录因子组合，开启不同的一组基因。来自相邻细胞的信号帮助决定细胞产生哪些转录因子。

6.7 Mutations突变

Changes in DNA sequence can change the protein, and so the phenotype.DNA 序列的改变可能改变蛋白质，进而改变表型。

A mutation is a change in DNA sequence. A substitution may be silent, change one amino acid (missense) or create a stop codon (nonsense); an insertion or deletion within a coding sequence that is not a multiple of three shifts the reading frame downstream. Mutations are random, and they supply the variation that natural selection acts on.

突变是 DNA 序列的改变。碱基替换可能是沉默的、改变一个氨基酸（错义）或产生终止密码子（无义）；编码序列中不是 3 的倍数的插入或缺失会使下游的阅读框移位。突变是随机的，为自然选择提供变异。

6.8 Biotechnology生物技术

PCR copies DNA, gels sort by size, sequencing reads it, transformation adds plasmids.PCR 复制 DNA，凝胶按大小分离，测序读出序列，转化导入质粒。

PCR copies a chosen DNA region by repeated cycles of heating to separate strands, cooling so primers bind, and extension by a heat-stable polymerase. Gel electrophoresis pulls negatively charged DNA toward the positive end, so small fragments travel farther; DNA sequencing reads the order of bases. In bacterial transformation, cells take up a plasmid, often one carrying an antibiotic-resistance gene, so only transformed cells grow on that antibiotic.

PCR 通过反复循环复制选定的 DNA 区段：加热使双链分开，降温让引物结合，再由耐热聚合酶延伸。凝胶电泳把带负电的 DNA 拉向正极，小片段跑得更远；DNA 测序读出碱基顺序。在细菌转化中，细胞吸收质粒（常带有抗生素抗性基因），所以只有被转化的细胞能在该抗生素上生长。
