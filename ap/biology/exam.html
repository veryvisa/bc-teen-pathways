The core核心

Half of the AP Biology score is reasoning on paper; official questions plus scoring guidelines show exactly what earns a point.AP 生物一半的分数在纸面推理上；官方真题加评分标准，清楚说明什么能得分。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Section I: 60 multiple-choice questions, 90 minutes, 50% — many in sets of 4–5 on one stimulus.第一部分：60 道选择题，90 分钟，占 50%——很多是 4–5 题共用一段材料。
  2. Section II: 6 free-response questions, 90 minutes, 50% — two 9-point and four 4-point questions.第二部分：6 道问答题，90 分钟，占 50%——两道 9 分、四道 4 分。
  3. Hybrid digital: multiple choice in the Bluebook app, free response handwritten; a calculator is allowed in both sections.混合数字化：选择题在 Bluebook 应用里作答，问答题手写；两部分都可以用计算器。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“Scoring guidelines are for teachers.” They are public, and reading them is the fastest way to learn what a full-credit sentence looks like.「评分标准是给老师看的。」它们是公开的，读它是最快学会满分句子长什么样的方法。
