A calm route to 3 May通往 5 月 3 日的平静路线

What to do each month, how to turn official questions into practice, the task verbs that decide points — and why we never touch leaked questions.每个月做什么、怎么把官方题变成练习、决定得分的任务动词——以及为什么我们从不碰泄露的题。

The core核心

Steady short sessions, official free-response questions marked with their scoring guidelines, and reviews of what you missed — that combination is most of good AP preparation.稳定的短时学习、用评分标准批改的官方问答题、复习自己错过的——这三样组合起来，就是好的 AP 备考的大部分。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Learn with your class; do the quick check and one practice round per unit the same week.跟着课学；同一周内做本单元的快速诊断和一轮练习。
  2. From March, one official FRQ a week, marked with the scoring guidelines.三月起，每周一道官方问答题，用评分标准批改。
  3. In April, two or three mocks; after each, fix the weakest unit × skill cells.四月做两三次模拟；每次之后补最弱的单元×技能格子。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“More hours = better score.” Sleep and spaced short sessions beat long cramming, especially in the last week.「时间越长分越高。」睡眠和间隔短时学习胜过长时间突击，最后一周尤其如此。

🔤 Task verbs decide the points任务动词决定得分

Identify
指出		Name it, no explanation needed.直接说出，不用解释。
Describe
描述		Give the relevant characteristics or what happens.说出相关特征或发生了什么。
Explain
解释		Say how or why, with the biology that connects cause and effect.说清怎样或为什么，用生物学原理连接因果。
Predict
预测		State what will happen; “explain/justify” then asks why.说出会发生什么；若同时要求解释/论证，再说为什么。
Justify
论证		Give evidence and reasoning that support a claim.给出支持论点的证据和推理。
Calculate
计算		Show the setup and the answer with units.写出算式和带单位的答案。
Construct / Represent
构建 / 表示		Draw the graph, diagram or model (axes, labels, units, key).画出图、示意图或模型（坐标轴、标签、单位、图例）。
State the null hypothesis
写出零假设		Say that there is no difference / no effect.说明没有差异或没有影响。
Support / refute a claim
支持 / 反驳论点		Use specific data from the stimulus, not general knowledge.用材料里的具体数据，而不是一般常识。

📄 How to use an official FRQ (the four-pass method)怎么用一道官方问答题（四遍法）

  1. Do it untimed, using the three moves: variables → prediction → reason.不计时做一遍，用三步：变量 → 预测 → 理由。
  2. Grade it with the scoring guidelines, one point at a time. Be strict: the words must be there.用评分标准逐点批改。要严格：字要真的写出来。
  3. Read the Chief Reader report for that question: it lists the common mistakes.读这道题的首席阅卷报告：它列出了常见错误。
  4. A week later, redo only the parts you missed — then log the reason in your class log.一周后，只重做丢分的小问——再把原因记进错题本。

🚫 Why we never use “recalled” or leaked exam questions为什么我们从不用「机经」或泄露的考题

  • AP exam questions are secure and copyrighted. Every student agrees to College Board rules that forbid sharing them; breaking them can cancel scores.AP 考题是保密且受版权保护的。每位考生都同意了禁止传播考题的 College Board 规定；违反可能被取消成绩。
  • Recalled questions are pieced together from memory — the numbers, the options and the “answers” are often wrong.回忆题是凭记忆拼凑的——数字、选项和「答案」常常是错的。
  • AP questions change every year. What transfers is the skill: reading a stimulus and reasoning with it. Official public questions plus original same-skill questions train exactly that.AP 题每年都变。能迁移的是技能：读懂材料并用它推理。官方公开题加上同技能的原创题，练的正是这个。

🌙 The last week and the day itself最后一周与考试当天

Last week最后一周

  • Review queue and the five cards per unit; no new material.复习队列和每单元 5 张卡；不学新内容。
  • One short untimed set a day, then stop.每天一小组不计时的题，然后就停。
  • Normal sleep times — sleep is when memories are consolidated.按平常时间睡——记忆在睡眠中巩固。

Exam day考试当天

  • Bring an approved calculator; check your school’s instructions for the Bluebook app.带上允许的计算器；按学校通知准备 Bluebook 应用。
  • Stuck on a multiple-choice question: mark it, move on, come back.选择题卡住：做标记，先跳过，回头再做。
  • In free response, answer every part — partial answers earn points.问答题每一小问都要作答——部分作答也能得分。

Nervous is normal. Box breathing for one minute before you start works for many students.紧张很正常。开考前做一分钟方框呼吸，对很多同学有用。

🤖 Good questions to ask an AI向 AI 提的好问题

Turn this month-by-month plan into a weekly plan for me from today to 3 May 2027, assuming 4 short sessions a week. Keep the last week light.
把这份逐月计划变成从今天到 2027 年 5 月 3 日的每周计划，按每周 4 次短时学习。最后一周保持轻松。
Give me one example of each AP task verb (identify, describe, explain, justify, predict) answered for the same biology question, so I can see the difference.
用同一道生物题，分别示范 AP 任务动词（指出、描述、解释、论证、预测）的回答，让我看出区别。
I get nervous in timed tests. Suggest three short practice routines that build familiarity with timing without raising stress.
我在计时考试时会紧张。建议三个短的练习方法，让我熟悉时间节奏又不增加压力。
How to check the answer怎么核对 AI 的回答 Check every fact against the College Board course page or the CED, and every calculation by redoing it yourself. If an AI gives a different answer from the explanation here, ask it for the evidence and the principle — and tell us if we are wrong.每个事实都对照 College Board 课程页或 CED 核对，每个计算自己重算一遍。AI 的答案和这里的解析不一样时，请它给出证据和原理——如果是我们错了，也请告诉我们。
