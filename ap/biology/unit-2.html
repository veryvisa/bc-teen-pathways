2.1 Cell Structure and Function细胞结构与功能6 Argumentation6 论证

A student claims bacteria cannot make proteins because they lack membrane-bound organelles. Which evidence best refutes this claim?

一名学生声称细菌没有膜包被的细胞器，所以不能合成蛋白质。哪项证据最能反驳？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Each organelle has a structure that fits its job. Ribosomes build proteins, rough ER folds them, the Golgi modifies and ships them, mitochondria make ATP and lysosomes digest. Prokaryotes have ribosomes and DNA but no membrane-bound organelles.

每个细胞器的结构都与功能相符。核糖体合成蛋白质，粗面内质网使其折叠，高尔基体修饰并运送，线粒体产生 ATP，溶酶体负责消化。原核生物有核糖体和 DNA，但没有膜包被的细胞器。

Claim论点

Bacteria make proteins using ribosomes that are not membrane bound.

细菌用不被膜包被的核糖体合成蛋白质。

Evidence证据

Ribosomes are present in the cytoplasm of bacterial cells.

细菌的细胞质中有核糖体。

Reasoning推理

Protein synthesis requires ribosomes, not a membrane-bound organelle, so the premise of the claim is wrong.

2.2 Cell Size细胞大小5 Statistical Tests and Data Analysis5 统计检验与数据分析

A fictional cube-shaped cell has sides of 4 µm. What is its surface area to volume ratio (SA:V)?

一个虚构的立方体细胞边长为 4 µm。它的表面积与体积比（SA:V）是多少？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

For cells of the same shape, surface area scales with the square of a length and volume with its cube, so SA:V falls as a cell gets bigger. For a cube, SA = 6s², V = s³ and SA:V = 6 ÷ s. Cells stay small or add folds so exchange keeps up with demand.

对形状相同的细胞，表面积与某个长度的平方成比例，体积与其立方成比例，所以细胞越大 SA:V 越低。对立方体，SA = 6s²，V = s³，SA:V = 6 ÷ s。细胞保持较小或增加褶皱，使物质交换跟上需求。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

The SA:V is 1.5 per µm.

SA:V 为每 µm 1.5。

Evidence证据

SA = 6 × 4² = 96 µm² and V = 4³ = 64 µm³.

表面积 = 6 × 4² = 96 µm²，体积 = 4³ = 64 µm³。

Reasoning推理

SA:V = 96 ÷ 64 = 1.5; for a cube the ratio is 6 ÷ side length, so it falls as the cell grows.

SA:V = 96 ÷ 64 = 1.5；立方体的比值为 6 ÷ 边长，所以细胞变大时比值下降。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
B. Inverts the ratio and divides volume by surface area.把比值颠倒，用体积除以表面积。
C. Uses 6 ÷ 1, the value for a 1 µm cube, ignoring the side length.用的是边长 1 µm 时的 6 ÷ 1，忽略了实际边长。
D. Multiplies 6 by 4 and forgets to square the side and divide by the volume.把 6 乘以 4，既没平方也没除以体积。

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

细胞膜是柔韧的磷脂双分子层，极性头部朝向水，非极性尾部在内部。蛋白质、胆固醇和糖类嵌入或附着其上，许多成分可以横向移动。这就是流动镶嵌模型。

The polar phosphate heads face the water on both sides.

极性的磷酸头部在两侧朝向水。

Evidence证据

The tails sit between the two layers, so the heads must be on the outside.

尾部位于两层之间，所以头部必然在外侧。

Reasoning推理

Polar heads interact with water; nonpolar tails are shielded in the interior, which is the arrangement in the fluid mosaic model.

极性头部与水相互作用；非极性尾部被屏蔽在内部，这就是流动镶嵌模型的排列。

2.5 Membrane Transport膜运输4 Representing and Describing Data4 数据表示与描述

A student finds a nutrient at 0.50 mM inside cells and 0.05 mM outside. When cyanide blocks ATP production, uptake stops. What process moves the nutrient into the cells?

一名学生发现某营养物质在细胞内为 0.50 mM、细胞外为 0.05 mM。氰化物阻断 ATP 生成后，吸收就停止了。该营养物质是通过什么过程进入细胞的？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Passive transport moves substances down their gradient with no ATP; this includes diffusion and osmosis. Active transport moves substances against a gradient and needs energy and a transport protein: primary active transport uses ATP directly, while secondary active transport (cotransport) uses an ion gradient made earlier. A clue is whether uptake stops when ATP is blocked.

被动运输使物质顺梯度移动，不耗 ATP，包括扩散和渗透。主动运输使物质逆梯度移动，需要能量和转运蛋白：初级主动运输直接用 ATP，次级主动运输（协同运输）则利用事先建立的离子梯度。可看阻断 ATP 后吸收是否停止。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

The nutrient is moved by active transport.

营养物质通过主动运输进入。

Evidence证据

Concentration is higher inside than outside, yet uptake continues until ATP is blocked.

细胞内浓度高于外部，吸收仍持续，直到 ATP 被阻断。

Reasoning推理

Movement against a gradient requires energy, usually from ATP, and a transport protein.

逆梯度移动需要能量（通常来自 ATP）和转运蛋白。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
B. Simple diffusion would carry the nutrient out, from high to low concentration.简单扩散会使它从高浓度流向低浓度，即流出细胞。
C. Facilitated diffusion is passive and cannot build a higher internal concentration.易化扩散是被动的，不能使内部浓度更高。
D. Osmosis describes water movement, not a nutrient moving against its gradient.渗透指水的移动，不是营养物质逆梯度移动。

2.6 Facilitated Diffusion易化扩散4 Representing and Describing Data4 数据表示与描述

A student finds that uptake of a sugar by cells rises with sugar concentration, then levels off at high concentrations. Which explanation fits best?

一名学生发现细胞对某种糖的吸收随糖浓度升高而增加，在高浓度时趋于平稳。哪个解释最合适？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Facilitated diffusion is passive movement through a channel or carrier protein, down the gradient and without ATP. Carrier proteins can saturate, so the rate levels off at high concentration; channels can also limit the rate. Aquaporins speed water movement but water can still cross slowly without them.

易化扩散是通过通道或载体蛋白的被动移动，顺梯度、不耗 ATP。载体蛋白可以饱和，所以高浓度时速率趋于平稳；通道的数量也可能限制速率。水通道蛋白加快水的移动，但没有它水仍能缓慢通过。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

Carrier proteins are saturated.

载体蛋白已经饱和。

Evidence证据

The rate plateaus even as concentration keeps rising.

浓度继续升高而速率不再增加。

Reasoning推理

Facilitated diffusion has a limited number of proteins, so the maximum rate is reached when all are busy.

易化扩散的蛋白质数量有限，全部处于工作状态时就达到最大速率。

B. Impermeability would reduce uptake at all concentrations.不通透会使各浓度下的吸收都减少。
D. Water supply is not the limiting factor in this pattern.水不是这一规律中的限制因素。

2.7 Tonicity and Osmoregulation张力与渗透调节6 Argumentation6 论证

A student claims that animal cells and plant cells both burst in pure water. Which evidence best shows the claim is wrong for plant cells?

一名学生声称动物细胞和植物细胞放进纯水中都会胀破。哪项证据最能说明这一说法对植物细胞不成立？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Water moves toward lower water potential: Ψ = Ψp + Ψs, with Ψs = −iCRT and temperature in kelvin. More solute means lower Ψs. A cell in a hypotonic solution gains water, in a hypertonic solution loses water, and a plant cell wall builds pressure that limits swelling.

水流向较低的水势：Ψ = Ψp + Ψs，Ψs = −iCRT，温度用开尔文。溶质越多 Ψs 越低。低渗溶液中细胞吸水，高渗溶液中细胞失水，植物细胞壁产生的压力限制膨胀。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

Plant cells do not burst because the wall limits swelling.

植物细胞不会胀破，因为细胞壁限制了膨胀。

Evidence证据

The key difference between the two cell types is the cell wall.

两种细胞的关键差别是细胞壁。

Reasoning推理

Water enters until Ψp from the wall pushes back; Ψ = Ψp + Ψs reaches equilibrium and the cell becomes turgid.

水不断进入，直到细胞壁产生的压力势 Ψp 抵挡住；Ψ = Ψp + Ψs 达到平衡，细胞变得膨胀饱满。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Storage does not stop expansion.储水并不能阻止膨胀。
C. Plant membranes are permeable to water, and aquaporins help it.植物细胞膜对水是通透的，水通道蛋白还起帮助作用。
D. Water leaves passively; cells do not pump water directly.水是被动流出的；细胞不会直接泵水。

Mitochondria have bacteria-like DNA and ribosomes.

2.1 Cell Structure and Function细胞结构与功能

Each organelle's structure fits its job, from ribosomes to the Golgi.每个细胞器的结构与其功能相符，从核糖体到高尔基体。

Each organelle has a structure that fits its job. Ribosomes build proteins, rough ER folds them, the Golgi modifies and ships them, mitochondria make ATP and lysosomes digest. Prokaryotes have ribosomes and DNA but no membrane-bound organelles.

每个细胞器的结构都与功能相符。核糖体合成蛋白质，粗面内质网使其折叠，高尔基体修饰并运送，线粒体产生 ATP，溶酶体负责消化。原核生物有核糖体和 DNA，但没有膜包被的细胞器。

2.2 Cell Size细胞大小

Small cells have larger SA:V, so exchange keeps up with demand.小细胞的 SA:V 更大，物质交换能跟上需求。

For cells of the same shape, surface area scales with the square of a length and volume with its cube, so SA:V falls as a cell gets bigger. For a cube, SA = 6s², V = s³ and SA:V = 6 ÷ s. Cells stay small or add folds so exchange keeps up with demand.

对形状相同的细胞，表面积与某个长度的平方成比例，体积与其立方成比例，所以细胞越大 SA:V 越低。对立方体，SA = 6s²，V = s³，SA:V = 6 ÷ s。细胞保持较小或增加褶皱，使物质交换跟上需求。

2.3 Plasma Membrane细胞膜

Fluid mosaic: phospholipid bilayer with proteins, cholesterol and carbohydrates.流动镶嵌：磷脂双分子层加蛋白质、胆固醇和糖类。

The membrane is a flexible phospholipid bilayer with polar heads facing the water and nonpolar tails inside. Proteins, cholesterol and carbohydrates are embedded or attached, and many of them can move sideways. This is the fluid mosaic model.

细胞膜是柔韧的磷脂双分子层，极性头部朝向水，非极性尾部在内部。蛋白质、胆固醇和糖类嵌入或附着其上，许多成分可以横向移动。这就是流动镶嵌模型。

2.4 Membrane Permeability膜的通透性

Small nonpolar molecules cross freely; polar and charged ones need proteins.小的非极性分子可自由通过；极性和带电分子需要蛋白质。

The nonpolar core lets small nonpolar molecules such as oxygen and carbon dioxide pass freely. Charged ions and large polar molecules cannot cross easily and need transport proteins. This is selective permeability.

非极性的内部使氧气和二氧化碳等小的非极性分子可自由通过。带电离子和较大的极性分子不易穿过，需要转运蛋白。这就是选择透过性。

2.5 Membrane Transport膜运输

Passive transport follows gradients; active transport spends energy against them.被动运输顺梯度；主动运输耗能逆梯度。

Passive transport moves substances down their gradient with no ATP; this includes diffusion and osmosis. Active transport moves substances against a gradient and needs energy and a transport protein: primary active transport uses ATP directly, while secondary active transport (cotransport) uses an ion gradient made earlier. A clue is whether uptake stops when ATP is blocked.

被动运输使物质顺梯度移动，不耗 ATP，包括扩散和渗透。主动运输使物质逆梯度移动，需要能量和转运蛋白：初级主动运输直接用 ATP，次级主动运输（协同运输）则利用事先建立的离子梯度。可看阻断 ATP 后吸收是否停止。

2.6 Facilitated Diffusion易化扩散

Channels and carriers speed passive movement down a gradient, no ATP.通道和载体加快顺梯度的被动移动，不耗 ATP。

Facilitated diffusion is passive movement through a channel or carrier protein, down the gradient and without ATP. Carrier proteins can saturate, so the rate levels off at high concentration; channels can also limit the rate. Aquaporins speed water movement but water can still cross slowly without them.

易化扩散是通过通道或载体蛋白的被动移动，顺梯度、不耗 ATP。载体蛋白可以饱和，所以高浓度时速率趋于平稳；通道的数量也可能限制速率。水通道蛋白加快水的移动，但没有它水仍能缓慢通过。

2.7 Tonicity and Osmoregulation张力与渗透调节

Water moves to lower water potential; tonicity predicts cell volume.水流向水势较低处；张力可预测细胞体积。

Water moves toward lower water potential: Ψ = Ψp + Ψs, with Ψs = −iCRT and temperature in kelvin. More solute means lower Ψs. A cell in a hypotonic solution gains water, in a hypertonic solution loses water, and a plant cell wall builds pressure that limits swelling.

水流向较低的水势：Ψ = Ψp + Ψs，Ψs = −iCRT，温度用开尔文。溶质越多 Ψs 越低。低渗溶液中细胞吸水，高渗溶液中细胞失水，植物细胞壁产生的压力限制膨胀。

2.8 Mechanisms of Transport运输机制

Pumps and vesicles move ions and bulk cargo using energy.泵和囊泡耗能转运离子与大批货物。

Pumps use ATP to move ions against gradients. Large cargo moves in vesicles: endocytosis brings material in and exocytosis sends it out, and both need energy. Vesicles fuse with membranes rather than passing through channels.

泵用 ATP 使离子逆梯度移动。大批货物通过囊泡运输：胞吞带入，胞吐送出，两者都需要能量。囊泡是与膜融合，而不是穿过通道。

2.9 Cell Compartmentalization细胞区室化

Membrane-bound compartments separate reactions and raise efficiency.膜包被的区室把反应分开并提高效率。

Membrane-bound compartments let a cell run different reactions under different conditions, such as the low pH of lysosomes. They concentrate enzymes, protect the rest of the cell, and folded membranes add surface for reactions.

膜包被的区室使细胞能在不同条件下进行不同反应，如溶酶体的低 pH。它们浓缩酶、保护细胞其余部分，褶皱的膜还增加反应表面。

2.10 Origins of Cell Compartmentalization细胞区室化的起源

Mitochondria and chloroplasts likely began as engulfed prokaryotes.线粒体和叶绿体很可能起源于被吞入的原核生物。

Endosymbiosis proposes that mitochondria and chloroplasts began as prokaryotes engulfed by an ancestral cell. Evidence includes their own circular DNA, bacteria-like ribosomes, double membranes and independent division.

内共生学说认为线粒体和叶绿体起源于被祖先细胞吞入的原核生物。证据包括它们自己的环状 DNA、类细菌核糖体、双层膜和独立分裂。
