The core核心

A wrong answer is information: the reason tells you what to practise next.错题是信息：原因告诉你下一步练什么。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Log only the number, unit, skill and reason — never copy the question.只记题号、单元、技能和原因——不抄题目。
  2. Patterns appear after a handful of entries.记上几条，规律就会出现。
  3. Each entry also goes into your review queue.每条记录也会进复习队列。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“Write the whole question out so I remember it.” Copying class material isn’t needed; the reason is what changes your next answer.「把整道题抄下来才记得住。」不需要抄课上的材料；改变你下一次答案的是原因。
