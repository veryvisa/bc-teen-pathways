1.1 Structure of Water and Hydrogen Bonding水的结构与氢键6 Argumentation6 论证

A student claims a water strider can rest on a pond because surface water molecules attract each other strongly. Which evidence best supports this claim?

一名学生声称水黾能停在池塘水面上，是因为表面的水分子彼此强烈吸引。下列哪项证据最能支持这一说法？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Water is polar, so neighbouring molecules attract each other by hydrogen bonds. These many weak bonds give water cohesion, surface tension, a high specific heat and a solid form that is less dense than the liquid. Whenever a question about water gives data, trace it back to hydrogen bonds.

水是极性分子，相邻分子之间靠氢键相互吸引。这些数量众多的弱键使水具有内聚力、表面张力、高比热，而且固态比液态密度小。遇到带数据的水的题，都回到氢键上来解释。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

Hydrogen bonding among surface molecules creates surface tension that supports the insect.

表面分子间的氢键产生表面张力，托住了昆虫。

Evidence证据

The claim is about attraction between surface molecules of the same substance.

说法涉及同种物质表面分子之间的吸引。

Reasoning推理

Attraction between like molecules is cohesion; extra inward pull at the surface is surface tension.

同种分子之间的吸引是内聚力；表面多出的向内拉力就是表面张力。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
B. True fact about hydrogen bonds, but boiling point does not test surface behaviour.这是关于氢键的事实，但沸点并不检验表面性质。
C. Describes polarity and solvent ability, not attraction between water molecules at the surface.说的是极性与溶解能力，而不是表面水分子之间的吸引。
D. Another consequence of hydrogen bonding, but it does not link to the insect on the surface.这也是氢键的结果，却和水面上的昆虫没有联系。

Water drops form beads on a waxy leaf instead of spreading flat. Which best explains the beads?

水滴在蜡质叶片上聚成珠状而不是摊平。哪项最能解释？

Wax is nonpolar, so water sticks to itself more than to the leaf.

1.2 Elements of Life生命元素4 Representing and Describing Data4 数据表示与描述

A fictional molecule is analysed. It contains only carbon, hydrogen and oxygen, in a ratio of 1 C : 2 H : 1 O, and no nitrogen or phosphorus. Which class is it most likely to belong to?

分析一种虚构分子：只含碳、氢、氧，比例为 1 C : 2 H : 1 O，不含氮和磷。它最可能属于哪一类？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Living things are built mostly from C, H, O, N, P and S. Every protein and nucleic acid contains nitrogen; phosphorus marks nucleic acids and phospholipids; sulfur appears in only a few amino acids. Knowing which atoms belong to which molecule lets you choose a label or identify an unknown.

生物主要由 C、H、O、N、P、S 构成。所有蛋白质和核酸都含氮；磷是核酸和磷脂的标志；硫只出现在少数氨基酸中。知道哪种原子属于哪类分子，就能选择标记物或判断未知分子。

Claim论点

It is most likely a carbohydrate.

它最可能是糖类。

Evidence证据

Only C, H and O appear, in the ratio typical of sugars (CH2O).

只含 C、H、O，比例是糖类的典型比例 (CH2O)。

Reasoning推理

Proteins and nucleic acids contain nitrogen, and phospholipids and nucleic acids contain phosphorus.

蛋白质和核酸含氮，磷脂和核酸含磷。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Proteins have nitrogen in every amino acid.蛋白质的每个氨基酸都含氮。
B. Nucleic acids contain nitrogen and phosphorus.核酸含氮和磷。
C. Phospholipids contain phosphorus, and are far less oxygen-rich.磷脂含磷，且含氧比例低得多。

A researcher wants to label newly made DNA in growing bacteria but not newly made protein. Which radioactive atom suits this best?

研究人员想在生长的细菌中标记新合成的 DNA，而不标记新合成的蛋白质。哪种放射性原子最合适？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Living things are built mostly from C, H, O, N, P and S. Every protein and nucleic acid contains nitrogen; phosphorus marks nucleic acids and phospholipids; sulfur appears in only a few amino acids. Knowing which atoms belong to which molecule lets you choose a label or identify an unknown.

生物主要由 C、H、O、N、P、S 构成。所有蛋白质和核酸都含氮；磷是核酸和磷脂的标志；硫只出现在少数氨基酸中。知道哪种原子属于哪类分子，就能选择标记物或判断未知分子。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

Radioactive phosphorus labels DNA but, in newly made protein, almost none.

放射性磷标记 DNA，而新合成的蛋白质几乎不被标记。

Evidence证据

The goal is to separate DNA from protein by a difference in elemental composition.

目标是靠元素组成的差异把 DNA 和蛋白质区分开。

Reasoning推理

Every nucleotide carries phosphate in the sugar-phosphate backbone, while most amino acids contain no phosphorus (proteins can be phosphorylated later, but not as they are built); nitrogen and carbon are in every amino acid, and sulfur is in only a few R groups.

每个核苷酸的糖磷酸骨架都含磷酸，而大多数氨基酸不含磷（蛋白质以后可被磷酸化，但合成时并不带磷）；每个氨基酸都含碳和氮，只有少数 R 基含硫。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Thinks only DNA has carbon; all macromolecules do.以为只有 DNA 有碳；所有大分子都含碳。
C. Forgets that every amino acid has an amino group, so proteins contain nitrogen too.忘了每个氨基酸都有氨基，蛋白质同样含氮。
D. Thinks hydroxyl groups are unique to DNA; carbohydrates and many amino acids have them.以为羟基是 DNA 独有的；糖类和不少氨基酸也有羟基。

A researcher wants to label newly made protein but not newly made DNA. Which radioactive atom is the best choice?

研究人员想标记新合成的蛋白质而不标记新合成的 DNA。哪种放射性原子是最佳选择？

Cysteine and methionine contain sulfur, so proteins that include them are labelled; DNA contains no sulfur.

半胱氨酸和甲硫氨酸含硫，含它们的蛋白质会被标记；DNA 不含硫。

1.3 Introduction to Macromolecules大分子概述5 Statistical Tests and Data Analysis5 统计检验与数据分析

A polypeptide is made of 50 amino acids joined in one chain. How many water molecules are used when it is completely hydrolyzed into free amino acids?

一条多肽由 50 个氨基酸连成一条链。把它完全水解成游离氨基酸，需要消耗多少个水分子？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Many large biological molecules are polymers built from monomers; lipids are not true polymers. Dehydration synthesis joins monomers and releases one water per bond; hydrolysis uses one water to break a bond. A chain of n monomers has n minus 1 bonds.

许多生物大分子是由单体构成的聚合物；脂质不是真正的聚合物。脱水缩合连接单体，每形成一个键释放一个水；水解则用一个水分子断开一个键。n 个单体的链有 n − 1 个键。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

Complete hydrolysis uses 49 water molecules.

完全水解需要 49 个水分子。

Evidence证据

A chain of 50 units has 50 − 1 = 49 peptide bonds.

50 个单元的链有 50 − 1 = 49 个肽键。

Reasoning推理

Each peptide bond formed by dehydration synthesis releases one water; hydrolysis adds one water to break each bond.

每形成一个肽键，脱水缩合释放一个水分子；水解则每断开一个键消耗一个水分子。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Halves the number of units, mixing up units with pairs.把单元数减半，把单元和成对关系弄混了。
B. Subtracts two, as if both ends needed a bond count adjustment.多减了 1，好像两端都要调整。
C. Counts amino acids instead of bonds; a linear chain has one fewer bond than units.数的是氨基酸而不是键；线性链的键数比单元数少一个。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

A short peptide is built from 20 amino acids in one chain. How many water molecules are released as it forms?

由 20 个氨基酸连成一条链的短肽。形成时释放多少个水分子？

A chain of 20 units has 19 bonds, and each bond releases one water.

20 个单元的链有 19 个键，每个键释放一个水。

1.4 Carbohydrates糖类2 Visual Representations2 图示分析

Diagram X shows glucose units all facing the same way in a coiled, branched polymer. Diagram Y shows each glucose flipped relative to its neighbour, forming straight chains that lie side by side. Which is cellulose, and why?

图 X 中葡萄糖单元朝向一致，形成卷曲且有分支的聚合物。图 Y 中每个葡萄糖相对邻居翻转，形成并排的直链。哪一个是纤维素，为什么？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Carbohydrates are sugars and their polymers. Starch is amylose (mostly unbranched, coiled) plus amylopectin (branched), and glycogen is highly branched, so both store glucose compactly and release it easily. Cellulose has straight chains that hydrogen-bond into strong fibres. Same sugar, different linkage, different job.

糖类是糖及其聚合物。淀粉由直链淀粉（基本无分支、卷曲）和支链淀粉（有分支）组成，糖原则高度分支，二者都能紧凑储存葡萄糖并易于释放。纤维素是直链，链间氢键形成强韧纤维。同一种糖，连接方式不同，功能不同。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

Y is cellulose.

Y 是纤维素。

Evidence证据

Y has alternating glucose orientation and straight, side-by-side chains.

Y 的葡萄糖交替朝向，链笔直并排。

Reasoning推理

Cellulose has beta linkages that produce straight chains; hydrogen bonds between chains form strong fibres. Starch has alpha linkages that coil.

纤维素是 β 连接，形成直链，链间氢键构成强韧纤维；淀粉是 α 连接，会卷曲。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
B. Describes starch, a storage polymer, not the structural wall material.描述的是淀粉这种储能多聚物，而不是结构性的细胞壁材料。
C. Hydrolysis breaks bonds; polymers form by dehydration synthesis.水解是断键；聚合物由脱水缩合形成。
D. Mixes up the shape of the structural polymer with branching, which belongs to glycogen and some starch.把结构多糖的形状和分支搞混了；分支属于糖原和部分淀粉。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

Glycogen is stored in muscle cells and can be released quickly as glucose. Which feature best explains this?

糖原储存在肌细胞中，可以迅速释放葡萄糖。哪项特征最能解释？

Branching creates many ends for enzymes to work from.

分支产生许多可供酶作用的末端。

1.5 Lipids脂质4 Representing and Describing Data4 数据表示与描述

A student records two 18-carbon fatty acids. Fatty acid P has no double bonds and melts at 69 °C. Fatty acid Q has one cis double bond and melts at 13 °C. Which statement best explains the difference?

一名学生记录了两种含 18 个碳的脂肪酸。脂肪酸 P 无双键，熔点 69 °C；脂肪酸 Q 有一个顺式双键，熔点 13 °C。下列哪项最能解释这一差异？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Lipids are mostly nonpolar because they are rich in C–H bonds. Saturated tails are straight and pack tightly, while cis double bonds kink unsaturated tails and keep them looser. Phospholipids have a polar head and nonpolar tails, so they form bilayers in water.

脂质含大量 C–H 键，因而大多是非极性的。饱和尾部笔直、堆积紧密，顺式双键使不饱和尾部弯折而更松散。磷脂有极性头部和非极性尾部，所以在水中形成双分子层。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

The cis double bond bends Q's tail, so Q packs loosely and melts at a lower temperature.

顺式双键使 Q 的尾部弯折，堆积松散，熔点更低。

Evidence证据

Both have 18 carbons, but Q melts 56 °C lower and has one cis double bond.

两者都是 18 个碳，但 Q 熔点低 56 °C，并有一个顺式双键。

Reasoning推理

Saturated tails are straight and pack tightly with many weak attractions; kinks from cis double bonds reduce packing and lower the melting point.

饱和尾部笔直、紧密堆积，弱相互作用多；顺式双键造成的弯折减少堆积，熔点随之降低。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Both have 18 carbons, so carbon count cannot explain a difference.两者碳数相同，所以碳数不能解释差异。
C. Double bonds in the hydrocarbon tail do not add charge; the tail stays nonpolar.烃尾中的双键不会带电；尾部仍是非极性的。
D. Reverses saturation: P, with no double bonds, is the saturated one.把饱和度弄反了：没有双键的 P 才是饱和的。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

Fish living in cold water have membrane fats that stay liquid at low temperature. Which feature of the fatty acid tails explains this?

生活在冷水中的鱼，膜脂在低温下仍保持液态。脂肪酸尾部的哪个特征能解释？

Bent tails cannot pack tightly, so the membrane stays fluid.

弯折的尾部不能紧密堆积，所以膜保持流动。

1.6 Nucleic Acids核酸6 Argumentation6 论证

A student claims that in RNA, uracil pairs with cytosine. Which statement best shows the claim is wrong?

一名学生声称 RNA 中尿嘧啶与胞嘧啶配对。哪项最能说明该说法错误？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Nucleic acids are chains of nucleotides with a sugar-phosphate backbone and a direction, 5′ to 3′. In DNA the two strands are antiparallel and pair A with T and G with C, so the percentages of A and T match, as do G and C.

核酸是由核苷酸组成的链，有糖磷酸骨架，并有 5′ 到 3′ 的方向。DNA 的两条链反向平行，A 配 T、G 配 C，所以 A 与 T 的百分比相同，G 与 C 也相同。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

Uracil pairs with adenine.

尿嘧啶与腺嘌呤配对。

Evidence证据

Uracil takes thymine's place in RNA.

RNA 中尿嘧啶取代了胸腺嘧啶的位置。

Reasoning推理

Base pairing follows complementary shape and hydrogen bonding: A with T or U, G with C.

碱基配对由互补的形状和氢键决定：A 配 T 或 U，G 配 C。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错

C. Both are pyrimidines, and that would not make them pair.它们都是嘧啶，而且这并不会使它们配对。
D. RNA is usually single stranded, and pairing is specific.RNA 通常是单链，且配对有特异性。

1.7 Proteins蛋白质6 Argumentation6 论证

A student says: “All proteins share the same backbone, so replacing one amino acid cannot change function.” Which observation best argues against this?

一名学生说：“所有蛋白质的主链都相同，所以替换一个氨基酸不会改变功能。”下列哪项观察最能反驳这一说法？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Proteins are chains of amino acids joined by peptide bonds. The sequence is the primary structure; backbone hydrogen bonds give secondary structure; R-group interactions give tertiary structure; several chains give quaternary structure. Heat or pH changes break weak interactions and denature the protein but not the peptide bonds.

蛋白质是由肽键连接的氨基酸链。序列是一级结构；主链氢键形成二级结构；R 基相互作用形成三级结构；多条链构成四级结构。加热或 pH 变化破坏弱相互作用使蛋白质变性，但不破坏肽键。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

A single amino acid change can alter folding and function.

一个氨基酸的改变就能改变折叠和功能。

Evidence证据

The R group, not the backbone, differs between amino acids and drives interactions.

氨基酸之间不同的是 R 基而不是主链，R 基决定相互作用。

Reasoning推理

R-group interactions (hydrophobic, ionic, hydrogen bonds, disulfide bridges) fold the chain, so changing one can change shape and function.

R 基之间的相互作用（疏水作用、离子键、氢键、二硫键）使链折叠，改变一个就可能改变形状和功能。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Supports the student: the shared backbone parts are what the student described.这恰好支持学生的说法：它讲的就是共有的主链部分。
B. States how bonds form but says nothing about the effect of changing one amino acid.讲的是键如何形成，没有说明替换氨基酸的影响。
D. Describes directionality, a feature of every chain, not evidence about one substitution.描述的是方向性，每条链都有，与单个替换无关。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

Two proteins each have 120 amino acids but fold into different shapes. Which best explains the difference?

两种蛋白质都有 120 个氨基酸，但折叠成不同形状。哪项最能解释？

The backbone is the same; the R-group sequence differs.

主链相同，不同的是 R 基序列。

The core核心

Life's molecules are built from a few elements, joined and split with water, and their shapes decide what they do.生命分子由少数几种元素构成，靠水来连接与分开，它们的形状决定了功能。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Water's polarity and hydrogen bonds explain cohesion, surface tension, high specific heat and floating ice.水的极性和氢键解释了内聚力、表面张力、高比热和冰浮于水。
  2. Dehydration synthesis builds polymers and releases water; hydrolysis breaks them by adding water.脱水缩合构建聚合物并释放水；水解加水把它们分开。
  3. Structure leads to function: tail shape in lipids, strand direction in DNA, R-group folding in proteins.结构决定功能：脂质的尾部形状、DNA 的链方向、蛋白质 R 基引起的折叠。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

Myth: hydrogen bonds are bonds inside a water molecule. Fix: the bonds inside water are covalent; hydrogen bonds are weak attractions between molecules.常见误区：氢键是水分子内部的键。纠正：水分子内部是共价键；氢键是分子之间较弱的吸引力。

Monomers join by dehydration synthesis and split by hydrolysis.单体通过脱水缩合连接，通过水解分开。

Many large biological molecules are polymers built from monomers; lipids are not true polymers. Dehydration synthesis joins monomers and releases one water per bond; hydrolysis uses one water to break a bond. A chain of n monomers has n minus 1 bonds.

许多生物大分子是由单体构成的聚合物；脂质不是真正的聚合物。脱水缩合连接单体，每形成一个键释放一个水；水解则用一个水分子断开一个键。n 个单体的链有 n − 1 个键。

Glucose linkage shape decides storage in starch versus strength in cellulose.葡萄糖的连接方式决定淀粉的储能与纤维素的强度。

Carbohydrates are sugars and their polymers. Starch is amylose (mostly unbranched, coiled) plus amylopectin (branched), and glycogen is highly branched, so both store glucose compactly and release it easily. 糖类是糖及其聚合物。淀粉由直链淀粉（基本无分支、卷曲）和支链淀粉（有分支）组成，糖原则高度分支，二者都能紧凑储存葡萄糖并易于释放。纤维素是直链，链间氢键形成强韧纤维。同一种糖，连接方式不同，功能不同。
