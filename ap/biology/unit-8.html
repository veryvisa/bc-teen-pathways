8.1 Responses to the Environment对环境的反应6 Argumentation6 论证

A fictional plant flowers only when nights last at least 10 hours. Plants kept in 14-hour nights flower. Plants given 14-hour nights with a 5-minute flash of light in the middle do not flower. What is the best conclusion?

一种虚构植物只有在夜长至少10小时时开花。14小时夜晚的植物开花；14小时夜晚中间加5分钟闪光的植物不开花。最佳结论是？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Organisms respond to cues such as light, temperature and chemicals with behaviours that raise survival and reproduction, including foraging, migration and communication. Such behaviours persist because natural selection favours individuals (or their relatives) that do them.

生物对光、温度和化学物质等信号作出反应，行为包括觅食、迁徙和通讯，这些行为提高存活和繁殖。它们得以保留，是因为自然选择偏爱做出这些行为的个体（或其亲属）。

Claim论点

Flowering depends on a continuous dark period.

开花取决于连续的黑暗期。

Evidence证据

Both groups had 14-hour nights, but the interrupted group did not flower.

两组夜长都是14小时，但被打断的一组没有开花。

Reasoning推理

Cutting the dark period with light removed the cue, so the plant measures night length.

用光打断黑暗期去掉了信号，说明植物测量的是夜长。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Focuses on daylight length and ignores that the night was the part changed.只盯着白昼长度，忽略了被改变的是夜晚。
B. Reads the flash as a trigger when it stopped flowering.把闪光当成开花的触发，而它其实阻止了开花。
C. Ignores the difference between the two dark treatments.忽略了两种黑暗处理之间的差别。

8.2 Energy Flow Through Ecosystems生态系统中的能量流动5 Statistical Tests and Data Analysis5 统计检验与数据分析

Producers in a fictional grassland fix 40,000 kJ per square metre per year. Assume about 10 percent of energy passes to each next trophic level. How much energy reaches tertiary consumers?

虚构草地中的生产者每年每平方米固定40,000 kJ。假设每传到下一营养级约10%。到达三级消费者的能量是多少？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Energy flows one way from the sun through producers and consumers, and about 10 percent (an approximation) passes to each next trophic level. Energy not passed on is lost mostly as heat from respiration, or stays in uneaten biomass, waste and detritus used by decomposers. Matter such as carbon and nitrogen is recycled by biogeochemical cycles.

能量从太阳经生产者和消费者单向流动，每个营养级约有10%（近似值）传到下一级。未传递的能量主要以呼吸产生的热损失，或留在未被吃掉的生物量、废物和碎屑中，由分解者利用。碳、氮等物质通过生物地球化学循环被循环利用。

Claim论点

40,000 × 0.1 × 0.1 × 0.1 = 40 kJ.

40,000 × 0.1 × 0.1 × 0.1 = 40 kJ，即约40 kJ。

Evidence证据

Producers to primary, secondary and tertiary consumers is three transfers.

从生产者到初级、次级、三级消费者共三次传递。

Reasoning推理

Energy falls by about 90 percent at each transfer, so count the arrows, not the levels.

能量每次传递减少约90%，要数的是箭头数，而不是层数。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Applies the 10 percent transfer only once.只应用了一次10%传递。
B. Applies the transfer twice, reaching secondary consumers.应用了两次传递，只到达次级消费者。
D. Applies the transfer four times, going one level too far.应用了四次传递，多算了一级。

Reasoning推理

In exponential growth the rate is the per capita rate times the number of individuals.

指数增长中，增长速率等于人均增长率乘以个体数。

A fictional fish population has N = 300, K = 1,200 and r_max = 0.10 per month. Using dN/dt = r_max N (K − N)/K, what is the population growth rate?

一个虚构鱼类种群N = 300，K = 1,200，r_max为每月0.10。用dN/dt = r_max N (K − N)/K，种群增长速率是多少？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Unlimited growth is exponential: dN/dt = r_max N. Real populations level off near the carrying capacity K, described by logistic growth: dN/dt = r_max N (K − N)/K.

不受限制的增长是指数增长：dN/dt = r_max N。真实种群在环境容纳量K附近趋平，用逻辑斯谛增长描述：dN/dt = r_max N (K − N)/K。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

0.10 × 300 × 900/1,200 = 22.5.

0.10 × 300 × 900/1,200 = 22.5，所以每月22.5条。

Evidence证据

(1,200 − 300)/1,200 = 0.75.

(1,200 − 300)/1,200 = 0.75，即还有75%的K可用。

Reasoning推理

Logistic growth is exponential growth reduced by the fraction of K still available.

逻辑斯谛增长是指数增长乘以仍可用的K的比例。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Ignores the (K − N)/K term, which is the exponential equation.忽略了(K − N)/K项，这样用的是指数方程。
B. Multiplies by K − N without dividing by K.乘了K − N却没有除以K。
D. Uses N/K instead of (K − N)/K.用了N/K而不是(K − N)/K。

一个虚构鹿群N = 250，K = 1,000，r_max为每年0.20。用逻辑斯谛方程，dN/dt是多少？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Density-dependent factors such as competition, disease and predation become stronger as density rises and regulate populations. Density-independent factors such as fires and storms act regardless of density.

竞争、疾病和捕食等密度制约因素随密度升高而增强，调节种群。火灾和风暴等非密度制约因素不论密度如何都起作用。

Disease spread depends on crowding.

Evidence证据

Parasite transmission rises with density.

寄生虫传播随密度升高。

Reasoning推理

A density-dependent factor changes in strength as population density changes, giving negative feedback.

密度制约因素的强度随种群密度变化，形成负反馈。

8.5 Community Ecology群落生态学6 Argumentation6 论证

On a fictional rocky shore, predator P was removed from some plots. After a year, mussel cover rose from 30% to 95% in those plots and species richness fell from 12 to 4; control plots did not change. Which claim is best supported?

在虚构岩岸上，部分样方移除了捕食者P。一年后这些样方的贻贝覆盖率从30%升到95%，物种丰富度从12降到4；对照样方没有变化。哪项说法最合理？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Species interact through predation, competition, parasitism, mutualism and commensalism. A keystone species has an effect far larger than its abundance, and Simpson's index D = 1 − Σ(n/N)² measures diversity.

物种通过捕食、竞争、寄生、互利共生和偏利共生相互作用。关键种的影响远大于其数量，辛普森指数D = 1 − Σ(n/N)²用来衡量多样性。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

P is a keystone predator.

P是关键捕食者。

Evidence证据

Without P, mussel cover rose and richness fell, but nothing changed in controls.

没有P时贻贝覆盖上升、丰富度下降，对照无变化。

Reasoning推理

A keystone species has a large effect relative to its abundance; removing it lets a dominant competitor exclude others.

关键种相对于其数量有很大影响；移除它使优势竞争者排挤其他物种。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Reads the change as a loss of food rather than a rise of mussels.把变化理解为食物减少，而不是贻贝增加。
C. Cannot explain why mussels increased when P was removed.无法解释移除P后贻贝为何增加。
D. Misreads the control as showing no effect, when it is the comparison.把对照没变化误读为没有影响，其实对照是比较基准。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

In fictional forest plots where wolf-like predator W was removed, deer doubled, tree seedlings fell, and herb species dropped from 20 to 9; control plots were unchanged. Which claim is best supported?

在移除类狼捕食者W的虚构森林样方中，鹿数量翻倍，树苗减少，草本物种从20降到9；对照样方无变化。哪项说法最合理？

Removal produced a large community change compared with controls.

与对照相比，移除引起群落大变化。

一个虚构草地有三种植物，个体数为60、30和10。用辛普森指数D = 1 − Σ(n/N)²，D是多少？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Species interact through predation, competition, parasitism, mutualism and commensalism. A keystone species has an effect far larger than its abundance, and Simpson's index D = 1 − Σ(n/N)² measures diversity.

物种通过捕食、竞争、寄生、互利共生和偏利共生相互作用。关键种的影响远大于其数量，辛普森指数D = 1 − Σ(n/N)²用来衡量多样性。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

0.6² + 0.3² + 0.1² = 0.46, so D = 0.54.

0.6² + 0.3² + 0.1² = 0.46，因此D = 0.54。

Evidence证据

N is 100 and the proportions are 0.6, 0.3 and 0.1.

N为100，比例为0.6、0.3和0.1。

Reasoning推理

Simpson's D is 1 minus the sum of squared proportions; higher means more diversity.

辛普森D等于1减去比例平方和；越高多样性越大。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Forgets to subtract the sum from 1.忘了用1减去平方和。
B. Squares only the most common species.只对最常见物种取平方。
C. Squares only the rarest species and subtracts that from 1.只对最稀少物种取平方再从1中减去。

The official topics, in plain words官方主题，讲成大白话

8.1 Responses to the Environment对环境的反应

Behaviours and responses help organisms survive and reproduce.行为和反应帮助生物生存与繁殖。

Organisms respond to cues such as light, temperature and chemicals with behaviours that raise survival and reproduction, including foraging, migration and communication. Such behaviours persist because natural selection favours individuals (or their relatives) that do them.

生物对光、温度和化学物质等信号作出反应，行为包括觅食、迁徙和通讯，这些行为提高存活和繁殖。它们得以保留，是因为自然选择偏爱做出这些行为的个体（或其亲属）。

8.2 Energy Flow Through Ecosystems生态系统中的能量流动

Energy flows one way and shrinks at each trophic level.能量单向流动，每一营养级都会减少。

Energy flows one way from the sun through producers and consumers, and about 10 percent (an approximation) passes to each next trophic level. Energy not passed on is lost mostly as heat from respiration, or stays in uneaten biomass, waste and detritus used by decomposers. Matter such as carbon and nitrogen is recycled by biogeochemical cycles.

能量从太阳经生产者和消费者单向流动，每个营养级约有10%（近似值）传到下一级。未传递的能量主要以呼吸产生的热损失，或留在未被吃掉的生物量、废物和碎屑中，由分解者利用。碳、氮等物质通过生物地球化学循环被循环利用。

8.3 Population Ecology种群生态学

Populations grow exponentially or logistically, shaped by resources.种群呈指数或逻辑斯谛增长，受资源影响。

Unlimited growth is exponential: dN/dt = r_max N. Real populations level off near the carrying capacity K, described by logistic growth: dN/dt = r_max N (K − N)/K.

不受限制的增长是指数增长：dN/dt = r_max N。真实种群在环境容纳量K附近趋平，用逻辑斯谛增长描述：dN/dt = r_max N (K − N)/K。

8.4 Effect of Density on Populations密度对种群的影响

Density-dependent factors regulate; density-independent ones strike regardless.密度制约因素调节种群；非密度制约因素不论密度都会起作用。

Density-dependent factors such as competition, disease and predation become stronger as density rises and regulate populations. Density-independent factors such as fires and storms act regardless of density.

竞争、疾病和捕食等密度制约因素随密度升高而增强，调节种群。火灾和风暴等非密度制约因素不论密度如何都起作用。

8.5 Community Ecology群落生态学

Species interactions and keystone species shape communities.物种间相互作用与关键种塑造群落。

Species interact through predation, competition, parasitism, mutualism and commensalism. A keystone species has an effect far larger than its abundance, and Simpson's index D = 1 − Σ(n/N)² measures diversity.

物种通过捕食、竞争、寄生、互利共生和偏利共生相互作用。关键种的影响远大于其数量，辛普森指数D = 1 − Σ(n/N)²用来衡量多样性。

8.6 Biodiversity生物多样性

Greater diversity often brings stability and resilience.多样性越高，往往稳定性和恢复力越强。

Biodiversity includes genetic, species and ecosystem diversity. More diverse communities are often more resilient, especially when several species fill similar roles.

生物多样性包括遗传、物种和生态系统多样性。多样性高的群落往往恢复力更强，尤其是当多个物种承担相似角色时。

8.7 Disruptions in Ecosystems生态系统的干扰

Disruptions and invasive species change populations and communities.干扰和入侵物种改变种群与群落。

Natural and human disruptions, including habitat loss, pollution, climate change and invasive species, change populations and communities. Effects can spread through direct and indirect links in a food web; their size and direction depend on the species and conditions.

自然和人为干扰，包括栖息地丧失、污染、气候变化和入侵物种，会改变种群和群落。影响可通过食物网中的直接和间接联系传播，其大小和方向取决于物种和条件。

What is a keystone species?什么是关键种？

A species with an effect on its community much larger than its abundance suggests; removing it changes the community greatly.对群落的影响远大于其数量所显示的物种；移除它会使群落大变。

How do you calculate Simpson's diversity index?如何计算辛普森多样性指数？

D = 1 − Σ(n/N)², where n is the count of one species and N is the total; a value nearer 1 means more diversity.D = 1 − Σ(n/N)²，其中n为某物种个体数，N为总数；越接近1多样性越高。

Why do food chains rarely have more than four or five levels?为什么食物链很少超过四五个营养级？
