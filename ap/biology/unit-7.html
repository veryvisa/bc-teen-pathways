7.1 Introduction to Natural Selection自然选择导论1 Concept Explanation1 概念解释

In a fictional moth population, trees darken after a wildfire. Over many generations dark moths become more common. Which statement correctly explains the change?

某虚构蛾种群所在的树木在一场山火后变黑。经过许多代，深色蛾变得更常见。哪一句正确解释了这一变化？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Evolution is a change in the allele frequencies of a population over generations. Natural selection needs heritable variation, more offspring than can survive, and differences in survival and reproduction.

进化是种群等位基因频率在世代间的改变。自然选择需要可遗传变异、后代多于能存活的数目，以及存活和繁殖上的差异。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

Dark moths had higher survival and reproduction, so dark alleles rose in frequency.

深色蛾存活和繁殖更多，因此深色等位基因频率上升。

Evidence证据

Dark moths became more common over generations after the background changed.

背景改变后，深色蛾在许多代中逐渐增多。

Reasoning推理

Natural selection acts on existing heritable variation: individuals whose traits fit the environment leave more offspring, so the population, not the individual, changes.

自然选择作用于已有的可遗传变异：性状适合环境的个体留下更多后代，因此改变的是种群而不是个体。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Thinks individuals evolve during their lifetime; populations evolve across generations.以为个体在一生中进化；进化发生在种群、跨越世代。
C. Thinks the environment creates the mutations it needs; variation exists before selection.以为环境会产生它所需要的突变；变异在选择之前就已存在。
D. Gives organisms intentions; selection is the result of differences in survival, not choice.把生物想成有意图；选择是存活差异的结果，而不是选择行为。

After a fictional island's grasses grow taller, lizards with longer legs catch more insects and leave more offspring. What happens over many generations?

某虚构岛上的草长高后，腿较长的蜥蜴捕到更多昆虫、留下更多后代。许多代后会怎样？

Longer-legged lizards reproduce more, so their alleles rise in frequency; the population, not the individual, changes.

长腿蜥蜴繁殖更多，其等位基因频率上升；改变的是种群而不是个体。

7.3 Artificial Selection人工选择1 Concept Explanation1 概念解释

Farmers bred a fictional grain for 30 generations, planting only seeds from the tallest plants each year. How does this differ from natural selection?

农民连续 30 代培育一种虚构谷物，每年只种最高植株的种子。这与自然选择有何不同？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

In artificial selection, humans choose which individuals breed. It uses the same logic as natural selection and shows how fast heritable traits can shift.

在人工选择中，由人类决定哪些个体繁殖。它与自然选择的逻辑相同，显示可遗传性状能改变得多快。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

In artificial selection, humans, not the environment, determine which individuals reproduce.

在人工选择中，决定哪些个体繁殖的是人类而不是环境。

Evidence证据

Farmers chose seeds from the tallest plants each generation.

农民每一代都选择最高植株的种子。

Reasoning推理

Both processes need heritable variation and both change allele frequencies; the difference is the selecting agent.

两种过程都需要可遗传变异，也都改变等位基因频率；区别在于选择者是谁。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Selection of any kind works only if the trait is heritable.任何选择都只在性状可遗传时才起作用。
B. If plants get taller over generations, allele frequencies did change.若植株一代代变高，等位基因频率确实改变了。
D. Breeders select among variation that already exists; they do not create alleles by choosing.育种者是在已有变异中选择；挑选本身不会创造等位基因。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

Breeders keep only the calmest fictional foxes for breeding each generation. Which statement is true?

饲养者每代只留最温顺的虚构狐狸繁殖。哪一句正确？

Artificial selection works only on heritable variation that already exists; humans are the selecting agent.

人工选择只作用于已有的可遗传变异；选择者是人类。

7.4 Population Genetics种群遗传学6 Argumentation6 论证

A flood killed most of a fictional snail population at random, leaving 20 snails. Afterward, a band-pattern allele that had been rare became common. Which claim is best supported?

一场洪水随机杀死了某虚构蜗牛种群的大部分个体，只剩 20 只。之后，一个原本罕见的条纹等位基因变得常见。哪个结论最有依据？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Mutation creates new alleles; drift changes allele frequencies by chance, especially in small populations; gene flow moves alleles between populations. Bottleneck and founder effects are two kinds of drift.

突变产生新的等位基因；漂变靠偶然改变等位基因频率，在小种群中尤甚；基因流在种群间传递等位基因。瓶颈效应和建立者效应是两种漂变。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

The change is best explained by genetic drift after a bottleneck.

这一变化最好用瓶颈后的遗传漂变来解释。

Evidence证据

Survival was random and the population fell to only 20 snails.

存活是随机的，种群骤减到只有 20 只。

Reasoning推理

In a small population, chance alone can shift allele frequencies a lot, regardless of how well an allele fits the environment.

在小种群中，仅凭偶然就能大幅改变等位基因频率，与该等位基因是否适应环境无关。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
B. Assumes every change is natural selection; the stem says deaths were random.以为一切变化都是自然选择；题干说死亡是随机的。
C. Gene flow needs migration from another population, which is not described.基因流需要其他种群的迁入，题干没有提到。
D. Thinks the environment produces the mutations; the allele already existed.以为环境产生突变；该等位基因原本就存在。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

Ten fictional birds blown to a new island start a population with an allele that was rare on the mainland but common among them. What is this?

10 只被风吹到新岛的虚构鸟建立了一个种群，其中某等位基因在大陆罕见、在它们中常见。这是什么？

A few founders carry a chance sample of alleles, so the new population differs from the source; this is the founder effect, a kind of drift.

少数建群者携带的是偶然的等位基因样本，因此新种群与来源不同；这是建立者效应，一种漂变。

7.5 Hardy-Weinberg Equilibrium哈迪-温伯格平衡6 Argumentation6 论证

A student claims a fictional lizard population is in Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium. Which observation most directly argues against her claim?

某学生认为一个虚构蜥蜴种群处于哈迪-温伯格平衡。哪项观察最直接地反驳了她的说法？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Hardy-Weinberg describes a population that is not evolving: p + q = 1 and p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1. It holds only with a large population, random mating, no migration, no mutation and no selection.

哈迪-温伯格描述一个不进化的种群：p + q = 1，p² + 2pq + q² = 1。只有在大种群、随机交配、无迁移、无突变、无选择时才成立。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

Non-random mating violates Hardy-Weinberg conditions.

非随机交配违反了哈迪-温伯格条件。

Evidence证据

Females choose mates by throat colour.

雌性根据喉部颜色选择配偶。

Reasoning推理

Equilibrium requires a large population, random mating, no migration, no mutation and no selection; mate choice breaks random mating and is also a form of sexual selection.

平衡需要大种群、随机交配、无迁移、无突变、无选择；择偶打破了随机交配，也是一种性选择。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. A large population supports equilibrium; it reduces drift.大种群支持平衡；它能减少漂变。
C. No migration is one of the conditions for equilibrium.无迁移本身就是平衡条件之一。
D. Equal survival means no selection, which supports equilibrium.存活率相同表示没有选择，支持平衡。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

Which observation would most directly show that a fictional frog population is NOT in Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium?

哪项观察最直接地说明某虚构蛙种群不处于哈迪-温伯格平衡？

Migration brings gene flow, which breaks the no-migration condition; the other three support equilibrium.

迁移带来基因流，打破了「无迁移」条件；其余三项都支持平衡。

7.6 Evidence of Evolution进化的证据1 Concept Explanation1 概念解释

The forelimbs of a bat, a whale and a human have the same set of bones arranged in the same order, though they are used for flying, swimming and grasping. What does this best show?

蝙蝠、鲸和人的前肢有相同的一组骨骼，排列顺序相同，尽管它们分别用于飞行、游泳和抓握。这最能说明什么？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Fossils, homologous and vestigial structures, molecular sequences and the geographic spread of species all support evolution. Analogous structures have similar functions but evolved independently (convergent evolution), so they do not show a recent common ancestor for that feature, though the organisms may still share deeper ancestry.

化石、同源与退化结构、分子序列以及物种的地理分布都支持进化。同功结构功能相似但独立进化而来（趋同进化），因此不能说明该特征来自近期的共同祖先，不过这些生物仍可能有更久远的共同祖先。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

The forelimbs are homologous structures inherited from a common ancestor.

这些前肢是从共同祖先继承的同源结构。

Evidence证据

The same bones in the same arrangement appear despite different functions.

尽管功能不同，却有相同的骨骼和相同的排列。

Reasoning推理

A shared underlying structure modified for different uses is best explained by descent from a shared ancestor.

共同的基本结构被改造用于不同用途，最好用源自共同祖先来解释。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Analogous structures have similar function but different structure, the reverse of this case.同功结构是功能相似但结构不同，与本例相反。
B. These limbs clearly work; vestigial means reduced with little or no function.这些前肢功能完好；退化结构是指缩小且几乎无功能。
D. Use during life does not change inherited bone patterns.一生中的使用不会改变遗传的骨骼结构。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

Bird wings and butterfly wings both allow flight but are built from very different tissues. What are they?

鸟翅和蝴蝶翅都能飞，但由完全不同的组织构成。它们是什么？

Similar function with different structure comes from convergent evolution, so they are analogous, not evidence of a recent shared ancestor.

功能相似而结构不同源于趋同进化，因此是同功结构，不能作为近期共同祖先的证据。

7.7 Common Ancestry共同祖先6 Argumentation6 论证

Which piece of evidence best supports the claim that all living organisms share a common ancestor?

哪项证据最支持「所有生物都有共同祖先」这一说法？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

All life uses DNA, nearly the same genetic code, ribosomes and a plasma membrane, which points to one common ancestor. Eukaryotes also share membrane-bound organelles and linear chromosomes.

所有生物都使用 DNA、几乎相同的遗传密码、核糖体和细胞膜，这指向一个共同祖先。真核生物还共享膜包被细胞器和线状染色体。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

A nearly universal genetic code supports common ancestry of all life.

几乎通用的遗传密码支持所有生物有共同祖先。

Evidence证据

The same codons specify the same amino acids in bacteria, archaea and eukaryotes.

细菌、古菌和真核生物中，相同密码子编码相同氨基酸。

Reasoning推理

A shared, arbitrary code is unlikely to arise many times independently, so it was most likely inherited from one ancestor.

一套共享且任意的密码不太可能多次独立出现，最可能继承自同一个祖先。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Size varies enormously across life and says nothing about ancestry.生物大小差异极大，与祖先关系无关。
B. Not true of all organisms, and habitat is not inherited molecular evidence.并非所有生物如此，而且栖息地不是可遗传的分子证据。
C. Only eukaryotes have a nucleus; prokaryotes do not.只有真核生物有细胞核；原核生物没有。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

Which shared feature of bacteria, plants and animals is the strongest evidence of a single common ancestor?

细菌、植物和动物共有的哪项特征是单一共同祖先最有力的证据？

Ribosomes and DNA-based genetics are shared by all cells and are best explained by inheritance from one ancestor; bacteria lack a nucleus.

核糖体和以 DNA 为基础的遗传为所有细胞所共有，最好用继承自同一祖先来解释；细菌没有细胞核。

7.8 Continuing Evolution持续进化4 Representing and Describing Data4 数据表示与描述

A student tracked fictional hospital bacteria: the percentage resistant to drug X was 2% in year 1, 10% in year 3, 25% in year 5 and 40% in year 7. Which statement best describes the data?

某学生追踪虚构医院细菌：对药物 X 有抗性的比例第 1 年为 2%，第 3 年 10%，第 5 年 25%，第 7 年 40%。哪一句最准确地描述了数据？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Evolution continues today, as seen in antibiotic and pesticide resistance and changing viruses. A treatment favours individuals carrying resistance alleles that are already present or that arise by random mutation; it does not cause mutations because they are needed.

进化至今仍在进行，耐药性、抗农药性和不断变化的病毒都是例子。治疗手段偏向携带抗性等位基因的个体，这些等位基因原本就存在或由随机突变产生；治疗不会因为「需要」而引起突变。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

The percentage of resistant bacteria increased every interval, with larger jumps later.

抗性细菌比例每段时间都在上升，后期增幅更大。

Evidence证据

The increases were 8, 15 and 15 percentage points over each two-year interval.

每两年的增幅分别为 8、15、15 个百分点。

Reasoning推理

Describing data means stating the trend and its size accurately; this pattern fits continuing selection by the drug.

描述数据要准确说出趋势与幅度；这一模式符合药物持续选择的结果。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
B. Reads a fall that is not in the data.读出了数据中不存在的下降。
C. Looks only at the first value.只看了第一个数值。
D. Overstates the data: 40% is well below 100%.夸大了数据：40% 远低于 100%。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

Fictional field data: the percentage of weeds surviving herbicide Y was 1% in year 1, 1% in year 2, 12% in year 4 and 30% in year 6. Which description is accurate?

虚构田间数据：在除草剂 Y 下存活的杂草比例第 1 年 1%，第 2 年 1%，第 4 年 12%，第 6 年 30%。哪种描述准确？

The value stayed at 1% for two years and then rose to 12% and 30%, which fits resistance spreading under repeated spraying.

数值两年保持 1%，随后升到 12% 和 30%，符合反复喷药下抗性扩散。

7.10 Speciation物种形成1 Concept Explanation1 概念解释

Two fictional frog species live in the same pond. One breeds in early spring and the other in late summer, so they never mate. What type of reproductive isolation is this?

两种虚构蛙生活在同一个池塘。一种在早春繁殖，另一种在夏末繁殖，所以它们从不交配。这是哪种生殖隔离？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

New species form when populations become reproductively isolated. Prezygotic barriers stop mating or fertilization; postzygotic barriers make hybrids weak or sterile; extinction removes lineages and can open niches.

当种群出现生殖隔离时形成新物种。合子前屏障阻止交配或受精；合子后屏障使杂种虚弱或不育；灭绝移除谱系，并可能空出生态位。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

This is temporal isolation, a prezygotic barrier.

这是时间隔离（temporal isolation），属于合子前屏障。

Evidence证据

The species share a pond but breed at different times of year.

两种蛙共享池塘，但在一年中不同时间繁殖。

Reasoning推理

Barriers that act before fertilization are prezygotic; different breeding seasons prevent mating from happening at all.

在受精之前起作用的屏障属于合子前隔离；繁殖季节不同使交配根本不会发生。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. They share the same habitat, so habitat isolation does not apply.它们生活在同一生境，所以不是生境隔离。
C. No hybrids form, so a postzygotic barrier is never reached.根本没有杂种产生，所以谈不上合子后屏障。
D. Mechanical isolation is about incompatible body parts, not timing.机械隔离是指生殖器官不匹配，与时间无关。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

Two fictional fireflies live together, but each species flashes a different light pattern, and females answer only their own pattern. Which barrier is this?

两种虚构萤火虫生活在一起，但每种的闪光模式不同，雌虫只回应本物种的模式。这是哪种屏障？

Different courtship signals stop mating, a prezygotic behavioural barrier.

求偶信号不同阻止了交配，这是合子前的行为隔离。

7.11 Variations in Populations种群中的变异6 Argumentation6 论证

A new fungal disease reaches two fictional pine populations. Population A has high genetic diversity; population B descends from a few trees and has low diversity. Which prediction is best justified?

一种新的真菌病到达两个虚构松树种群。种群 A 遗传多样性高；种群 B 源自少数几棵树，多样性低。哪个预测最有依据？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

Genetic variation is the raw material for selection. Populations with more variation are more likely to include individuals that survive a new disease or a change in climate.

遗传变异是选择的原材料。变异越多的种群，越可能有个体能熬过新疾病或气候变化。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

Population A is more likely to persist because it is more likely to contain resistant individuals.

种群 A 更可能延续，因为它更可能含有抗性个体。

Evidence证据

A has high genetic diversity and B has low diversity.

A 遗传多样性高，B 遗传多样性低。

Reasoning推理

Selection can only act on existing variation; more variation raises the chance that some individuals carry alleles that help them survive a new threat.

选择只能作用于已有变异；变异越多，越可能有个体携带能帮助其应对新威胁的等位基因。

Why the other options are wrong其他选项为什么错
A. Reverses the link between diversity and resilience.把多样性与抗逆性的关系弄反了。
B. Ignores the difference in variation between the populations.忽略了两个种群变异程度的差异。
C. Thinks needed mutations appear on demand; mutation is random and slow.以为需要的突变会按需出现；突变是随机且缓慢的。
🔁 One more on the same idea同一概念，再来一题

A fictional coral population has many alleles for heat tolerance; a second has very few. Ocean temperatures rise. Which prediction is best?

某虚构珊瑚种群有许多耐热等位基因；另一个很少。海水温度上升。哪个预测最合理？

More variation makes it likelier that some individuals tolerate heat, giving selection something to act on.

变异越多，越可能有个体耐热，选择才有作用对象。

7.12 Origins of Life on Earth地球生命的起源2 Visual Representations2 图示分析

A model of early life shows: simple molecules form nucleotides; nucleotides link into RNA; some RNA copies itself and speeds reactions; RNA is enclosed in a lipid vesicle. Which hypothesis does this model represent?

一个早期生命模型显示：简单分子形成核苷酸；核苷酸连成 RNA；部分 RNA 能自我复制并加速反应；RNA 被包进脂质囊泡。该模型代表哪个假说？

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

At AP level, simple molecules on early Earth may have formed organic monomers and then polymers. The RNA world hypothesis proposes that RNA stored information and catalyzed reactions before DNA and proteins.

在 AP 层面，早期地球上的简单分子可能先形成有机单体，再形成聚合物。RNA 世界假说认为，在 DNA 和蛋白质之前，RNA 既储存信息又催化反应。

Find it on the unit map →在单元图谱里找到它 →

Claim论点

The model represents the RNA world hypothesis.

该模型代表 RNA 世界假说（RNA world hypothesis）。

💡 See this concept first先看这个概念

哈迪-温伯格描述一个不进化的种群：p + q = 1，p² + 2pq + q² = 1。只有在大种群、随机交配、无迁移、无突变、无选择时才成立。

Claim论点

About 480 plants are expected to be heterozygous.

Reasoning推理

Heterozygote frequency = 2pq = 2 x 0.6 x 0.4 = 0.48, and 0.48 x 1,000 = 480.

7.1 Introduction to Natural Selection自然选择导论

Evolution is change in allele frequencies of populations across generations.进化是种群等位基因频率在世代间的改变。

Evolution is a change in the allele frequencies of a population over generations. Natural selection needs heritable variation, more offspring than can survive, and differences in survival and reproduction.

进化是种群等位基因频率在世代间的改变。自然选择需要可遗传变异、后代多于能存活的数目，以及存活和繁殖上的差异。

7.2 Natural Selection自然选择

Heritable variation plus differential reproduction changes populations; fitness depends on environment.可遗传变异加上繁殖差异改变种群；适合度取决于环境。

Fitness means reproductive success in a particular environment. Selection acts on phenotypes, but what changes over time is the frequency of the underlying alleles; selection can be directional, stabilizing or disruptive.

适合度指在特定环境中的繁殖成功。选择作用于表型，但随时间改变的是其背后等位基因的频率；选择可以是定向、稳定或分裂选择。

7.3 Artificial Selection人工选择

Humans choose which individuals breed, shifting traits quickly over generations.由人类决定哪些个体繁殖，使性状在世代间快速改变。

In artificial selection, humans choose which individuals breed. It uses the same logic as natural selection and shows how fast heritable traits can shift.

在人工选择中，由人类决定哪些个体繁殖。它与自然选择的逻辑相同，显示可遗传性状能改变得多快。

7.4 Population Genetics种群遗传学

Mutation, drift, gene flow and selection all change allele frequencies.突变、漂变、基因流和选择都会改变等位基因频率。

Mutation creates new alleles; drift changes allele frequencies by chance, especially in small populations; gene flow moves alleles between populations. Bottleneck and founder effects are two kinds of drift.

突变产生新的等位基因；漂变靠偶然改变等位基因频率，在小种群中尤甚；基因流在种群间传递等位基因。瓶颈效应和建立者效应是两种漂变。

7.5 Hardy-Weinberg Equilibrium哈迪-温伯格平衡

p + q = 1 and p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1 describe a non-evolving population.p + q = 1 与 p² + 2pq + q² = 1 描述一个不进化的种群。

Hardy-Weinberg describes a population that is not evolving: p + q = 1 and p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1. It holds only with a large population, random mating, no migration, no mutation and no selection.

哈迪-温伯格描述一个不进化的种群：p + q = 1，p² + 2pq + q² = 1。只有在大种群、随机交配、无迁移、无突变、无选择时才成立。

7.6 Evidence of Evolution进化的证据

Fossils, homologous structures, molecules and biogeography all support evolution.化石、同源结构、分子和生物地理都支持进化。

Fossils, homologous and vestigial structures, molecular sequences and the geographic spread of species all support evolution. Analogous structures have similar functions but evolved independently (convergent evolution), so they do not show a recent common ancestor for that feature, though the organisms may still share deeper ancestry.

化石、同源与退化结构、分子序列以及物种的地理分布都支持进化。同功结构功能相似但独立进化而来（趋同进化），因此不能说明该特征来自近期的共同祖先，不过这些生物仍可能有更久远的共同祖先。

7.7 Common Ancestry共同祖先

Shared genetic code and cell features point to one common ancestor.共享的遗传密码和细胞特征指向同一个共同祖先。

All life uses DNA, nearly the same genetic code, ribosomes and a plasma membrane, which points to one common ancestor. Eukaryotes also share membrane-bound organelles and linear chromosomes.

所有生物都使用 DNA、几乎相同的遗传密码、核糖体和细胞膜，这指向一个共同祖先。真核生物还共享膜包被细胞器和线状染色体。

7.8 Continuing Evolution持续进化

Evolution is ongoing, seen in resistance and changing populations today.进化仍在进行，耐药性和今天变化的种群就是例子。

Evolution continues today, as seen in antibiotic and pesticide resistance and changing viruses. A treatment favours individuals carrying resistance alleles that are already present or that arise by random mutation; it does not cause mutations because they are needed.

进化至今仍在进行，耐药性、抗农药性和不断变化的病毒都是例子。治疗手段偏向携带抗性等位基因的个体，这些等位基因原本就存在或由随机突变产生；治疗不会因为「需要」而引起突变。

7.9 Phylogeny系统发育

Trees show relatedness through nodes and shared derived characters.系统发育树通过节点和共有衍征表示亲缘关系。

Phylogenetic trees are hypotheses about relationships. Nodes are common ancestors, relatedness comes from the most recent shared node, and an outgroup lacks the shared derived characters of the others.

系统发育树是关于亲缘关系的假说。节点是共同祖先，亲缘远近看最近的共有节点，外类群缺少其他类群的共有衍征。

7.10 Speciation物种形成

Reproductive isolation lets populations diverge into new species.生殖隔离使种群分化成新物种。

New species form when populations become reproductively isolated. Prezygotic barriers stop mating or fertilization; postzygotic barriers make hybrids weak or sterile; extinction removes lineages and can open niches.

当种群出现生殖隔离时形成新物种。合子前屏障阻止交配或受精；合子后屏障使杂种虚弱或不育；灭绝移除谱系，并可能空出生态位。

7.11 Variations in Populations种群中的变异

More genetic variation makes populations more able to survive change.遗传变异越多，种群越能应对环境变化。

Genetic variation is the raw material for selection. Populations with more variation are more likely to include individuals that survive a new disease or a change in climate.

遗传变异是选择的原材料。变异越多的种群，越可能有个体能熬过新疾病或气候变化。

7.12 Origins of Life on Earth地球生命的起源

Simple molecules to polymers to self-replicating RNA to protocells, at AP level.AP 层面：简单分子到聚合物，到自我复制的 RNA，再到原始细胞。

At AP level, simple molecules on early Earth may have formed organic monomers and then polymers. The RNA world hypothesis proposes that RNA stored information and catalyzed reactions before DNA and proteins.

在 AP 层面，早期地球上的简单分子可能先形成有机单体，再形成聚合物。RNA 世界假说认为，在 DNA 和蛋白质之前，RNA 既储存信息又催化反应。
