The core核心

A journal turns 'I studied' into 'I know what works for me'.日志把「我学了」变成「我知道什么方法对我有用」。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Write one line, every day you study. That's all.学习的每一天写一句。就这些。
  2. A mood colour is for noticing, not judging — no colour is good or bad.心情颜色是为了觉察，不是评判——颜色没有好坏。
  3. Every Sunday, read back the week — that's metacognition.每周日读一遍这一周——这就是元认知。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“Bad days should not be written down.” Low days are the most useful entries: they show what to change.「状态差的日子不用记」——低落的日子最有用：它们告诉你该调整什么。
