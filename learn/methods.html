The core核心

Effective studying is less about hours and more about how you use them: recall, spacing, mixing and reflecting.有效学习不在于时长，而在于怎么用：提取、间隔、交错、复盘。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Start with retrieval and spacing — they help in every subject.先从提取和间隔开始——每科都管用。
  2. Methods that feel harder often work better.感觉更难的方法往往更有效。
  3. Use one new method per week, not all seven at once.每周只试一种新方法，不要一次全上。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“Learning styles: I'm a visual learner, so I should only use pictures.” Research doesn't support matching teaching to a 'style'; everyone benefits from words plus pictures.「学习风格：我是视觉型，所以只该看图」——研究不支持按「风格」匹配教学；每个人都能从文字＋图中受益。

🎣 Retrieval practice提取练习

Pull information out of your memory instead of putting it in again.

把信息从记忆里「拿出来」，而不是再「放进去」一遍。

REAL EXAMPLE实际案例

Case — AP Biology, cell membranes: after reading, a student closes the book and writes everything they remember about the fluid mosaic model on a blank page for 5 minutes, then checks the textbook and adds the missing parts in a different colour. The gaps (cholesterol's role, glycoproteins) become tomorrow's flashcards.

案例——AP 生物，细胞膜：读完后合上书，用 5 分钟在白纸上写出关于流动镶嵌模型记得的一切，再对照课本，用另一种颜色补上漏掉的（胆固醇的作用、糖蛋白）。这些空缺就成了明天的卡片。

分几天复习，间隔越拉越长。

REAL EXAMPLE实际案例

Case — French vocabulary: instead of 60 minutes the night before, 12 minutes on five days (Mon, Tue, Thu, Sun, next Wed). Words she gets right move to longer gaps; missed words come back tomorrow. The review queue on this site does this automatically.

案例——法语单词：不是考前一晚学 60 分钟，而是分五天各 12 分钟（周一、周二、周四、周日、下周三）。答对的词间隔拉长，答错的明天再来。本站的复习队列会自动这样安排。

🔀 Interleaving交错练习

Mix problem types so you learn to choose the method.

把不同题型混在一起练，学会「选方法」。

REAL EXAMPLE实际案例

Case — Pre-Calculus 11: rather than 20 factoring problems in a row, a set mixes factoring, the quadratic formula and completing the square. It feels harder, but on the test the student can tell which tool fits — the skill blocked practice never trains.

案例——预备微积分 11：不是连做 20 道因式分解，而是因式分解、求根公式、配方法混着做。感觉更难，但考试时能认出该用哪种工具——这正是连续同类练习练不到的能力。

👉 Mix chapters in your self-tests; label each question with its method afterwards.自测时把几章混在一起；做完后给每题标上用的方法。

🧑‍🏫 Feynman explaining费曼复述

Explain it simply, find where you get stuck, go back, simplify again.

用简单的话讲出来，找到卡住的地方，回去补，再讲简单。

REAL EXAMPLE实际案例

Case — Physics 11, why a satellite doesn't fall: explaining it to an imaginary 12-year-old, the student gets stuck at 'it is falling, but…'. Re-reading reveals the missing piece: it moves sideways fast enough that the ground curves away as it falls.

案例——物理 11，卫星为什么不掉下来：给想象中的 12 岁孩子讲时，卡在「它其实在下落，但是……」。回去重读发现缺的那块：它横向速度够快，下落时地面也在弯下去。

❓ Elaborative questioning精细化提问

Keep asking 'why?' and 'how does this connect?'

不停追问「为什么？」「它和什么有关？」

REAL EXAMPLE实际案例

Case — Psychology: 'Why does sleep help memory?' → because the brain replays and consolidates the day's learning → how? hippocampus to cortex transfer during deep sleep → so what? an all-nighter can undo the studying it was meant for.

案例——心理学：「为什么睡觉有助记忆？」→ 因为大脑会重放、巩固白天学的 → 怎么做到的？深睡时从海马体转移到皮层 → 所以呢？通宵复习可能抵消掉它本想完成的学习。

🧭 Metacognition: plan, monitor, reflect元认知：计划—监控—复盘

Think about your own thinking before, during and after studying.

在学习前、中、后都想一想自己是怎么学的。

REAL EXAMPLE实际案例

Case — Before: 'Goal: explain the 3 stages of cellular respiration without notes.' During: after 15 minutes, 'I can do glycolysis, not the electron transport chain.' After: 'Retrieval worked; highlighting didn't. Tomorrow: 10 minutes on ETC first.'

案例——学前：「目标：不看笔记讲出细胞呼吸三个阶段。」学中：15 分钟后，「糖酵解会了，电子传递链还不会。」学后：「提取有用，划线没用。明天先花 10 分钟攻电子传递链。」
