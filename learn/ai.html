The core核心

Use AI to make you think more — questions, explanations, feedback, plans — never to do the thinking for you.用 AI 让自己想得更多——出题、讲解、批改、计划——而不是替你思考。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Try first, then ask; you learn from the gap.先自己试，再问；差距就是你学到的东西。
  2. Verify facts and numbers with your textbook, teacher or an official site.事实和数字要用课本、老师或官网核对。
  3. Follow your school's rules on AI; when unsure, ask the teacher.遵守学校关于 AI 的规定；不确定就问老师。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“If the AI said it confidently, it must be right.” AI can sound sure and still be wrong, especially with numbers, dates and local rules.「AI 说得很肯定，那一定对」——AI 可能很笃定却是错的，尤其是数字、日期和本地规定。

🎯 Make me questions给我出题

I'm studying AP Biology Unit 2 (cell structure). Write 6 multiple-choice questions at AP difficulty that test understanding, not vocabulary. Don't show answers until I reply with mine. Then explain each answer and the common wrong idea behind each wrong option.
我在学 AP 生物第 2 单元（细胞结构）。出 6 道 AP 难度、考理解而非背词汇的选择题。等我回复答案后再公布。然后讲解每题，并说明每个错误选项背后常见的错误想法。

Sample reply (shortened): “1. A cell's surface-area-to-volume ratio decreases as it grows. Which consequence follows? A) faster diffusion per unit volume B) slower exchange per unit volume… — reply with your answers when ready.”示例回复（节选）：「1. 细胞长大时表面积与体积比下降。会导致什么？A) 单位体积扩散更快 B) 单位体积物质交换更慢……——准备好了就回复你的答案。」

💡 Explain it another way换种方式讲

Explain limits in calculus three ways: (1) with a real-life analogy, (2) with a small table of values, (3) with a graph description. Then ask me one question to check I understood.
用三种方式讲微积分里的极限：(1) 生活中的类比，(2) 一个小数值表，(3) 描述图像。然后问我一个问题检查我是否理解。

Sample reply (shortened): “Analogy: walking halfway to a wall again and again — you get as close as you like… Table: x = 1.9, 1.99, 1.999 → f(x) = 3.9, 3.99, 3.999… Check question: what does the table suggest the limit is?”示例回复（节选）：「类比：每次走到墙的一半——想多近就有多近……数值表：x = 1.9、1.99、1.999 → f(x) = 3.9、3.99、3.999……检查问题：这张表说明极限是多少？」

✍️ Give feedback on MY work批改我写的

Here is my paragraph answering 'Explain why enzymes denature at high temperature': [paste my own paragraph]. Don't rewrite it. Point out (a) what is correct, (b) any science error, (c) one sentence I could make clearer, using the Claim–Evidence–Reasoning structure.
这是我回答「解释为什么酶在高温下变性」的段落：[粘贴我自己写的]。不要替我重写。指出 (a) 哪里是对的，(b) 有没有科学错误，(c) 哪一句可以按「论点—证据—推理」结构写得更清楚。

Sample reply (shortened): “Correct: heat disrupts the enzyme's shape. Error: you wrote the active site 'dies' — enzymes aren't alive; say the shape changes so the substrate no longer fits. Clearer: add evidence, e.g. reaction rate dropping above about 40 °C in your lab data.”示例回复（节选）：「正确：高温破坏了酶的形状。错误：你写活性位点『死了』——酶不是活的；应说形状改变、底物不再契合。更清楚：加上证据，比如你实验数据里约 40 °C 以上反应速率下降。」

My Chemistry 11 unit test is in 8 days. I can study 30 minutes on weekdays and 60 on weekends. Topics: moles, stoichiometry, limiting reagent. Make a spaced plan with self-testing every day, mixed practice from day 4, and a light last evening.

Sample reply (shortened): “Day 1 (30 min): 10-min blank-page recall of moles, 20 min problems… Day 4: mixed set of 12 across all three… Day 8: 15 min flashcards, early night.”示例回复（节选）：「第 1 天（30 分钟）：白纸回忆摩尔 10 分钟，做题 20 分钟……第 4 天：三个主题混合 12 题……第 8 天：卡片 15 分钟，早睡。」

🚫 Don't不要

  • Hand in AI-written work as your own — it skips the learning and usually breaks school academic-integrity rules.把 AI 写的东西当作自己的交上去——跳过了学习，通常也违反学校学术诚信规定。
  • Trust numbers, dates, quotes or admission rules without checking.不核对就相信数字、日期、引文或入学规定。
  • Paste personal information (name, school, health) or other people's work.粘贴个人信息（名字、学校、健康）或别人的作品。
  • Let it replace sleep, practice or asking your teacher.让它取代睡眠、练习或请教老师。
  • Sign up for a tool you're too young for: many AI tools have minimum ages or need a parent's consent — check the tool's terms and your school's rules.注册年龄不够的工具：很多 AI 工具有最低年龄要求或需要家长同意——看清工具条款和学校规定。

✅ Do要

  • Ask for questions, hints and feedback rather than answers.要题目、提示和反馈，而不是答案。
  • Ask “what are you unsure about?” and “what's the source?”问「你哪里不确定？」「出处是什么？」
  • Compare two sources, then explain it in your own words (the Feynman page helps).对照两个来源，再用自己的话讲出来（费曼页能帮忙）。
  • Put what you missed into your review queue.把没掌握的放进复习队列。

🔍 A worked example: one hard idea, several sources完整示范：一个难点，多个来源

  1. Question: why does a fever make you feel tired?问题：为什么发烧会让人觉得累？
  2. Source 1 — textbook: immune signalling molecules (cytokines) act on the brain.来源 1——课本：免疫信号分子（细胞因子）作用于大脑。
  3. Source 2 — AI explanation: 'sickness behaviour' saves energy for fighting infection. Ask it: what's your source? Is anything uncertain?来源 2——AI 讲解：「疾病行为」为对抗感染节省能量。追问：出处是什么？哪里不确定？
  4. Source 3 — a reputable health site or your teacher: confirms the general idea.来源 3——可靠的健康网站或老师：证实大体思路。
  5. In my own words: the immune system sends chemical signals that tell the brain to slow down, so the body can put energy into fighting the infection.用我自己的话：免疫系统发出化学信号让大脑「减速」，好让身体把能量用来对抗感染。
