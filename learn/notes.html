  1. Pick one template per purpose, not one for everything.按用途挑模板，不是一个用到底。
  2. Notes are for reviewing: build in questions you can test yourself with.笔记是用来复习的：要留下能自测的问题。
  3. Short and regular beats perfect and rare.简短而规律，胜过完美而偶尔。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“Beautiful notes = good learning.” Copying neatly can feel productive while skipping the thinking.「笔记漂亮＝学得好」——工整抄写会让人觉得很努力，却跳过了思考。

Split the page: cues on the left, notes on the right, a summary at the bottom. Cover the right side and quiz yourself from the cues.

Cues线索
  • Where does glycolysis happen?糖酵解在哪里发生？

Notes — AP Biology: cellular respiration笔记——AP 生物：细胞呼吸

$$\mathrm{C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6\,O_2 \rightarrow 6\,CO_2 + 6\,H_2O} + \text{energy (ATP)}$$

  • Glycolysis — cytoplasm: glucose → 2 pyruvate; net 2 ATP + 2 NADH.糖酵解——细胞质：葡萄糖 → 2 个丙酮酸；净得 2 ATP＋2 NADH。
  • Krebs cycle — mitochondrial matrix: releases CO₂, makes NADH and FADH₂ (electron carriers).克氏循环——线粒体基质：释放 CO₂，生成 NADH 和 FADH₂（电子载体）。
  • Electron transport chain — inner membrane: O₂ is the final electron acceptor; most ATP is made here (roughly 30–32 in total per glucose).电子传递链——线粒体内膜：O₂ 是最终电子受体；大部分 ATP 在这里产生（每个葡萄糖合计约 30–32 个）。
Summary总结 Glucose is broken down in three stages; oxygen at the end of the chain lets most of the ATP be made.葡萄糖分三个阶段分解；电子传递链末端的氧气让大部分 ATP 得以生成。

EXAMPLE实例

Idea: the action potential (Psychology / Biology)概念：动作电位（心理学 / 生物）

My explanation: a neuron is like a line of dominoes. When the signal is strong enough, gates open, sodium rushes in, and the 'fall' runs down the axon.我的讲解：神经元像一排多米诺骨牌。信号够强时闸门打开，钠离子涌入，「倒下」沿着轴突传下去。

Where I got stuck: why can't a weak signal make a small action potential?卡住的地方：为什么弱信号不能产生一个小一点的动作电位？

Fixed: it's all-or-none — below the threshold nothing fires; above it, the spike is always the same size. Stronger signals fire more often, not bigger.修正：它是「全或无」——低于阈值就不放电；超过阈值，峰值大小总是一样。信号更强是放电更频繁，不是更大。

⛓️ Question chain问题链

Start with one question and keep asking why / how / so what. Each answer becomes the next question.

从一个问题开始，不停追问「为什么 / 怎样 / 那又怎样」。每个答案都变成下一个问题。

EXAMPLE实例
  1. Why do pharmacists ask what other medicines you take? → Because some drugs interact.药剂师为什么要问你还吃什么药？→ 因为有些药会相互作用。
  2. How do they interact? → One drug can speed up or slow down the liver enzymes that break the other down.怎么相互作用？→ 一种药可以加快或减慢分解另一种药的肝酶。
  3. So what? → The second drug's level can become too high (side effects) or too low (doesn't work).那又怎样？→ 第二种药的血药浓度可能过高（副作用）或过低（无效）。
  4. What would change my answer? → If the drugs are cleared by the kidneys instead, liver enzymes matter less.什么会改变我的答案？→ 如果药物经肾脏排出，肝酶的影响就小。
