So why do we constantly confuse simply recognizing a page of notes with actually learning the material?

This source material is a straightforward, stress-free guide to making information stick without burning yourself out.

First, let's look at memory retrieval over rereading.

Rereading feels easy because the text is right in front of you.

But true learning happens when you're forced to pull the info from your own memory.

Testing yourself always beats rereading.

The core核心

Learning sticks when you pull it out of your memory, spread it over days, and check how you're doing.知识要牢，靠的是从记忆里把它提取出来、分几天复习、并检查自己学得怎样。

3 things to know必须知道的 3 件事
  1. Testing yourself beats re-reading.自测胜过重读。
  2. Short sessions on several days beat one long night.分几天的短时间学习，胜过一个通宵。
  3. A calm body learns better: breathe first when stress spikes.身体平静才学得进去：紧张时先呼吸。
Most common mix-up最常见的误会

“If it feels easy, I've learned it.” Re-reading feels easy because the page is in front of you — recall is the real test.「感觉轻松就是学会了」——重读觉得轻松，是因为书就在眼前；能回忆出来才是真的会。
